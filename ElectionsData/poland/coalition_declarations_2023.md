**Whether a Polish candidacy draws a line.** The President designates the prime minister, who proposes the cabinet — Art. 154 ust. 1,
"Prezydent Rzeczypospolitej desygnuje Prezesa Rady Ministrów, który proponuje skład Rady Ministrów." (`records_by_date.md` §4
[TK-KONST]); the Sejm elects one only if that cabinet is not appointed or loses its vote (ust. 3). The 2023 record gives a candidacy
rule nothing to read: the parties left the prime minister to after the vote (PO's board named Tusk on 19 October — after it). So Poland
carries **no candidacy facts**, and `CandidacyRefuses` stays Sweden's alone **by absence of input** — this file rules nothing about
Polish candidacies; a later vintage that declares one is Elias's.
