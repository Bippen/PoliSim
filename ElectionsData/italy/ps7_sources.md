## The derived tables - `collegi2020/`, GENERATED by `Tools/it_collegi_prep.pl`

`perl Tools/it_collegi_prep.pl` writes them; `--check` regenerates in memory and compares byte for byte; `--istat <zip>` adds the
cross-check against ISTAT's table. Each file's header names its sources with their SHA-256. Per chamber:

- `<ch>2022_liste.csv` - per 2020 uninominal college: electorate, voters, blank ballots, candidate-only votes, each list's votes (`V:`)
  and its *cifra elettorale di collegio uninominale* (`C:`, DPR 361/1957 art. 77 c.1 lett. c: the candidate-only votes split among the
  candidate's lists by Hare largest remainders). Candidate-only colleges carry `voto` = `solo candidati` and no list figures.
- `<ch>2022_candidati.csv` - each candidate's votes and linked lists per college (lett. a/b: the winner is the most votes).
- `<ch>2018_su_collegi2020.csv` - **K150**: 2018's comune returns re-aggregated onto the 2020 colleges (electorate, voters, blank
  ballots, candidate-only votes, each 2018 list's votes).
- `<ch>_comuni_2018_2022.csv` - the join used, comune by comune, with its route.

### DECLARED readings in the generator

1. **The comune-to-college assignment is the 2022 returns' own** (each row of the 2022 file names the college the comune or piece voted
   in). The Ministry's table and ISTAT's `UT_Collegi2020` are cross-checks, printed by every run.
2. **A comune split between 2020 colleges** (Camera: Torino, Milano, Genova, Roma, Napoli, Palermo; Senato: Milano, Genova, Roma) has its
   2018 returns apportioned to the pieces **in proportion to each piece's 2022 electorate**, integer largest remainders (ties to the earlier
   piece) - so every comune's totals and every national total are kept exactly (the run asserts it). K150 names the comune as the unit;
   within a split comune the 2018 file cannot say where a vote was cast.
3. **2018 comune to 2022 comune**: same name within the circoscrizione; else the 2018 file's lost-accent bytes as wildcards; else ISTAT's
   suppressions (from 2017-01-01, because the 2018 file still spells some pre-polling-day mergers by their old names), renamings and
   province transfers; a 2022 comune carved out of a 2018 one (ISTAT 'CS ... scorporo': Misiliscemi from Trapani) shares its parent's
   2018 returns by 2022 electorate. One join no name rule reaches is declared in the generator with its ISTAT record (Sen Jan di Fassa,
   ISTAT 022250, renamed San Giovanni di Fassa 2018-12-07). Partial territory transfers between comuni (ISTAT AQ/CE) are not modelled.
4. **The 2018 file's row 'CANDIDATO NON PRESENTE'** (a list whose college candidate was not on the ballot) counts as list votes with no
   candidate.
5. **art. 77 lett. c's last sentence** (the recognised-minority list excluded from the split in the colleges where it fielded its own
   candidate) is **not modelled**.
