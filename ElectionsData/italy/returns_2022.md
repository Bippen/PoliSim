### Electoral rules
- Mixed system ("Rosatellum", Legge 3 novembre 2017 n. 165: "Modifiche al sistema di elezione della Camera dei deputati e del Senato della Repubblica…"): part first-past-the-post in single-member colleges, part proportional with closed lists in multi-member colleges. https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2017-11-03;165
- As applied in 2022 (colleges per D.Lgs. 23 dicembre 2020 n. 177): Camera — 147 collegi uninominali (incl. Valle d'Aosta) = 36.75% of 400; 49 collegi plurinominali electing 245 deputies proportionally = 61.25%; 8 deputies in the circoscrizione Estero; 392 elected on national territory. https://documenti.camera.it/leg18/dossier/testi/AC0445a.htm (Camera dossier on D.Lgs. 177/2020, quoting "147 collegi uninominali… e 49 collegi plurinominali", "392 deputati")
- Thresholds: 3% of national valid votes for single lists, 10% for coalitions (a coalition must contain at least one list ≥3%); special rules for linguistic-minority lists (how SVP–PATT won seats at 0.42%). https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2017-11-03;165 (art. 83 DPR 361/1957 as amended) and https://www.camera.it/temiap/documentazione/temi/pdf/1104098.pdf
- Linked-coalition mechanics: the voter casts one vote on one ballot, which counts both for a list in the proportional part and for the single uninominal candidate jointly supported by that list's coalition — no split-ticket voting. A vote marked only for the uninominal candidate is apportioned among the coalition's lists in proportion to the votes each obtained in that college (art. 77-referenced distribution rule in the consolidated law text on normattiva). https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2017-11-03;165
- Size reduction 630→400: Legge costituzionale 19 ottobre 2020 n. 1 ("Modifiche agli articoli 56, 57 e 59 della Costituzione…") sets the Camera at "quattrocento" deputies, "otto dei quali eletti nella circoscrizione Estero" — first applied at this election. https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge.costituzionale:2020-10-19;1
