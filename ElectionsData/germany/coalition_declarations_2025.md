# Germany — declarations for the formation after the Bundestag election of 23 February 2025 [SOURCED]

saved whole under `raw/records/` (digests below). The game reads these facts from `DeclaredRedLines.GermanyTimeline` (C# literals, the

## 1. The Union's incompatibility resolutions

**The game's shape for all three: SYMMETRIC and support-blocking** — "I will not be in, or support, a government that depends on you".
A coalition is refused and so is every *similar form of cooperation*, which takes in a Union cabinet governing on the AfD's toleration
as much as the Union tolerating an AfD cabinet. K-1's one-way shape (Sweden's C → V) would be wrong here: it refuses cabinets that
*contain* the other party and lets that party support a cabinet the declarer sits in — the one arrangement these resolutions name. (The
first cut of §705 used the one-way shape; its own check found the 2025 chamber forming CDU+CSU on the AfD's support.)

**CDU → AfD and CDU → Die Linke** — support-blocking (coalitions *and similar forms of cooperation*), from **2018-12-08**:
> "Beschlüsse C 76, C 101, C 164 und C 179 — Die CDU Deutschlands lehnt Koalitionen und ähnliche Formen der Zusammenarbeit sowohl mit der
> Linkspartei als auch mit der Alternative für Deutschland ab."

— *Sonstige Beschlüsse des 31. Parteitags der CDU Deutschlands, 7. bis 8. Dezember [2018], Hamburg Messe* [CDU-PT31]. The party-congress
day of the vote is 8 December 2018 (the congress ran 7–8 December; the document dates the congress, not the motion — DERIVED as its last day).
The CDU's later paper *Unsere Haltung zu Linkspartei und AfD* [CDU-HALTUNG19] is kept but not relied on: its title is read, its body is
glyph-coded and its date is not read from it.

**CSU → AfD** — support-blocking ("Zusammenarbeit … ab – egal, auf …"), from **2023-07-24**:
> "Die Brandmauer gegen die AfD steht — Söder stellt klar: … Zusammenarbeit mit der AfD ab – egal, auf … Denn die AfD sei
> demokratiefeindlich, rechtsextrem und spalte die Gesellschaft. Das sei mit den Werten der CSU nicht vereinbar."

— *Sitzung des Parteivorstands: Keine Kooperation mit der AfD — Humanität und Ordnung bei der Migration*, CSU, 24 July 2023 [CSU-PV23].
⚠ The document's font is partly glyph-coded; the elisions (…) are words the extraction could not read, never words removed. **No CSU
resolution against Die Linke was found**: the CSU–Linke pair stays DERIVED.

## 2. The Union — one Fraktion

**CDU and CSU sit as one Fraktion** in the Bundestag, by § 10 Abs. 1 GO-BT as the Bundestag's Datenhandbuch quotes it [BT-DHB51]:
> "Die Fraktionen sind Vereinigungen von mindestens fünf Prozent der Mitglieder des Bundestages, die derselben Partei oder solchen Parteien
> angehören, die auf Grund gleichgerichteter politischer Ziele in keinem Land miteinander in Wettbewerb stehen."

The CSU stands in Bayern alone and the CDU in the other fifteen Länder (§697's candidacy facts), so the two do not compete in any Land.
The game carries this as a **joint group**: a cabinet holds both or neither, and outside a cabinet the two vote as one (the larger's side).

the party forms; it draws no line.

## 4. The reference — what actually formed

- **The count** (`returns_2025.md`, re-verified 2026-09-30 [BWL-99-25]): CDU 164 · AfD 152 · SPD 120 · Grüne 85 · Linke 64 · CSU 44 · SSW 1 ·
  BSW 0 · FDP 0 (630).
- **The chancellor's election** (Art. 63 GG) [BT-KW25]: on **6 May 2025** the Bundespräsident's candidate Friedrich Merz failed the first
  ballot — "Statt der erforderlichen 316 Stimmen erhielt der Kandidat aber nur 310 Stimmen" — and was elected the same day in a second
  ballot (Art. 63 Abs. 3), "mit 325 von 618 abgegebenen" votes.
- **The government**: CDU/CSU and SPD, Merz (CDU) chancellor, Lars Klingbeil (SPD) vice-chancellor [BT-BREG25, records_by_date.md §2].
