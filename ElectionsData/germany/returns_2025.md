### Electoral rules
- Pure Zweitstimmen-proportional allocation, Sainte-Laguë/Schepers: the 630 seats are distributed to parties solely according to nationwide Zweitstimmen by the Sainte-Laguë/Schepers divisor method — https://www.bundeswahlleiterin.de/bundestagswahlen/2025/informationen-waehler/wahlsystem.html (allocation provisions in the BWahlG, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bwahlg/BJNR003830956.html).
- 630 seats fixed: § 1(1) BWahlG — "Der Deutsche Bundestag besteht aus 630 Abgeordneten" — https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bwahlg/BJNR003830956.html
- No Überhang/Ausgleich: "Überhangmandate sind ab der Bundestagswahl 2025 wegen der erforderlichen Zweitstimmendeckung nicht mehr möglich"; compensatory seats thereby obsolete — https://www.bundeswahlleiterin.de/service/glossar/u/ueberhangmandate.html
- Zweitstimmendeckung: a plurality of Erststimmen in a constituency yields a seat only if covered by the party's Zweitstimmen seat entitlement; winners are ranked by descending first-vote share and seated in that order against the entitlement (§ 6(1) BWahlG) — https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bwahlg/BJNR003830956.html and https://www.bundeswahlleiterin.de/bundestagswahlen/2025/informationen-waehler/wahlsystem.html; upheld as constitutional by BVerfG 30 July 2024 — https://www.bundesverfassungsgericht.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2024/bvg24-064.html
- 5% threshold and Grundmandatsklausel status: § 4(2) sentence 2 no. 2 BWahlG sets the 5% threshold; the BVerfG judgment of 30 July 2024 (2 BvF 1/23 et al.) declared it unconstitutional in its 2023 form and ordered that, until re-legislation, it applies only to parties whose candidates won fewer than three constituencies — i.e. the Grundmandatsklausel was in force at the 2025 election — https://www.bundesverfassungsgericht.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2024/bvg24-064.html, restated by the Bundeswahlleiterin at https://www.bundeswahlleiterin.de/bundestagswahlen/2025/informationen-waehler/wahlsystem.html
- SSW minority exemption: § 4(2) BWahlG — the threshold clause "findet keine Anwendung auf Listen, die von Parteien nationaler Minderheiten eingereicht wurden"; hence SSW's 1 seat on 0.2% — https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bwahlg/BJNR003830956.html

### Caveats
- BSW is shown as 4.98% (kerg2.csv final value 4.981%); rounding to one decimal would give 5.0% and misstate its position below the threshold. FDP exact: 4.328%; CSU exact: 5.970%; CDU 22.551%, AfD 20.803%, SPD 16.413%, Grüne 11.606%, Linke 8.775%.
- Seat numbers come from the Bundeswahlleiterin final-result page (bund-99); kerg2.csv carries votes/shares, not party seat totals. Both are marked final (endgültiges Ergebnis; CSV Status "Endgültig", Stand 14.03.2025).
- Regional shares are computed to 0.1% directly from the official CSV's Land-level rows; the Bundeswahlleiterin's HTML per-Land pages present the same final data.
- Full party long-form names are the standard registered names; the abbreviations and all figures are as printed in the official sources. In 2025 no party actually entered via the Grundmandatsklausel alone (Die Linke cleared 5%; CSU won 44 constituencies and 5.97% — its participation is uncontested under § 4 BWahlG as applied); the clause's availability is the ruling of BVerfG press release 64/2024.
- Threshold basis: valid Zweitstimmen nationwide, 49,649,512 (kerg2.csv, Bund row "Gültige", Stimme 2).
