# BILLED - the price of a German support signature (SP-5, S5, 2026-10-30)

K165 rules ballot access "time plus money at a sourced price per signature". No primary source on disk prices a support signature
(Unterstuetzungsunterschrift) for a Bundestag list, so the price is BILLED and the game charges none: GATHER spends volunteer days only.
Design's "EUR 2.40" on the S5 board is ILLUSTRATIVE (its RETURN.md) and is not used.

Searched on 2026-10-30, nothing primary found:
- the statute and the ordinance in this folder fix the counts and the dates, not a cost; the Federal Returning Officer's
  candidates' page (bwl_btw25_informationen-wahlbewerber.html) says the voting-eligibility certificates are on the same form and names no fee;
- a web search for a per-signature price or a collection service's published rate (German small parties, the 2025 snap election)
  returned press reports of the burden (no figures) and no published price.

What would close it: a party's own published accounts (a Rechenschaftsbericht line for signature collection with the count collected),
or a collection service's own published per-signature rate, fetched byte-exact into this folder with its digest. Until then the money
half of K165 is not modelled, and the HQ prints "Per signature · BILLED".
