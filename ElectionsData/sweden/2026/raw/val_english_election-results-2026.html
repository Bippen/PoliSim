Voting patterns

In the 2026 Riksdag elections:

  • The number of eligible voters was 8,051,355.
  • The voter turnout was 84.9 per cent (6,834,413 people).
  • The share of personal votes was 22.2 per cent.
  • The advance voting share was 54.7 per cent (3,736,703 votes).
