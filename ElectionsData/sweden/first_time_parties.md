## The rule

A party is **first-time** at the first election 2006–2026 whose national list names it, when no list since 2002 names it (by abbreviation or by
its name, case and punctuation folded) **and**, on the 2010–2018 pages, Valmyndigheten gives it no previous-election figure. Both are needed:
the page's own previous column marks a party new when its *registration* changed (Direktdemokraterna is "new" on the 2018 page and listed on
the 2014 one), and a name match alone misses a renamed party (Landsbygdspartiet Oberoende 2014, formerly Landsbygdsdemokraterna; Liberalerna
2018, formerly Folkpartiet), which the page's previous figure catches. The 2002 list — including write-ins — is the base of "seen before" and
yields no first-timer itself. Lists are the parties that ordered ballots; the write-in total is not a party.
