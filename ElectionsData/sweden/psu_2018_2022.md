Reading: in MAY, support-lending from the bloc's large party to its threshold partner is small
and inside its own margin (M → KD 0.5 % of M's sympathisers in 2018, M → L 0.7 % in 2022),
while the threatened party's OWN sympathisers are already leaking to the larger partner (L → M
3.9 %, MP → S 8.7 % in 2022). The September lift therefore happened between May and the count —
the final weeks the PSU does not observe. **A May figure is a lower bound on the lending, not
its size.**

## Caveats

- The PSU is fielded in May and November; there is no PSU in the final week of a campaign. A
  final-week poll of record (the newspapers' commissioned polls, or the SVT/Valu exit poll) is
  NOT on disk and is billed (`DATA_BILL.md`) — any claim about the last week's swing waits for
  it.
- The May → September Δ mixes tactical lending with genuine movement over four months of
  campaign (KD's 2018 rise is widely attributed to both). The catalog records the figures, not
  the attribution.
- ± is SCB's published 95 % margin for the estimate; the Δ columns are arithmetic on the
  figures here, not SCB's.
