Vetoes by President Donald J. Trump

President Donald J. Trump vetoed 10 bills during his first administration.

 

116th Congress, 2nd Session (2020)
Bill No.    Subject    Veto Date    Presidential Message    Status
S.J.Res.68    Iran War Powers Resolution    May 06    S.Doc. 116-15    The Senate sustained the veto on May 7 by vote No. 84 (49-44).
H.J.Res.76    Borrower Defense Institutional Accountability regulation rule    May 29    H.Doc. 116-131    The House sustained the veto on June 26 by vote No. 120 (238-173).
H.R.6395    National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2021    Dec 23    H.Doc. 116-174    Veto overridden by the House on Dec 28 by vote No. 253 (322-87).

Veto overridden by the Senate on Jan 1, 2021 by vote No. 292 (81-13).

Veto overridden
