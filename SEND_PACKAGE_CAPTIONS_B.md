# The frames, by digest - one line each: the screen and its state (part B - Laws, Politics, Energy, the campaign, election night)

In the order a player meets them. **GAME** - the protocol's game, loaded and played forward by the game's own day path; where a frame needs a moment play takes hours to reach (a draft mid-drag, an act at signing, a decision pending) the harness sets that one state through the game's own types. **READING** - a staged reading of a screen (the campaign screens' three readings each; election night's four instants, the sourced 2022 night replayed). **SCENARIO** - a scenario starts its own world.

Every frame is the 1280 × 720 capture, losslessly optimised (oxipng 10.2.1, level max; the decoded pixels proved identical to the capture's through two decoders that share nothing with it). **The 2560 × 1440 frame of every one exists on disk under the same name with `2560` for `1280`, and is available by frame name.**
