## 7. What this layer does not do yet

The fleet does not invest or retire; the load does not grow (so the bills' share of a growing GDP drifts down - the missing load growth, stated); the reservoirs' seed fill and the water value's answer to a deficit have no source yet (the slope is authored and armed by probe; a dry year is stage 8's event); the neighbours outside the six are an exogenous exchange; the energy screen is a later stage's. The carbon tax reaches transport's tonnes and nothing in this layer (EN-4d, §467: ETS-covered plant is exempt of the national carbon tax by statute so the two prices do not stack, applied here at sector level for want of an installation register - the deviation, stated in EnergyMarket's class doc); since EN-4c its rate has one meaning - the country's currency per tonne of CO₂, the statutory figure at the seed. The fleet's carbon price is the ETS, exogenous - the 2023 mean carried by the price level, stepped only by a probe - until a stage gives it a path. Sweden's statutory indexation of its rate (2 kap. 1 b §) is not carried - a known divergence, its own row (EN-4e).
