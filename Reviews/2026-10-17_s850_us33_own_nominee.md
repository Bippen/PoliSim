### L1. The person-of-record refusal matches only the exact formal name

- **Where:**
  - `Assets/Scripts/Elections/UsTermLimit.cs:328-337` (`IsPersonOfRecord`), used at `Assets/Scripts/Data/OwnNominee.cs:124`.
  - The unnamed-form test at `OwnNominee.cs:123`.
- **What it does:** it compares NARA's formal names after trimming and ignoring case, and nothing more. Out of engine:
  - **Refused:** "Donald J. Trump", "donald j. trump", "Hillary Clinton", "Barack Obama", "Mitt Romney".
  - **Accepted:** "Donald Trump", "Trump", "Kamala Harris", "Joe Biden", "J.D. Vance", "Mike Pence", "Hillary Rodham Clinton", "Barack H. Obama", "Donald  J. Trump" (two spaces).
  - **Also accepted:** "Republican Party’s candidate (2028)". The test matches only the ASCII apostrophe. This one is cosmetic: the game's labels use ASCII, so the name collides with nothing.
- **Scenario, run out of engine:**
  - A REP nominee named "Donald Trump" wins the 2024 count. `ElectionsOf("Donald Trump")` is 1.
  - In 2028, REP fields "Donald Trump" with the basis "the president, who may be elected again - elected 2024".
  - He is barred only in 2032 ("elected 2024 and 2028").
  - The limit is consistent with its own string rule, since no record election is carried over. But the declared reading ("your nominee is a new person") is defeated by a common variant of the name.
- **Fix:**
  - Normalise before comparing: collapse internal whitespace, drop middle initials and generational suffixes, and compare first name plus `WorldClock.SurnameOf`.
  - Or state the limitation in the declared reading.
  - In both tests, treat ’ the same as '.

It is reachable wherever `PlayerPartyAbbrev` is re-seated. `UiScreenshotDriver.cs:1930` and `:3603` (bills staging) re-seat the prime minister's party during a `-shotcreatestart` run.
- **Fix:** do one of these, and say which in the doc:
  - drop the `PlayerPartyAbbrev` condition in `OwnOf`, so the registry (one per country) is the authority; or
  - apply the same condition to the moved point.
