# K300 prereg: the majority bonus w, committed before its code and before its first Unity run (2026-10-29)

K300 (Elias, 2026-10-29) rules variant (B) of K289's measurement: a cabinet with a majority of its own is preferred. It supersedes K273: the support term is dropped (f = 0).

**Honest note.**
- Variant (B) was measured on w = 0 to 0.30 out of Unity (`Tools/k273_support_sweep`, mode `majority-bonus`, on `wave/k273` 816b27e2) before this prereg, and that measurement is what Elias ruled on. It found records 7/7 and every Sweden 2022 row unmoved for w = 0.07 to 0.30, with 0.30 the grid's own end.
- **The lower edge is Poland's own margin.** At the record's seats KO+TD on NL outscores KO+TD+NL by 1.979 points (1.980 at the game's count), and the bonus at w is w × 30 points, so w must exceed 1.979 / 30 = 0.066. The rule is set by the very record it lifts. That is why the acceptance below adds what the four records cannot see: the Sweden 2022 rows and the in-run governments.
- Everything below is fixed before the grid is extended past 0.30 and before any Unity run. The bars may not re-choose w.

## 1. The rule measured

The bonus is `w × CoalitionFormation.WeightSeatStrength × 100`. It is added to a cabinet's own score (the base score that the formation ranks and every party's hold-out reads) when the cabinet's OWN seats reach the chamber's absolute majority, `CoalitionMath.Majority` (more than half of all seats).
- Under positive investiture (the Sejm's Art. 154, the Bundestag's Art. 63, Italy's art. 94) that majority is the investiture line itself.
- Under the Riksdag's negative rule, a cabinet holding it can never be refused, because at most total − majority < majority can vote against.
- So one definition serves both rules: the seats at which a cabinet needs no one outside it.

The support term is gone (f = 0). Nothing else in the formation changes.

## 2. The grid

w ∈ {0.00, 0.01, …, 1.00}. 1.00 is the scale's natural end: the bonus then equals the seat-strength term of a cabinet holding the whole chamber. Above it, the bonus would outweigh the seat term it is a fraction of.

  - Rows under the other investiture rule are reported, not accepted on: no Swedish government is formed under positive investiture.

## 4. The choice

- **The passing interval** is the longest contiguous run of passing grid points (on a tie, the lower run): [w_lo, w_hi].
- **The chosen w** is its midpoint, rounded down to the grid.
- **Why the midpoint:** it is furthest from both edges. w_lo is Poland's margin, which a seat or two in the count moves. w_hi is the next flip, or the scale's end.
- **The smallest passing w is rejected:** it sits on Poland's margin by construction.
- **If w_hi is the grid's end** (no flip below 1.00), the midpoint is taken against that end. The report says so.

## 5. The acceptance in-engine (iii)

The dump is `TrajectoryBaselineDump`, both of its seeds (777 and 424242), its default horizon, at the chosen w. Its log is read against main's last dump log by `Tools/k273_support_sweep`, mode `dump-check <branch log> <main log>`. The tool reports, per seed, per country and per election, the government installed: cabinet, head and support. Then:

- **(a)** No installed government in any country is a lone-party minority carried by a party it is red-lined against.
  - A lone-party minority is one party, or one parliamentary group such as CDU+CSU, holding fewer seats than the chamber's majority.
  - Red-lined means any red line, declared or derived, cabinet or support-blocking, in either direction, between the cabinet's party and a supporter.
  - The lines read are those in the reading of the election that seated the chamber. A mid-term formation reads its own day's lines, so this is an approximation for mid-term installs, and the tool says which installs are mid-term.
- **(b)** No country's in-run governments are newly NO GOVERNMENT.
  - Matched on polling day: an election after which the branch's log installs no government before the next election (or the run's end), where main's log installed one.
  - Where polling days diverge, counted per seed and country: the branch's count of elections with no government installed may not exceed main's.
- The USA and France form no government by this formation (the House and the presidency; France's governing mode). They are reported as such.
