## New findings

### Low

**N1. `Conserve`'s statute quote is attributed to lett. a, which says something else.** `ItalianCount.cs:701`: "art. 17 lett. a and b divide 'la somma delle cifre elettorali delle liste ammesse al riparto per il numero di seggi'". The raw D.Lgs. 533/1993 has that phrase only in lett. b ("... per il numero di seggi individuato ai sensi della lettera a)"). Lett. a reads "divide il totale delle cifre elettorali regionali delle coalizioni di liste ... e delle singole liste ... per il numero di seggi da attribuire nei collegi plurinominali della regione". The substance is the same (a floored quotient that can be zero), but quote each letter's own words.

**N2. (j)'s 300-vote wide sweep would have passed before K283.** `ItalianCountDiagnostic.cs:149-162` uses seed 283, each list 0-3× its share, roster 1. Its tables are identical at f22bf02f and 1a5ad92c, so it never reaches a lost seat. Only (j)'s single review vote bites. To make the sweep a real guard, aim it at the failure family: a coalition's only seated list falling below its 2018 share while its other lists sit at 1-3 %. Review 3's targeted generator lost seats in about half of its votes. Alternatively, drop the sweep's claim.

**N3. The Senato fallback leaves no trace.** Lett. b's fallback (`ItalianCount.cs:687`) seats lists that lett. b does not admit, namely a coalition's lists under 3 % with no 20 % here. When it fires and `FlooredHare` fills the unit, nothing goes into `Trace` and `ListSeatsShort` stays 0. In the crafted Toscana case, AVS, +E and IC took seats and the trace was empty. Every other K283 path logs "seated by K283's rule", so add the same line here. It is reachable only on crafted cifre: a coalition admitted solely on a member's regional 20 %.

**N4. Seat changes can still occur in long simulations, but only on votes that lost seats before.** K283 changes the count wherever it used to lose seats. 2022 and every conserving vote are byte-identical, so nothing pinned on the record moves. A seeded multi-decade run that ever produces such an Italian vote would now seat differently. That cannot be checked without Unity. The bar's simulation group will show it if it happens.
