# Review: PS-1's code half - the world clock; the family `ps1wc` (2026-09-25, s618)

**What was reviewed.** The staged change (the patch `git diff HEAD -- Assets/Scripts Assets/Editor` plus the two new files whole) to the money paths `Assets/Scripts/Simulation/SimulationManager.cs` (the epoch settable, `SetEpoch`, `DefaultEpoch`, `ResetClockToEpoch`, `EpochScope`), `Assets/Scripts/Data/PartySystem.cs` (`ElectionVintage`'s new members, `SeatsAt`, `InitialSeats(id, vintage)`, `SeatsSourced`, `ChamberSizeAt`, `IsPlayable`, the Poland units SLD/PSL/MN, `TryHistory`'s resolve), `Assets/Scripts/Data/WorldFactory.cs` (the chamber of record seated, the capital's mandate), `Assets/Scripts/Elections/WorldClock.cs` (new), `Assets/Scripts/Elections/SeatedGovernment.cs` (`TryAt`), `Assets/Scripts/Elections/DeclaredRedLines.cs` and `LiveCampaignSetup.cs` (the resolve), `Assets/Scripts/Persistence/SaveGame.cs` and `SaveGameService.cs` (the epoch in the save, format 25), with the UI and Editor files beside them, and the baseline's re-base from `k1ep` to `ps1wc` (`TrajectorySentinelCheck.cs`: seed 777 `438e1148d46691f5086116373a6ab3d5cb08d92087b807931f2c1a1d83569446`, seed 424242 `fc163628c6dd184b47c6d472d6923343ba3345913ff511118ee372b5dc6ee905`).
