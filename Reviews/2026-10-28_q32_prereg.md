# MF q32 hold - the pass rule, registered before DebtPathHoldDiagnostic's first run (2026-10-28)

Drafted by the implementer of `wave/q32` (6e862446, after the review's L5; unchanged at ff07b2a2); committed by the integrator before the first run.

Each country's no-policy century (NoPolicyCentury, seed 777, its own start) is read over the same calendar years as the source (DebtPathAnchors, generated from the stored workbooks). The model is read from its opening state at its start's calendar year S (a mid-year start read as its year; the record after y years read as S + y), the source from year S, and the CHANGE in the debt ratio is compared:
- (h1) Sweden, Germany, France, Italy, Poland: the change from S to 2029 lies within [DSM p10(2029) - DSM baseline(S), DSM p90(2029) - DSM baseline(S)].
- (h2) The same five: the change from S to 2035 lies within +/- half of (p90(2029) - p10(2029)) of DSM baseline(2035) - DSM baseline(S).
- (h3) USA: from F = max(S, 2026), the change from F to F + 10 lies within +/-10 points of CBO(F + 10) - CBO(F), and from F to F + 30 within +/-25 points of CBO(F + 30) - CBO(F).

A start outside 2022-2029 for the five, or a century or CBO series that does not reach a horizon, fails as UNVERIFIED. A miss fails, is printed as BAND MISS, and is put to Elias (under his standing instruction of 2026-10-27, Code's recommendation is recorded with the measurement). A debt crisis inside a horizon is printed beside the band. Bands (h2) and (h3) are [AUTHORED-DRAFT].
