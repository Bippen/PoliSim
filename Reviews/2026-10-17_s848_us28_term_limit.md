### LOW

**L1. `Pick` accepts any date, not only a polling day.**

- **Where:** `UsTermLimit.cs:420-435`. Nothing checks that `pollingDay` is `WorldClock.TryNextPollingDay(country.Id, pollingDay)`.
- **Scenario (harness S9):** a REP player with the game's 2024 Trump contest picks for 2028-11-08, and again for 2030-11-05.
  - Both return true and both are stored (`UsNomineePicks.Count` 1, then 2).
  - `TryPickOf` matches the exact date (`:410`), so these orphan picks are never read, but they ride the save.
- **Impact:** the UI passes only `TryNextPollingDay(today + 1)`, so play cannot reach this.
- **Fix:** refuse with "that day is not a presidential polling day" unless `TryNextPollingDay(country.Id, pollingDay.Date, out d) && d == pollingDay.Date`.

**L2. Two of the refusals are untested, though the report says all five are.**

- **What `TermLimitDiagnostic` tests:**

  - not term-limited: (f);
  - day already counted: never tested;
  - the field of record (`:427`): never tested.
- **Fix:** in (e), after `byPick` is counted, assert `!Pick(usa, Polled2028, "REP", Vance, ...)` with the counted reason and the pick count unchanged. Also assert `!Pick(..., Polled2024, ...)` on a world with no contest.

**L3. The class doc presents reading 3's 2024 half as a consequence of R-US2 (a).**

- **Why it matters:** this reading decides the main path, where Vance is REP's 2028 default after a game Trump win.
