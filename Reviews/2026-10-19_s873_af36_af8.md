# Review: AF-36 (ruling q36) - `git diff 920c4b65 wave/af36` (e73d017f)

Reviewer: independent adversarial pass, read only (worktree `G:/UNITY/Projects/PoliSim-wt/af36`). No Unity run.

## Verdict: SHIP WITH FIXES

The sim side holds. Clearing `TaxLine.PassedForYear` in `CarbonRateStatute.AdvanceYear` is inert for the trajectory. I traced every reader and every branch below. The yield date matches the boundary the statute moves the figure at. The carry is reachable only from the player's draft. Two things need fixing before ship. The carry draft never clears, so a box left ticked carries the figure forward forever. And the carry is invisible to the chamber's concern and to the hike's approval cost.

## Findings

### M1 - The carry draft is sticky: once its bill passes it is a live change again, and every later player budget re-carries the figure
- `Assets/Scripts/UI/GameController.cs:185` (`_taxCarryInputs`) is cleared only in `RestoreUiDrafts` (:996). `ResetPolicyInputs` (:6199) never touches it. Rate drafts clear themselves at rest (GA-8-2, `GameController.BudgetV35.cs:540`); the carry has no equivalent.
- `CarbonCarryCarries` (`GameController.cs:9606-9612`) returns true while `PassedForYear < DraftPassageYear + 1`.
- Scenario (Germany or Sweden, calendar fiscal year): the bill passes on 22 Jan Y with the carry, so the stamp becomes Y+1. Introducing the bill closed the process (`SimulationManager.cs:1793`), so `PassageYear` now tables at next 1 January and returns Y+1 (`SimulationManager.cs:6177-6191`). Y+1 < Y+2, so the carry counts again right away. The Budget page shows "1 change" (`BudgetV35.cs:121`) and the slip reads "THE BILL CARRIES 80 INTO Y+2". The next January's bill re-stamps Y+2, and so on. The box stays filled from last year, so as long as the player passes a budget each year the statute never takes the figure back.
- The comment at `GameController.cs:9674-9675` claims "a carry left on counts no change once it has done its work". That is true only until the passage estimate rolls over to the next year, which happens the day the bill is introduced.
- Fix: drop `TaxType.CarbonTax` from `_taxCarryInputs` once a bill carrying it passes, for example in `ResetPolicyInputs` by comparing the stamp against `_carbonRateBeforeTurn`'s pattern, or when the pending budget resolves. Alternatively, record the stamp the draft was made against and treat the carry as spent once the stamp is at least that value + 1. If chained carries are meant to be a standing policy, Elias should rule on it. q36 says "into the next year".

### M2 - The chamber and the approval cost never see a carry
- `ParliamentSystem.GetBudgetBillConcern` (`ParliamentSystem.cs:1034-1071`) reads `bill.TaxLines` only. The hike in `ApplyBillResult` (:566-584) counts only lines the bill moves. The carry block (:589-597) adds neither.
- Scenario (Germany): 80 EUR/t passes in 2025 and pays its +25-point hike. A 2026 bill that only carries is an empty tax concern and costs no hike. Yet without that bill the 2026 boundary would set the law's 55, and past the schedule the carried 80 becomes the base forever (`AdvanceYear`'s after-schedule branch, the reading q36 confirmed). The same economic decision, re-passing 80 after the revert, costs a 55 to 80 hike and moves the chamber. A carry is a free way to keep a rate above (or below) the statute.
- Fix: price a carry as the move it prevents. That is the passed figure minus the statute's figure at `YieldsOn`, on `StanceAxis.Redistribution` in the concern and in `totalHike`. Or get Elias's ruling that a carry is costless and state the premise.

### L1 - `ApplyBillResult`'s carry guard is weaker than the UI's
`ParliamentSystem.cs:594` checks `PassedForYear > 0`, not `CarbonRateStatute.PassedFigureStands`. It also does not check `StatuteMoves`. On a pre-AF-36 save, a stale stamp (left after the statute already moved the line) would be extended by a carry, freezing the statute's own figure. Today this is unreachable: the UI filters with `PassedFigureStands` (`GameController.cs:9610`) and no older save holds a carry. Defence in depth: use `PassedFigureStands(country.Id, line, <last boundary year>)` here, or pass the last boundary year in.

### L2 - The slip's "PASSED IN {passage}" is wrong for a carry of a standing figure
`BudgetV35.cs:566`: when `carries` is true because an earlier figure is standing (no new figure drafted), the slip says the figure was "PASSED IN" the draft's passage year. It was passed in `PassedForYear`'s year. Word it as "CARRIED BY A BILL OF {passage}".

### L3 - The tabling-to-passage gap can silently void a drafted carry
`CarbonCarryCarries` is evaluated when the bill is built, but the stamp is read when the bill passes, 21 days later (`BillDurationDays`). A boundary in that gap that moves the line clears the stamp, and the carry then carries nothing. The slip promised it would. Reachable only for starts whose boundary falls between 1 and 22 January. Either state it or have the yield date shown at tabling.

### L4 - Calendar copy: "indexed by the statute from today" on a future date
`GameController.cs:9624` (caps:false) is used for the Desk calendar entry (`:3374`), which sits on the yield day, not today. Use "from that day".

### L5 - Diagnostic section 8 does not prove the "inert" claim
`CarbonTaxUnitDiagnostic` section 8 asserts the stamp is cleared and the carried rates. It never runs a no-clear control or a passed-zero case, so "inert for the trajectory" rests on reading the code. That reading is below and holds. It also never exercises the UI gate (`CarbonCarryCarries` / `BuildBudgetBillFromDrafts`), which is where M1 lives. The checks it does make are non-vacuous: a null `ShownYield` fails the `!=` against the boundary, `Near` uses a relative tolerance well inside one year's ratio, and the Sweden "carry of the statute's figure" case would fail if a carry stamped it.

## Checked and clean
- **Clear inertness.** The only readers of `PassedForYear` are `AdvanceYear` :126, :127 and :143, plus `TaxLine.Clone` and the new carry and UI code.
  - :143 is read only at `currentTurn == 0`, and no clear can happen before the first boundary.
  - :127: the clear runs only once `stamp < boundaryYear`, and later boundaries have larger years.
  - :126 (`Rate <= 0 && stamp == 0`) skips a cleared passed zero. The statute maps 0 to 0 in every branch reachable there: Sweden 0 × ratio; Germany past the schedule with `currentTurn > 0` gives 0 × ratio; in-schedule years set a non-zero scheduled figure first; France makes no move.
  - The preview's `AdvanceYear` (`SimulationManager.cs:6311`) runs on deep-cloned lines (`ClonePreviewTaxLines`, `TaxLine.Clone` copies the stamp).
- **Yield date.** `YieldsOn` (first boundary from `CurrentTurn+1` whose year is greater than the stamp) is exactly `AdvanceYear`'s `stamp >= boundaryYear` skip. `LastBoundaryYear` = `TurnBoundary(CurrentTurn).Year` agrees with `ComingBoundaryDate` = `TurnBoundary(CurrentTurn+1)`. `CarbonYieldWords`' first-boundary case (`on == TurnBoundary(1) && stamp < LastScheduledYear`) mirrors `lawsFigureInForce`.
- **Germany and Sweden.** A carried figure stands unindexed and then moves by one year's ratio (`PriceLevelAtLastIndex` advances every boundary). Germany past the schedule carries the passed figure as its base, as GA-8-1 and q36 read it.
- **Stale stamps on older saves.** `PassedFigureStands` reads them as the statute's figure, and the next moving boundary clears them.
- **Who can carry.** Only `BuildBudgetBillFromDrafts` writes `CarriedTaxLines`. AI bills (`AiFinanceMinistry`, the partner's tax act) never carry. `WithoutRateChanges` drops the carry, `TaxActPart` keeps it, and `Without(other part)` shares the list read-only. `UsBudgetProcedure.PartOf` drops it, but that is a concern-only bill and the USA has no carbon line. Poland's line is not implemented and `StatuteMoves` is false, so no Polish carry exists.
- **No-player worlds.** No draft and no carry, so the trajectory dump is untouched.
- **Save.** Newtonsoft `ObjectCreationHandling.Auto` fills the initialised empty list. An older save has no field, so the list stays empty. `ui?.TaxCarryDrafts` is null-guarded. No format bump is needed.
- **IMGUI.** Control 3 is drawn on a predicate fixed for the session (`IsPerTonne && StatuteMoves(country)`), after the slider. A disabled button still takes its ID. Labels are Repaint-only. The hit rect starts at least 12 px right of `switchHit.xMax`, so it does not overlap the switch.
- **Locale.** Years use InvariantCulture. Dates use `CurrentCulture` "d MMMM yyyy", the Desk's existing convention. The diagnostic's `F()` and `Day()` are invariant.
- **Screenshot driver.** It puts back the stamp, the carry draft, the pins and `_onDesk`, and it fails loudly if the date is not shown.
