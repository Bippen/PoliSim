## Findings

### M1. K65 as composed gives a party a premium for its size: a large party in a quiet field gains up to 1.43× its sourced CACE
`CampaignPersuasion.cs:192-213` (`RelativeToField` composed with `Apply`).
The relative effects are e_p − r_p·ē, so they do not sum to zero: every idle party gets −s_S·e. `Apply` then absorbs each idle party's negative effect in proportion to the other parties' shares, so the large party also gets back the idle parties' "losses" in proportion to its own share. I measured on Sweden's prior (`probe_out.txt`):
- S campaigning alone at +0.40 moves **+0.573**, and at +0.80 moves **+1.147**. The premium is linear, not a rounding effect. Before K65, `Apply` gave +0.400.
- S and SD at equal effort (+0.40 each) with the rest idle: S **+0.535**, SD **+0.417**. Equal effort between two parties no longer gives equal gains. The large one gains more because it is large.
- L campaigning alone gains +0.388.

**This is not forced by "relative to the field".** A sum-preserving absorption balanced on both axes, Δ = e − W·e with W doubly stochastic, zero diagonal and close to share-weighted, also reads each party's effect against a weighted mean of the field's. It also moves nothing under equal effort, and it holds a lone campaigner at exactly its sourced e. The size premium comes from this composition, not from the ruling. It re-opens the size-neutrality that review 881b's N1 defended. §881 rejected candidate (i) because it made the effect depend on party size: small parties' effects shrank 4-5×. This form makes it depend on size the other way. Criterion (d) is still asserted only for the run-up (`Apply`). No check bounds the live share move, and the plan's (4)(b) bound is held on the pre-relative `CampaignEffectPp`.
*Scenario:* a player who leads the poll with 30 % outspends a field whose small parties are broke (in my live AI field MP's whole effect is +0.007 pp). Every point of the player's sourced effect lands as about 1.4 points. That is above the CACE's upper bound, which the bound check never sees.
*Fix:* put it to Elias in ask mode with the two readings and their measured lone and pairwise moves. If he wants the sourced size kept, build the balanced absorption, and assert that a lone campaigner's move equals e within 1E-9. The comment's DECLARED line is not a ruling.

### M2. K66's overlap factor min(1, r_target / r_attacker) is wrong for disjoint reaches, and Germany's CDU and CSU are disjoint
`CampaignPersuasion.cs:169`, `CampaignActions.cs:544`, and the diagnostic's mirror.
The factor assumes the smaller reach lies inside the larger one. It holds for every Swedish party and for the SSW against a national party. It does not hold for the CDU (0.836, every Land but Bayern) and the CSU (0.164, Bayern only), whose true overlap is **0**. Measured with `AttackEffectPp` at half an electorate of television exposure, all of it an attack (`PROBE_OVERLAP=1`):
- CSU on CDU: **0.0437 pp**, because the factor reads 1.
- CDU on CSU: **0.0207 pp**, because the factor reads 0.196. That is more than a national party takes from the CSU (0.0181), although no voter the CDU reaches can vote CSU.

*Scenario:* a German player as the CDU sets AGAINST: CSU and airs television. The CSU loses share among voters who never had it on the ballot. The 60 % of each contact spent on the attack is also taken from the CDU's own case. The AGAINST chip offers this target, and nothing refuses it.
*Fix:* compute the overlap from `Setup.Stands` per region: the electorate share where both parties stand, over the attacker's reach. `CampaignPressure` holds only the reach scalars, so pass the overlap matrix in, or compute the attack's effect where the regional tally is known. Then plant a CDU→CSU attack in `GermanCampaignDiagnostic` and assert it moves the CSU by 0.

### M3. K6 keeps the AI's [AUTHORED-DRAFT] strategy multipliers on the sourced CACE, and the player plays at 1.0
`CampaignRun.cs:886`. `Quality(trace)` is `Persuasion / weight` (`CampaignActions.cs:431-435`), so a strategy's `PersuasionMultiplier` scales q, and with q the class effect, up to the upper limit. For example: Populist on its focus issue ×1.5, SwingVoter 0.7 + 0.8·swing (about 1.18 at Sweden's loyalty), Negative ×0.8. Row 4 of the q4 table says "CUT as its own modifier", and A5 asked only whether the AI's *choosing* stays. K6 answers "the AI's five personalities keep choosing actions by strategy". That leaves open whether they keep an unsourced size multiplier the player cannot get. R2 and K5 say a choice moves where an effect lands, never its size. The measured symptom: the heavy scripted S campaign's own effect fell from +0.099 (base) to +0.048 (HEAD) at the same script (seed 777). Part of that is K66, and part is the dropped modifier.
*Fix:* add it to M1's ask. Either the AI keeps choosing by strategy at multiplier 1.0, or the asymmetry is ruled and declared.
