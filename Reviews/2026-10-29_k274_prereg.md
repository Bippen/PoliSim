# K274 - the AI USA's primary-balance reaction: the pass rule, registered before the first measurement of the reaction (2026-10-29)

Drafted by the implementer of `wave/k274` and committed on that branch BEFORE the reaction was first run (the "before" century below is the tree at 2dcc227c, which carries no US reaction). The integrator commits this text into the plan before its own run.

**What is measured.** `Tools/headless_hold/frf_play.sh <root> on none 100 <seed>` - the all-AI world (no player, every country `PolicyDecision.None`, a turn a year, Sweden's start), out of the engine - at seeds 777 and 424242, on the tree before (2dcc227c) and after (the `wave/k274` head). The USA's row each year.

**The coefficient, fixed before measurement.** Bohn (1998), "The Behavior of U.S. Public Debt and Deficits", Table I, column (1), 1916-95: the response of the primary surplus over GDP to the start-of-year debt over GDP, **0.054**. It is the paper's headline estimate (its Table III, model 1, repeats it as the linear model). It is applied as read; it is NOT tuned to the result. The other columns (0.028 to 0.054) and Table III's non-linear models are printed in the record as the alternatives a ruling could choose, never chosen by this measurement.

**The acceptance, DECLARED before measurement:**
- **(A) no US default in a century, on both seeds** - the USA's `SovereignCrisisState.Crises` is 0 at year 100 of frf_play at 777 and at 424242.
- **(B) the debt near CBO's long-term path at year 30, on both seeds** - the USA's debt ratio at year 30 within +/-25 points of CBO's figure for the matched calendar year: frf_play opens in 2026 (Sweden's start), so year 30 is 2056 by `DebtPathHoldDiagnostic`'s declared calendar rule (the record after y years is the opening year plus y), and CBO's figure is 175.076 % (federal debt held by the public, end of FY2056, *The Long-Term Budget Outlook Data: 2026 to 2056*, CBO publication 62044, February 2026, sheet "2. Debt and Deficits"). The band is [150.076, 200.076]. The width is q32's (h3) thirty-year band ([AUTHORED-DRAFT], preregistered at 8e07e1d9), reused, not new. DECLARED: (B) reads the LEVEL, as the item's words ("debt near CBO's long-term path") do, and this crosses a perimeter - the model's US stock is gross federal debt, CBO's is debt held by the public (about 24 points lower at the opening: the model's 124.69 % at year 1 against CBO's 100.605 % for 2026).
- **Printed, not judged:** the CHANGE reading (the model's change from its opening to year 30 against CBO's change 2026-2056, +74.471 points) - the reading `DebtPathHoldDiagnostic` (h3) holds the no-policy-change run to; before K274 the all-AI USA already rises less than CBO over thirty years, and a reaction to debt can only widen that, so this reading is reported beside (B) and not used to accept. Also printed: the debt ratio every ten years, the default years, the peak, revenue, total and non-interest spending and the primary balance as shares of GDP, before and after, both seeds.

**What follows a miss.** The item reports FAIL on the criterion missed, with the measurement. The coefficient is not re-chosen to pass. Code's recommendation is recorded with the measurement (Elias's standing instruction of 2026-10-27): either Bohn's Table III quadratic (its own stronger slope at high debt), or another column of Table I, measured again under a prereg of its own.
