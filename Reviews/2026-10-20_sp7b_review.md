## Findings (ranked)

### M1: Held statutes cannot be answered after a load (the plate is the only surface)
`GameController.PresidentialStart.cs:54` (`BuildSigningOrAnswer`), `GameController.cs:973` (`_signingQueue.Clear()` on load), `GameController.cs:2252` (the high-water mark).

The answer plate is the only caller of `SignDocketBill`/`VetoDocketBill`, and it is reached only when a division is first queued above `_seenDivisionNumber`.

- **Scenario:** a save is cut on 12 Aug with three statutes held (the diagnostic's case (e)) and then loaded. The mark is restored, the queue is cleared, and no plate is ever raised for the held statutes. The player-president cannot veto any of them, and each is signed on 1 Sep.
- **Same effect:** a ceremony dropped on a build failure ("a dropped ceremony is a silent resolution").
- **Text that claims more than the code does:** the plate cannot be dismissed unanswered, and it stops the clock. So "SIGN OR VETO BY <date> · UNANSWERED, IT IS SIGNED" offers a deferral that does not exist. In play, the 21- and 7-day terms only ever run out on these broken paths.
- **Fix:** after a load (and each day), queue an answer plate for every `GetDocket` item with `Answer == 0` that has not been shown, or add an answer row to the docket page. Then either reword the plate line or add a "later" answer.

### M2: Party levers are not handed to the AI while the player is President
`SimulationManager.cs:3809` (`LeaveGovernment`), `:3724` (`WithdrawSupport`); the UI chips at `GameController.ParliamentRows.cs:1082` and `:121`.

I14 (2a) as built says "the role gate refuses every lever" (`SimulationManager.PlayerPresident.cs` class doc), but these two verbs check only `Government.RoleOf(PlayerPartyAbbrev)`.

- **Scenario:** on 15 Jan 2025 TD (Hołownia) and NL (Biejat) are junior partners in KO+TD+NL. If either candidate wins, the desk still draws "LEAVE THE GOVERNMENT" and the call succeeds. The President pulls his "AI" party out of the cabinet and re-apportions the portfolios. "WITHDRAW SUPPORT" is open in the same way for a support party.
- `MoveNoConfidence` is refused for Poland only because its rules are `Unsourced`. That is not a presidency check.
- **Fix:** add `if (country.PlayerHoldsPresidency) { refusedBecause = PresidentOfficeWords; return false; }` to both verbs, and hide the chips when `PlayerHoldsPresidency` is true.

### M3: A budget window open at the oath is never closed, so no budget is ever tabled
`SimulationManager.PlayerPresident.cs:46` (`NotePlayerPresidency`), `SimulationManager.cs:3113` (`TryOpenBudgetProcess`).

The oath turns `PlayerGoverns` false but never calls `CloseBudgetWindowIfNotGoverning`. That is the §698 class, which every other government change handles.

- **Scenario:** the player is Trzaskowski (KO is the prime minister's party, the diagnostic's own world) with THE BUDGET WINDOW hold off. The arrival window opens on 15 Jan 2025 and is never used. On 6 Aug, `TryOpenBudgetProcess` returns at `_pendingBudgetProcessByCountry.Contains` every day from then on, so the AI government never tables a budget for the whole presidency. The player's own `IntroduceBudgetBill` is refused by the role gate.
- If the hold is turned back on later, the clock holds on a window nobody may use, which is a soft-lock.
- **Fix:** in `NotePlayerPresidency`, when `holds` turns true, call `CloseBudgetWindowIfNotGoverning(country)`. The reverse transition needs nothing.

### L1: The held budget's year depends on when the acts are answered, and disagrees with `PassageYear`
`SimulationManager.PlayerPresident.cs:226-227`, `SimulationManager.cs:6279`.

The loop gives `year = PresentedOn.Year` only while the budget act is still in `ActsHeld`.

- If the acts are answered together, the year is the adoption year.
- If the budget act lapses on D+8 and a statute act on D+22, the year is `CurrentDate.Year`.
- `PassageYear` (the preview's carbon stamp) uses `LawDay(…).Year`, the 21-day count.
- The US held acts use `CurrentDate.Year`, so "as the US budget's held acts do" is not true for the year.

**Scenario:** a budget adopted on 20 Dec 2027 with a tax act left unanswered applies on 11 Jan with year 2028, while one answered at once applies with 2027. The per-tonne carbon line's `PassedForYear`, and its carry, then differ by a year, and the preview showed something else again. The answer timing should not decide the year.

**Fix:** pick one rule. Either use `CurrentDate.Year`, the law day, as the US does and as `PassageYear` declares, or store the adoption date on the bill and make `PassageYear` agree. All held entries share `PresentedOn`, so `ActsHeld[0].PresentedOn.Year` is a one-line fix if the adoption year is intended.

### L2: A held budget can swallow the next year's budget
`SimulationManager.cs:3113`. `TryOpenBudgetProcess` returns while the slot is occupied, and the AI path tables only on the request day or on arrival. A Polish budget held across 1 January therefore skips that whole year's budget.

The plate forces a next-day decision, so this needs an unanswered hold (M1's paths): an adoption from about 10 Dec is unanswered past 1 Jan. Fixing M1 makes this unlikely. Otherwise, let the 1 January request open once the slot clears.

### L3: Possible NullReferenceException in the PRESIDENT slip
`GameController.Desk.cs:333`. `PartySystems.ShortName(id, g.PmParty).ToUpperInvariant()`: `ShortName` returns null for a null or empty abbreviation, and every other reader guards `PmParty` (`ParliamentRows.cs:709/736`, `FinancePartner.cs:120`, `EconomicVote.cs:65`). A provisional or empty government under a player-president throws while the desk draws. **Fix:** guard it the way `ParliamentRows.cs:736` does.

### L4: The no-policy and except-worlds never answer the docket
`ShadowBaseline.cs` (fork by save round trip, with `PlayerCountryId` kept). In every fork the player-president's statutes and budgets are held to D+22 or D+8. In the played world the plate decides them on D+1, and his vetoes never happen in the forks. The difference is booked to every family the player touched before the oath: the §865 M4 class that FORK-1 fixed for lapses. This is inherited from US-21b, but Poland now reaches it in ordinary play. **Fix:** mirror the played world's docket answers into the forks, or have a fork answer as the played world did.

### L5: The President row hides the re-election verdict
`GameController.PresidentialStart.cs:82`. The in-office branch returns "YOU" first. Between the re-election's first vote and the successor's oath, the row never says whether the player is in the run-off, out, or lost. **Fix:** check `PlayerContestOn(today)` for a contest whose `TakesOffice > today` before the in-office line.

- `SigningScreen.cs:867-869`: the new `AnswerLineWidth` and `BuildAnswerButtons` were inserted between `BuildSignButton`'s `<summary>` and the method, so `AnswerLineWidth` now carries two summaries and `BuildSignButton` has none.
- `PresidentialDocket.cs:126`: `<see cref="LastDay"/>` is now ambiguous between two overloads (CS0419 if XML docs are generated).
