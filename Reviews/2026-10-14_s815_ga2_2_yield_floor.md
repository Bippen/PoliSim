# Review - §815, the gameplay audit's Phase 4: GA-2-2, a statute that yields nothing raises nothing (2026-10-14)

One adversarial reviewer, a single read-only agent, read the change as a patch prepared outside the tree while §814's bars ran (`Assets/Scripts/Data/TaxSchedule.cs`: `TaxSchedule.YieldRatio` and `TaxSchedule.EffectiveRate`), against the file in the tree and every reader of the two functions. It was told the defect (GA-2-2: a yield at or below zero answered a ratio of one, the seed's whole revenue, which France's lever at 0 met), the approved fix ("a yield at or below zero is a ratio of zero; the documented fallbacks stay as they are") and the numeric-inertness rule (§402/§501), and asked to break the no-policy game's byte-identity, the readings at and near zero (Sweden's credit, the sub-rows, the memo), any other site with the same fallback, any test asserting the old behaviour, and the comments. It edited nothing and ran nothing that writes. Its report follows verbatim, then what was done.

## The report

**0 HIGH / 1 MEDIUM (an older defect next to this change, not caused by it) / 3 LOW.** Verdict: the patch is correct and matches the approved fix text. Tracing the code says it does not change the no-policy game. The standing rule (CLAUDE.md:51) still requires the simulation bar's dump diff to prove that.

Checked and correct:
- **No-policy inertness (CONFIRMED by tracing).** In the no-policy game the income-tax lever never goes below its seed, because the AI only raises it (AiFinanceMinistry.cs:247, FinancePartner.cs:276). So the yield (`now`) is above zero everywhere.
  - With `now > 0` and a seed yield above zero, both functions compute the same double expressions as before. `Math.Max(0.0, now)` returns `now` bit for bit.
  - There is no float local and no second write to a local.
  - In the old code, `IncomeTaxSeedAer` was also read after `YieldNow`. The patch only swaps the order of two reads that have no side effects. `RateSeedOf`'s lazy write still happens at the same point.
  - A yield of -0.0 counts as zero (the new branch, as intended). A NaN yield gave NaN from EffectiveRate before and still does. YieldRatio on a NaN yield now answers 0 instead of 1, which is harmless.
- **Callers.** YieldRatio's only engine caller is TaxBases.cs:149. That covers the turn's revenue, both sides of the household-burden term, and the Budget estimates. A ratio of 0 means revenue 0, and nothing downstream divides by it.
- **Negative yields are reachable**, in two ways:
  - Lever 0 after a fall in real wages. A negative lever shift takes the band rates below zero, and nothing floors them (TaxSchedule.cs:253, :274).
  - Sub-rows zeroed with the lever below the seed.
  - Flooring the revenue at zero matches the dial's 0 minimum (TaxLine.cs:73) and the ruling. EffectiveRate floors the same way.
- **Readers of EffectiveRate compare it with a baseline:**
  - Labour supply compares with `LaborTaxRateSeed`, which equals `RateSeed` (Country.cs:267-268, taken after WorldFactory.cs:1080). At zero yield the gap is minus the seed's yield, as it should be.
  - ParliamentSystem.cs:187-188 and :497-499 are now continuous. Under the old code, at France's lever 0, a bill raising one band read as a hike of about (RateSeed − seed yield) points.
  - The Gini term reads against the stale `BaselineIncomeTaxRate` (GA-11-1). The two fixes are independent and can land in either order. After GA-11-1, the gap at zero yield is minus the seed's yield for all four countries.
- **The patch closes a wider route than the audit row records.** Zeroing every band through the sub-rows (clamped at 0 or above: ParliamentSystem.cs:175, GameController.cs:10002) gives exactly zero yield at the seeded lever for:
  - France and the USA (every band can be moved);
  - Sweden (a municipal rate of 0 means no credit, EarnedIncomeCredit.cs:98);
  - Poland (only the levy is left).

  The old code kept the whole revenue there (ratio 1) and read such a bill as neutral. This is worth adding to GA-2-2's row.
- **Sweden and Italy.** Sweden's lever path stays above zero (the credit, per the audit), so it is unchanged. Italy does not respond, so both functions return on their first line and it is untouched.
- **The memo** stores only `now`. The same cached value gives the same result before and after, except where the yield is at or below zero. Rates within 0.005 share a memo key; near zero that collision could flip the old answer between 1 and about 0, and now it cannot.
- **No test asserts the old behaviour.** TaxScheduleDiagnostic probes the seed and +5. LeverLivenessCheck uses +10. HouseholdBurdenAnchorDiagnostic and LaborTaxParticipationDiagnostic use ±5 and +20. BudgetDraftEstimateDiagnostic uses +1. The audit driver's X-02 run will now move France's deficit; that is the fix's evidence.
- **File hygiene.** No new backticked names, and `Country.IncomeTaxSeedAer` resolves, so CommentClaimCheck is unaffected. The file is CRLF throughout with no BOM, and only the intended hunks differ.

Findings:

1. **MEDIUM (older defect, not caused by the patch, more visible after it) — CONFIRMED — MacroSystem.cs:159-164 with TaxBases.cs:147-149.**
   - The household burden does not read EffectiveRate, although the brief and the summary at patched :593 and TaxBases.cs:120 say it does.
   - It reads `RevenueAtRate` at `line.Rate` and at `BaselineTaxRates`. Both go through YieldRatio on the live line, with its current BracketRates.
   - At the seeded lever the two calls are identical, so any sub-row change leaves HouseholdBurdenGap at exactly 0.
   - Scenario: in Sweden, a bill sets the flat-layer and state-band sub-rows to 0. After the patch, income-tax revenue is 0 (correct), but consumption gets no relief from the household-burden term. Before the patch, the revenue was kept.
   - Every sub-row raise since F4-4 has had the same problem.
   - Fix, as its own audit row: the baseline side should read the seed's statute with no overrides, for example through a `RevenueAtRate` overload that takes the overrides.

2. **LOW — CONFIRMED in the code / PLAUSIBLE to reach — patched TaxSchedule.cs:588.**
   - The `<= 0f` test lets a NaN `IncomeTaxSeedAer` through, and YieldNow's own `<= 0f` guard (:556) never re-captures a NaN.
   - YieldRatio then returns now/NaN = NaN into the book. The old `IncomeTaxSeedAer > 0f` test returned 1.0, so the documented fallbacks do not quite stay as they were.
   - It can only happen with a NaN cohort sigma or count.
   - Fix: `if (!(country.IncomeTaxSeedAer > 0f)) { return 1.0; }`, and the same at :606 for symmetry.

3. **LOW — CONFIRMED — comments at patched :577-579, :593 and :598-600.**
   - (a) "A lever of 0 yields zero by construction" is true only at the seed's scales. YieldNow reads the boundary's income and threshold scales (:569), so the yield at lever 0 drifts with bracket creep and goes negative when real wages fall. It is never true for Sweden, whose credit is not linear in the shift.
   - (b) "Every other schedule's read the seed's rate less its yield" is false for Sweden. Name Germany, Poland and the USA instead.
   - (c) "ZERO where the statute yields nothing" should say "at or below zero".
   - (d) "France's lands … land a hair above it and lose theirs" is a present-tense derived claim that the next re-seed can make false. Past tense ("landed", "lost") keeps it a dated record.
   - (e) Line :593 and TaxBases.cs:120 name the household burden as a reader, which it is not (finding 1). TaxBases.cs:119-120 also describes the rate as "the seeded rate TIMES the schedule's yield ratio". That contradicts the points form ("never scaled") that the patch's continuity argument relies on; under TaxBases' wording, France's old reading of 0 would look correct. Fix both docs while the patch is open.

4. **LOW — CONFIRMED — PolicyWebRenderer.cs:1343.**
   - When the statute yields nothing, France's readout now prints "the effective rate" as RateSeed minus the seed yield, as Germany, Poland and the USA already did. That figure is above zero, because the seed is the rate on taxed income only.
   - It sits next to a yield from YieldNow that is printed without the floor, so it can read negative or "-0.0".
   - This is only a player-visible oddity, not a money-path problem. Fix: print the yield floored at zero, and call the figure the flat-equivalent rate.

## What was done

- **1 (MEDIUM, an older defect): filed, not built.** The household burden reads the income tax's revenue (`TaxBases.RevenueAtRate`) at the line's rate and at the seed's, both through the yield ratio on the live line with the sub-rows the bills have set, so a sub-row change never moves the burden's gap - confirmed in the code (`MacroSystem.HouseholdBurdenGap`). It predates this change (F4-4, 2026-09-13) and is not GA-2-2's row, so it is a row of its own: **GA-2-11** (S3, a clear bug), its fix the baseline side read without the line's sub-rows; put to Elias with the next questions. The two summaries that named the burden among the effective rate's readers are corrected (finding 3e).
- **2 (LOW): fixed.** Both fallbacks are written `!(country.IncomeTaxSeedAer > 0f)`, so a seed yield that is not a number takes the documented fallback as the old test did.
- **3 (LOW): all five fixed.** (a) the mechanism sentence holds at the seed's scales and says that the boundary's scales move a lever of 0 off zero and that Sweden's credit is not linear; (b) Germany, Poland and the USA are named where "every other schedule" stood; (c) "at or below zero"; (d) the past tense; (e) `TaxSchedule.EffectiveRate`'s summary names its readers (the Gini term, the labour-supply term, a bill's hike) and says the burden reads the revenue, and `TaxBases.EffectiveRate`'s summary - "the seeded rate times the schedule's yield ratio" - says points, never scaled, with the correction marked.
- **4 (LOW): carried to the UI items.** The readout's fix (the yield floored at zero, as the revenue reads it, and the figure beside it named the flat-equivalent rate) is written, but a UI change owes a scoped dry film and a filmed width, which Phase 4's queued UI rows (GA-10-1, GA-2-8, GA-2-7, GA-6-3, GA-3-6) owe in any case - it lands with them, and this item stays a SIMULATION one.
- **The wider route the review found** - every band zeroed through the sub-rows yields exactly zero at the seeded lever in France, the USA, Sweden and Poland, where the old answer kept the whole revenue - is closed by the same rule; it goes on GA-2-2's row when the row is closed.
- The changes after the report are comments and the two guards' form (finding 2, the review's own text); no second pass was run.
