## Findings, ranked

### M1. The nominees are swung on three-way priors, not two-party ones, and the card credits the effect to the ballots

`ElectoralCollege.cs:258` builds `nomineePrior[r] = { priorShares[r][D], priorShares[r][R] }`. Those are 2020 shares of all votes, so they sum to
1 − other2020[r], not 1. The target `{D/(D+R), R/(D+R)}` sums to 1. `RegionalSharesByUniformSwing` measures the swing against these unnormalised
priors, so the two nominees' swings carry a common add-on of about +0.9 pp each: 2020's whole "others" mass, handed out uniformly. `ApplySwing` then
renormalises each region. Adding the same amount to both nominees and renormalising pulls lopsided jurisdictions toward 50/50. This step is not
declared. The doc comment says "the same `RegionalSharesByUniformSwing` on the two of them", and "the two of them" reads as a two-party prior.

- **Measured** (perl, at the FEC's shares): the new path's margin moves against US-4's (a) column are DC +2.13 pp (the widest) and WY −0.49 pp. With two-party-normalised priors (D/(D+R)) the widest move is 0.58 pp (DC).
- So about 1.5 pp of the largest difference the diagnostic reports comes from the prior form, not from the ballots.
- `UsPresidentialElectionDiagnostic.cs:604` writes "the difference is the ballots'", and `:243` says "margins differ by what the ballots move". Both are wrong as worded.
- No call flips at the FEC's shares. The swing states move by 0.1 pp or less. Against the record's 2024 margins, the mean absolute error is 2.371 pp with three-way priors, 2.465 pp with two-party priors, and 2.464 pp on the old path.

**Fix:** either set `nomineePrior[r] = { D/(D+R), R/(D+R) }`, which is the literal "uniform swing on the two of them", or keep the form and DECLARE it: 2020's others go back to the nominees in equal parts, compressing landslides. Either way, reword the card so the reported widest difference is not credited wholly to the ballots.

### L1. Nothing bounds a jurisdiction's minor total, so a large pooled share can drive the nominees negative

`ElectoralCollege.cs:246` accumulates `othersAt[r]` with no ceiling. Line 266 computes `left = 1 − othersAt[r]`, and a negative `left` gives the
inner swing a negative region weight (line 257) and negative nominee shares.

- The old path cannot do this: `ApplySwing` floors at zero and renormalises.
- `othersAt` scales linearly with the pooled share. AK reaches 0.5 (others lead) at a pooled share of 21.8%, and 1.0 at 43.6%.
- It is unreachable today. The USA roster is REP and DEM, the poll's two parties sum to about 1, and the factors are about 0.95–1.02, so the pooled share stays near 1.9%.
- It becomes reachable if the field ever gains a roster third party, a poll that does not sum to 1, or a scaled minor base.

**Fix:** after the per-candidate loop, throw (or report) when any `othersAt[r] >= 1`, and assert `left > 0` before the inner swing. Mention the bound in the `Standing` doc.

### L2. Re-deriving a saved contest's states now depends on the build

`StatesOf` (`UsPresidentialElection.cs:418`) re-derives a stored 2024 contest through the new path. A 2024 contest counted and saved by a build
before US-6c will now re-derive different shares, and in a close game different calls, from the ones its stored electors came from. There is no
format step. Today the only consumer is the diagnostic, so this is latent. It will matter when US-26 (R-US11 (a): a later count swings from the
game's own states) reads `StatesOf`.

**Fix:** note it at `SaveGameService`'s format-39 comment and in `Country.cs:1019`, or have US-26 take the format step.

### L3. The perl guards are coarse: a partial misread of the fonts could pass them

`Tools/us_returns_prep.pl:158` reads italics correctly, as verified above. The guards behind it are only these:

1. no nominee's cell is italic;
2. every scattered write-in cell is italic;
3. the number of columns with at least one roman cell is 22 (24 − 2).

Suppose one italic xf were not recognised, for example a future re-save writing `<i val="true"/>` on one font (that form is handled) or `<i></i>`
(that form is not), and that xf were used only by named candidates' write-in cells. Those cells would read as ballot listings and every guard would
still pass. Two smaller gaps:

- Cells without `s` take xf 0, whose font is never checked.
- Italics carried by a shared string's rich-text runs are not read. No count cell uses them today.

**Fix:** fail when any count cell uses an xf whose font block holds `<i` but was not classified italic. Also pin a few known per-column listing
counts (Stein 37, Kennedy 31, Oliver 47), as reading 15 already prints, in the mutation suite or as typed constants.

### L4. Three existing crefs now point at an overload group

`UsPresidentialElection.cs:22`, `UsPresidentialElection.cs:291` and `UsStateSwingCheck.cs:23` have `<see cref="ElectoralCollege.FromNationalShares"/>`,
which now names two overloads. A documentation build would warn CS0419. Unity builds no XML docs, so nothing breaks.

**Fix:** qualify each cref with its parameter list, as `OnTheBallots`' doc already does.

### L5. Exception text follows the machine's culture

`ElectoralCollege.cs:249` interpolates `{pooled:R}` and `{national[ShareOthers]:R}` in the current culture, so on sv-SE it prints `0,0188…`. The message
is diagnostic-only, so this is a nit.

**Fix:** use `string.Format(CultureInfo.InvariantCulture, …)`, as the rest of the file does.

### L6. The count can throw on polling day

`OnTheBallots` throws (`ElectoralCollege.cs:245`, `:249`) when a column stands nowhere, and nothing catches it on the way up through
`Count` → `Hold` → the day loop. `GeneratedCatalogCheck` rules this out for the shipped catalog, because each column's rows sum to its national row.
The 3-argument path already throws on a catalog mismatch, so this follows an existing pattern. Recorded for completeness.

## Not findings: checked and dropped

- **Weights.** Minor columns spread on 2020's votes cast. The nominees are weighted by `W·(1 − othersAt)`, and Σ W(1 − o)/T = 1 − O = D + R, so the nominees' national shares close exactly. The 3-column residual is reported.
- **ME/NE.** The district swing reads `stateNext = D/(D+R)` from the derived shares, which equals `two[r][0]`, so it is consistent. Each district's own minor vote is not modelled, unchanged from before.
- **Jurisdiction codes.** All 51 codes in `CandidateStates` exist in both 2020 and 2024 `States`. There are no zero-vote cells.
- **Diagnostic (b)'s 1e-12 recompute.** It mirrors the implementation term for term, so it is a spec restatement, not an independent model, but it holds. `GeneratedCatalogCheck`'s row sums (each column to its national row, each jurisdiction to its others) are a strong guard on the table.
- **The note's number.** `/for the (\d+)\s*$/` matches the note's first cell ("…for the 24 ").
- **Output order.** The sort uses `@order`, defined at `us_returns_prep.pl:296`.
