## What I checked and found sound

- **K159 date.** 25 Mar 2025 + 46 months = Thursday 25 Jan 2029, so the first Sunday is 28 Jan 2029 (1 Jan 2029 is a Monday). The game's next term: 28 Jan + 30 days = 27 Feb 2029; + 46 months = Monday 27 Dec 2032, so 2 Jan 2033. 28 Jan 2029 + 46 months = 28 Nov 2032, a Sunday, so it is its own first Sunday. All of these are right, and `PollingDayDiagnostic` asserts them as hard-coded dates, not as values the code computes. The Art. 39 Abs. 1 and Abs. 2 quotes match `gg_art_39.html` word for word. `[GG-39]` and `[BT-K21]` are registered in `germany/records_by_date.md`. The `PollingDayDiagnostic` clause change is justified: it was inverted to match K159 and still pins the snap day and `[BWL-WT25]`.
- **First sittings.** Sweden's 15th day (RF 3:10), Germany's 30th (Art. 39 Abs. 2, its last day, DECLARED), Poland's 30th (Art. 109 ust. 2) and Italy's 20th (art. 61) are each right, and a polling day of record uses the record's own convening.
- **Sweden's extra election** does not move the ordinary one, because the ordinary day is a fixed cycle. Italy is re-anchored on the AI country's own last count (`TryNextOrdinaryDay`).
- **RNG.** The chamber count draws from `new System.Random(RegionalElectionNoiseSeedOn(country, day))`, keyed. The US counts use `ElectionNoiseOn(country, date)` and `TieSeedOf(MasterSeed, year)`. The formation code (`Formateur`, `SupportAgreement`, `ItalianFormation`, `SejmFormation`) and `PresidentialElection` contain no `SimulationRandom` draw. The only process-wide readout is `NationalElection.LastRegional*`: five fields, and `KeepRegionalReadout` restores all five. Every AI count is wrapped in it.
- **Installation.** `country.Government = GovernmentRecord.FromProjection(...)`, and `Discharge` raises `Version`. `ChamberVerdictCacheCheck`'s `DirectWrite` pattern is not tripped: `PmParty` is set in an object initialiser inside `GovernmentRecord`, and `Breaks.Add` is not a guarded member. `ChamberVerdicts` keys on reference plus version.
- **FinancePartner.** This is not a double write. `FinancePartnerRuns` already covered every AI country, and AtStart already allocated portfolios. A newly formed AI government changes `FormedOn`, so the stance restarts for the new holder, which is the designed "a new holder starts from zero". A procedure-formed government is `Provisional = false`, so a partner now acts where a provisional stand-in had none. That is consistent with §716's decision A.
- **Save 67.** The loader refuses any version other than 67 (`SaveGameService.cs:230`, `:421`). Everything the calendar reads is serialised: `ElectionHistory`, `Government` with `Caretaker`/`CaretakerSince`/`FormedOn` (only `Version` is `JsonIgnore`), chambers and contests. The load path re-seeds no AI government. `_calendarCounts` and `_calendarFormations` are read only by the diagnostic.
- **The player's own country** is skipped by `HoldWorldCalendar` (`PlayerCountryId` is read daily, so it follows a load) and excluded by `RecentAbroad`. A world with no player counts every country, consistently with its forks under LEDGER-1's `seatAsPlayed`.
- **The Docket item** passes `holdsTime: false`, and the `"globe"` icon exists. It does not set `anyPending`, which matches the K38 information items. The UI calls only `World` and `CurrentDate` on the manager, so `UiLeverVerbCheck` is untouched. There is no `{0}'s` in the diff. Line endings in the stored blobs match the base (LF in the blob, CRLF in the work tree under `core.autocrlf=true`).

## High

**H1 - An AI Germany whose first chancellor ballot fails stays a caretaker for the whole term, though the model runs Art. 63 Abs. 3-4.** The failure is in `SimulationManager.WorldCalendar.cs:128` (Bundestag goes to `ProjectRound`, then `FirstBallot`) together with `:134-147`, and in the declaration at `WorldCalendar.cs:22-24` ("Art. 63 Abs. 4 and Art. 155 are not modelled").

- Germany's projection stops at the first ballot, and the projection says so itself (`FormationProjection.Partial`).
- So any failure of phase 1 (no draft wins a majority of the members) reaches `outgoing.Discharge`. With `TryExtraElection` returning false for Bundestag rules, the outgoing cabinet then governs as a discharged caretaker until the next K159 day, four years away.
- The player's Bundestag path does model the later phases: phase 2 at `SimulationManager.cs:5145`, Abs. 4's plurality ballot at `:5007-5008` and `:5255-5310`, phase 3 at `:5279`. So the declaration is factually wrong for Germany, and the AI country is not "formed by its own procedure" (K161; §618's ruling 4).
- **Failure scenario:** the 2029 AI count returns a Bundestag where no coalition that excludes AfD holds 316 seats. The first ballot fails, and Germany is governed by the 2025 cabinet as caretaker until 2033. Its finance partner, agreements and the Docket card ("governs on as caretaker - no government was formed") all follow from that, where the model's own phase 3 would have elected a chancellor.
- **Fix:** run the AI Bundestag through phases 2-3 (the player's round code, answered by the AI's rule), or make it a K-line Elias can strike. As written, the declaration misstates the model.

(Poland's Art. 155 is genuinely unmodelled in the player's Poland too, at `SejmFormation.cs:49-50` and `SimulationManager.Sejm.cs:121`, so the Sejm half of the declaration is consistent.)
