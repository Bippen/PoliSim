## Findings (ranked)

### M1 — The new diagnostic is not registered in any bar, though its doc says it is
- Where: `Assets/Editor/HistoryRolledForwardDiagnostic.cs:34` ("Registered in the cheap bar; it writes nothing."). `Assets/Editor/CheckSuite.cs` has no entry for it. Its siblings are registered at `:297` / `:301` (and `:788` / `:790`). Nothing else in the repo or `Tools/` names it, and CheckSuite has no auto-discovery.
- Failure: the US-26 done-when ("a diagnostic shows ... ; bars") is met only by a run made by hand. The inertness proof (a), the second-election reads (b), the US 2026 poll (c) and R-US24 (b)'s chain (d) never run in the cheap bar. A later change that breaks the chain or the inertness, such as a caller that starts passing `played` with a date-less `before`, would pass every bar.
- Fix: add `("HistoryRolledForwardDiagnostic", HistoryRolledForwardDiagnostic.Run)` to the cheap-bar list in `CheckSuite.cs`, with a §-comment like its neighbours. If it is meant to run elsewhere, correct the line-34 claim.

### M2 (low reach) — `StatesOf(Country, Contest)` re-derives every later contest on the game's states, whatever form that contest was actually counted on
- Where: `UsPresidentialElection.cs:557-562` (`StatesOf(Country, Contest)` always takes the game form when a previous contest exists), `:577-584` (`CollegeOf`), `:587-598` (`StatesOn`). No save-format step anywhere in the diff.
- Failure: a save from a pre-US-26 build that holds a game count of 7 Nov 2028 was counted on the record's 2024 states (its `DayBasis` says "the states from 2024's ..."). After this build, `StatesOf(usa, c2028)` re-derives it with `FromGameStates` on the game's 2024 states. The states returned are not the ones the stored contest's elector tallies were counted on, so a close state can be called differently from the stored EV totals. The 2032 count then swings from those states (`Hold` at `:652-654`), and every later re-derivation inherits them. The "re-derived exactly from the stored contest" invariant (R-US24 (b)'s own wording, and what the Senate count relies on) silently fails for that save. US-6c faced the same situation and DECLARED it (`OthersOnTheBallots` doc, `:606-609`). Nothing here declares it.
- The same weakness shows when contests are inserted out of order. `PreviousCount` takes whichever contest is latest before the day at re-derivation time, not at count time. A contest added later with an earlier date (a planted night in a diagnostic, or a merged list) changes how every later contest re-derives.
- Fix (cheap, and it makes exactness hold by construction): in `StatesOn`, pick the form from the stored contest. Use the game form only when `contest.DayBasis` carries the game-form phrase ("the states from the game's own count of") and that date's contest exists. Otherwise use the record form. At minimum, add a DECLARED sentence to `StatesOf(Country, Contest)` and to the card, in US-6c's words.

### L1 — `OwnCounts` relies on list order and lets MinValue-dated records past the day gate
- Where: `PartySystem.cs:649-656` (ElectionHistory loop) and `:638-645` (UsCongressElections loop). Both take insertion order as "oldest first". `:652` lets `e.Date == DateTime.MinValue` through whatever `before` is.
- Failure: `TryHistory` takes `sequence[^1]` / `sequence[^2]` as the pair. A list that is not chronological gives the wrong prior and loyalty without any error. That can happen with diagnostics that add and remove planted US contests (`CongressElectionDiagnostic` (f), `HistoryRolledForwardDiagnostic` (d)), with a merged or repaired save, or with a MinValue record mixed with dated ones. A MinValue record (the Editor's turn-labelled runs, per `ElectionRecord.Date`'s doc) is also read by the election it belongs to, so the "strictly before the day" rule does not hold there. The US House prior elsewhere is order-independent (`UsCongressElection.Count:478` takes the max by date), so the two readers can disagree.
- Fix: sort `counts` by `Day` with a stable sort (break ties by list index). Treat a MinValue date as "not before" whenever `before` is given, or order MinValue records by `Turn`.

### L2 — A pre-build save loaded mid-way through a second campaign replays a different campaign
- Where: `SimulationManager.cs:5781` and `:5801` (`RestoreCampaign` re-stages with `played:`), together with `SaveGameService.RestoreInto:304-308` (the world, so ElectionHistory, is restored before the replay).
- Failure: a Swedish save cut by a pre-US-26 build during the 2030 campaign was stepped on the record pair. On load the replay is staged on the rolled pair (the 2026 game count as prior), so the replayed polls and AI decisions are not the ones the player saw. `AssertReplayLandedOn` may log a stream mismatch, and the campaign is "still restored" with a jump. There is no SaveVersion step.
- Fix: declare it next to the other "a save from before ..." notes, or have the replay stage on the record pair when the save's version predates US-26.

### L3 — `StatesOf(Contest)` (one argument) is still public and reads as the general re-derivation

## Integration notes (not defects of this branch)

- **FORK-2:** forks built by `ShadowBaseline` deserialize the save, so `played` in a fork is the fork's own Country, with its own ElectionHistory and UsCongressElections. That is correct. Two hazards for FORK-2 to watch:
  - The preview clone (`SimulationManager.cs:6927-6929`) shares `UsPresidentialElections` and `UsCongressElections` by reference and carries no `ElectionHistory`. A fork built on the clone that holds an election would write into the live lists. Its Sweden, Germany or Poland second election would also read zero own counts.
  - FORK-2's own election path must pass `played: <fork's country>` to `TryPredictShares` / `TryFor`. Otherwise a fork's second election reads the record pair while the live game reads its own counts, and the two diverge for a reason other than policy.
- **Trajectories that move by design:** an audit run (GameController-driven) through a second election moves from that election onward. That covers Sweden 2030, Poland's 2025 first round from the 2023 start, and the US House poll and states from 2028. The batch dump and `elec<N>` hold no player election and do not move. `GateReRun`, `LoyaltyHarness` and every Editor caller still use the overloads without a country.
- **Germany:** its calendar offers no polling day after the snap, so the rolled German path is reachable in play only through an extra election.

## Checked and clean

- **Inertness:** with no own count, `TryHistory(id, played, before, ...)` returns `TryHistory(id, out, out, vintage)`, the very call made before. The vintage passed through is the same one: Sweden's resolved `vintage`, Germany's `seated`, and `Seated` for `TryCompatibility`. Labels and notes are unchanged (`PairLabels` returns early when ownCounts is 0). The US count with `previous == null` reaches `FromNationalShares` with the same arguments, and the record-branch basis format string is unchanged character for character. `StatesOf(Contest)` and the Congress count's states for a first count are the same call. No RNG is drawn on any new path, and no new static is written.
- **Vintage with own counts:** the epoch pair followed by the game's counts is right for every start. Germany's snap gives 2017, 2021, then the game's 2025. Sweden gives the epoch pair, then the game's 2026. Poland's 2023 start gives 2015, 2019, then the game's 2023 (§767's gap closed). The USA gives 2020, 2022, then the game's 2024 House. Poland's presidential start (epoch 2025) has no own counts before its 2025 round. No caller pins an explicit vintage and passes `played` together.
- **Scale:** `ElectionRecord.Shares` are roster fractions summing to 1 (`Allocate:460-467`; Poland's `Run:412`). A US count is (V, 1−V). Both are scaled by the sum of the record's latest, so latest, previous and the record pair sit on one scale. `LoyaltyModel.PartyLoyalty` is scale-free, and the prior is normalised downstream. The off-roster mass is held constant and not double-counted.
- **Created parties:** `For` and the padded `TryHistory` have equal length. A Splinter's slice keeps the sum. Parties are created only at the selector (`GameController.PartyCreation.cs:32-47`), so every own count carries them. Poland's SLD and PSL zero columns map by key.
- **Date gate:** the count of the day is added after the prediction (`GameController.cs:6657-6673`) and excluded by `>=`. Poland's presidential rounds and the US House choice are not in ElectionHistory. An extra election inside a run-up re-begins the run-up (§636/§640), so a staging never misses a count before its own polling day.
