## Medium

**M1. The count drops list seats when a coalition's lett. g-seated lists all have zero votes in a circoscrizione (region) where the coalition holds a seat. The floor then exceeds what the leader ends with. I built votes where certain exceeds the end by up to 6 seats.**

This is a pre-existing PS-7 defect: the same code is on main at 86596a07. But it falsifies proof step (4), the comment's "one exit", (e)'s universal claim and the lane's "a floor, however the votes still out are cast".

How it happens:
- `SwungVotes` floors a falling list at exactly zero (`ItalianCount.cs:110`). Lega at 11-13 % (2018: 17.4 %) is zero in Campania, Calabria, Sicilia and others.
- Lett. h (`ItalianCount.cs:413-430`) shares the coalition's seats on its figure, which includes its 1-3 % members. So the coalition wins seats in circoscrizioni where only those members have votes.
- Lett. i then seats only the lists admitted by lett. g (`:440`). There, `Distribute` finds q = 0 (`:487`), and the decimals pass breaks with `best < 0` (`:518`).
- The coalition's seats there are given to no one. The short list cannot be compensated, because no unit is in excess.
- The Senato has the same hole in lett. b. When no admitted member has votes in the region, `FlooredHare` over `mv` returns all zeros (`ItalianCount.cs:639-640`, `Rosatellum.cs:179`).

Scenario: run `ItalianNight.At` with roster 1 on these shares: Lega 11.56, FdI 2.69, FI 2.96, NM 1.72, PD 4.75, AVS 2.15, +E 2.41, IC 2.80, M5S 18.29, Az-IV 16.73, ScN 16.73, NEW 17.20 (%).
- CDX leads (18.9 %) with Lega its only seated list. Lett. h gives CDX 46 seats, and lett. i seats 40 (5 circoscrizioni where Lega has 0 votes). CSX also loses 1.
- The Camera seats 238 of 245 list seats, so Italy has 385 seats against 392.
- From Camera count 141 to 146, CDX's certain seats exceed its end, peaking at 103 certain against an end of 97.
- A targeted search (CDX = Lega 8-16 % + FdI/FI just under 3 %, everyone else seated) gave 325 overshooting counts out of 3,477 votes. The largest loss was 7 seats, the largest overshoot 6.

What it does not do:
- **No wrong call.** The family needs a leader whose only seated list has fallen below its 2018 share, so the leader cannot be near a majority. Among overshooting votes, the largest leader end, counting every abroad seat, was 124 against a line of 201.
- **Not reachable on realistic votes:** FdI and FI under 3 % with Lega holding.
- **Never on the diagnostic's thirteen votes:** zero losses there.

Fix, either:
- **Fix the count:** in lett. i and in the Senato's lett. b, when no seated list has a figure where the unit holds a seat, give it to the seated list with the largest national figure, or route it through the last resort. Then re-pin anything the count's seats feed.
- **Or state it as a second exit** in `ItalianNight.cs:312-315` and in (e).

Either way:
