## Findings (ranked)

### M1 — The book is an absolute snapshot taken on the tabling day, fund terms included, and is applied whole up to ~33 days later
- Where: `SimulationManager.MajorityReconciliation.cs:55-58` (book built once at tabling), `:100` (`ApplyBillResult(country, bill, ..., ApplyBudgetBillSpendingAndSwf, ...)`); `UsMajorityReconciliation.cs:128` (`AiFinanceMinistry.AsBudgetBill` copies `SwfShouldExist` and every fund term as they stand that day); `SimulationManager.cs:2258-2279` (`ApplyBudgetBillSpendingAndSwf`: `!bill.SwfShouldExist` dissolves the fund).
- Failure scenarios:
  1. **Fund dissolved by a rates bill.** The USA has no fund on the tabling day (`SwfShouldExist = false`). Within the 21-day course plus up to 10 desk days, another law creates one (e.g. the president's budget reconciliation, which carries the fund rules — `UsBudgetProcedure.PartOf` / `Changes` include `ChangesFund`). When the majority's reconciliation becomes law (AI signature, an override, or the player's signature), `ApplyBudgetBillSpendingAndSwf` sees `SwfShouldExist == false` and dissolves the new fund, crediting its assets to debt. The same path overwrites any fund-term change enacted in between with the stale terms. That breaks the class doc's own claim that the book carries only "the rates" and "the funding of the government is not its business".
  2. **Rates reverted and the step bound broken.** The president's budget reconciliation (or any rate law) moves income tax from 30 to 35 after tabling. The majority's bill still carries its tabling-day 29.8 and moves the line 5.2 points at once, far beyond `FinancePartner.StepPointsPerYear`, quietly undoing a law passed in the meantime. The vote's concern is read at vote time (`RatesOf` against the standing law), so the vote itself is honest. The bill's content is not: it is no longer "the ministry's yearly step toward the party's rate".
- Reachability: low. With a hostile Congress, the president's reconciliation seldom passes, and US fund creation goes through that act. But this slot is the first one that runs alongside the president's budget in the same Feb–Sep window, so the collision is new to US-23.
- Fix:
  - (a) Apply rates only. Pass a no-op delegate instead of `ApplyBudgetBillSpendingAndSwf`, or rewrite the bill's fund fields from `country.SovereignWealthFund` just before applying.
  - (b) Rebuild the book on the vote day: call `UsMajorityReconciliation.Book(country, bill.TabledBy, ...)` against the standing law when `DaysRemaining` reaches 0, and let the bill fall if it is now null. Alternatively, store the move as per-line deltas.
  - A diagnostic step should plant a rate or fund law between tabling and vote.

### L1 — Two surfaces say nothing is before Congress while the majority's reconciliation is

### L3 — The spatial term's weight comes from the net redistribution move, so offsetting rate moves silence it
- Where: `StanceModel.cs:342` replaces term 1 on `Redistribution` with `SpatialBudgetVote.Alignment`. The axis weight in `BillConcern.Loaded()` is still `|net move| / total` from `GetBudgetBillConcern`.
- Scenario: a US budget raises income tax by about the same points as it cuts another rate. The net redistribution move is about 0, so its weight is about 0, and q16's spatial reading (which may be strongly toward or away from a party) counts for nothing.
- Reach is small: the US has no VAT, and the majority's own book moves its lines the same way.
- Fix: weight the spatial axis by the spatial move (`sqrt(move)`), or state the limitation in `SpatialBudgetVote`'s "Its reach".

## Integrator's addendum (2026-10-20, s889): the fix round's money-path edits, read from the diff

The fix round (5dee7baa, rebased as f175b657) answered M1 by storing the majority's moves on the bill and applying them from the law of the day it becomes law. Two money-path pieces were read from the staged diff before the rows were added:

- `Assets/Scripts/Data/BudgetBill.cs`: one additive field, `RateMoves` (`Dictionary<TaxType, float>`), null on every other bill, riding the save with the pending slot. No other bill reads it.
- `UsMajorityReconciliation.Applied` (`Assets/Scripts/Simulation/UsMajorityReconciliation.cs`): builds a rates-only `BudgetBill` (no fund named, no spending line). For each stored move it takes the line's rate on the day of law plus the move, never past the tabling party's own rate (`TryPartyRate`, both directions), clamped to the dial's `MinRate`/`MaxRate`, and skips moves under `MinPoints`. A rate law passed while the bill waited is therefore kept, and the step is never larger than the move voted. The no-op spending/fund delegate leaves every spending line and fund term as they stand.

Nothing here can reach a no-player world: the slot is filled only where a game-elected player-president faces an out-party Congress.
