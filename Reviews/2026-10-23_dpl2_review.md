## What was verified (the questions asked)

**1. `SwungVotes`.** The probe ran 2023's base S = 20,813,086 against okreg 21's size of 464,109 (S/size = 44.845).
- Bit identity: with every share at 2023's, `Allocate` returns `KO 157 · Konf 18 · MN 0 · NL 26 · PiS 194 · TD 65`, with every okreg's division equal to the base code's. `DistrictVotes` is bit-identical to the base expressions, compared as raw double bits. I also ran 2,000 random draws with MN held at 2023's and the other lists moved (a third of them with a created list). They gave 0 bit differences and 0 seat differences against the base code. MN set one ulp off 2023 still matches. The reason is that `change` is exactly 0, so `carry` is 0 and `part` is +0.0, and `x + 0.0` is exact. That is the IL-shape rule the head names. The record's shares (`Record2023Shares`, which divides a double sum of longs) and `national2023` (which divides by a long) are the same doubles, so the record pins hold.
- Invariants (MN at 0, 2023's, 0.2 %, 0.5 %, 1 %, 1.5 %, 1.75 %, 1.9 %, others scaled to sum one): every okreg sums to its 2023 size and every list's total equals share × base, both to rounding. MN has no vote outside okreg 21 at any share. Algebraically, Σ_d carry_d·size_d = 0, so every list's unfloored national total is exactly n·S whether or not the shares sum to one. Okreg sums equal size × Σ shares.
- The floor: NL is the first to run out, because its okreg-21 share relative to its national share is the lowest (0.839). At MN 2.0 % it is zeroed and okreg 21 overshoots by 158.1 votes. That confirms the implementer's figure, and it is declared. See L1 for what happens above that.
- Shares not summing to one (MN 1 % unscaled, sum 1.0088; everything × 0.97): MN still stands in okreg 21 alone, and okreg sums scale with the sum, as the base code's did. A created list (NEW at 6 % with MN at 1 %) gives clean sums and NEW 6 seats. With NEW at 0.2 % and MN at 2.5 %, NEW is floored in okreg 21 together with the others.
- MN absent from the keys gives MN no votes. Okreg 21 is then short MN's 2023 votes (25,203). This is declared, and it is the base code's behaviour too (L2).

**2. The night.** Over 600 sweeps (MN up to 3 %, every fifth with a created list at 4-8 %), the night at 41 of 41 equals the chamber in every key's seats. Across 156,240 key-instants (every declaration of every sweep), no IN call has the national share under its line and no OUT call has it at or above. The residue argument does **not** need a uniform per-okreg share. Take unfloored u and floored f = max(0, u). Then Residue = Σ(f - u), and N·B - (running - Residue) = Σ_undeclared f ≥ 0, so IN is sound. For OUT, N·B ≤ toward·B + outstanding, so OUT is sound. Both rest only on Σ_d u_d = N·B per list, which `SwungVotes` keeps for every list, MN and okreg 21 included. `StandsDeclared` for a sole committee now waits for its okreg alone, which is right.

**3. The sentinel cannot move.** `NationalElection.Run`'s Polish case is reached in play only through `SimulationManager.RunPlayerElection`. That is gated by `PollingDayToday`, which needs `PlayerCountryId` (`TryPlayerPollingDay`). `TrajectoryBaselineDump` never sets a player. The presidential path (`HoldRounds`) does not touch the Sejm spread. Diagnostics that count Poland on the model's own MN share (`PolishDeclarationsDiagnostic`'s played count, `PollingDayDiagnostic`) assert only the 460 seats and KO+TD+NL forming. MN competes only for okreg 21's 12 seats, so the risk is small. The simulation bar still has to confirm it. `FormationSweep` reads the seated chamber, not `Allocate`.

**4. The Docket item.** It is Poland's only and is drawn exactly while `RunOffPending() && FirstVote <= today < RunOffOn`. A contest joins the list only once its first vote is held, so the first-vote day shows it only after that night. The run-off day shows nothing. It is request-then-act: the draw only stamps `_presidentBetweenAsked`, and `Update` opens the night before the clock gates (`GameController.cs:737`), with no Canvas built in OnGUI. A request survives another takeover and opens when that night is dismissed the same day. It is dropped across a day, refused while a verdict waits, and cleared on load (`GameController.cs:1138`). The night carries no verdict (`ApplyElectionVerdict` clears nothing that matters), saves nothing, and holds no time (`holdsTime: false`). A fork world never reads controller state. The batch audits never click it, so it cannot stall them.

**6. Project rules.** No `§`, no backtick, no register id and no runtime possessive in the new player strings ("ART. 127" is a statute, not a register). There is no new numeric constant. `PresidentBetweenKey` is a key. The only new public static, `SoleOkreg`, is called from the night and the screen. `SwungVotes` is internal. No new `<see cref` line was added. The chip stays at `Px(14f)`.

## Findings

### H1 - the film films the Desk, not the Docket, and never reaches either e7q frame
`Assets/Scripts/Testing/UiScreenshotDriver.cs:5609-5626`, with `:5656`. `CapturePolishInterrupts` sets `_onDesk = true` at 5656, and nothing after it clears it. The new step sets only `_consolidatedTab = Decisions`. `GameController.cs:2781` draws the Desk whenever `_onDesk` is true, so `DrawDecisionsTab` (and with it `DrawPresidentBetweenItem`) never runs. The driver's own warning at 6662 names exactly this trap: "A tab set by field alone while `_onDesk` is true films the DESK". The failure goes like this. `e7q_docket_between_the_rounds` is captured as the Desk under the docket's name. Setting `_docketOpenItem = "president:between"` is inert, so `_presidentBetweenAsked` is never stamped. `_electionNight` stays null and the driver logs "raised NO night" and `_failed++`. The film fails, but `e7q_presidential_election_night_between` is never filmed. No `e7q_docket*` frame or un-suffixed `e7q_*_between` frame exists in `PoliSim-captures` (only the `real901*` `_staged` ones), so the film has not been run for this branch. A UI commit touching `ElectionNightScreen` owes a REAL film that reaches it (§578). Fix: `SetPrivateField(controller, "_onDesk", false)` before the docket frame, then restore it. Then run the real interrupts film at a filmed width.

### M1 - the docket frame asserts nothing, and the failure points at the wrong log
`UiScreenshotDriver.cs:5613-5625`. Even with H1 fixed, the docket frame is captured without checking that the item is on it. If `BetweenTheRounds()` returned null (no pending run-off on the day after, or a non-Polish controller), the film captures an empty docket under the item's name. The only failure would come afterwards, saying "the controller logs why (ELECTION: / CANVAS: lines)". The controller logs nothing on that path, because `DrawPresidentBetweenItem` returns silently. Assert the item first, for example that `_docketSlipBook.Anchors` holds `docket:president:between` after a Settle, or call `BetweenTheRounds` by reflection. On a miss, log the contest's state (`RunOffPending`, `RunOffOn`, today) and skip the OPEN.

### L1 - above about 2.23 % national, MN has more votes in okreg 21 than the okreg has voters, and no check covers the floor
`PolishSejmAllocation.cs:149` and the head at `:19-30`. MN's votes are n·S, all in okreg 21, with no cap. At 2.5 % that is 520,327 votes in an okreg of 464,109. Okreg 21's total is then 59,740 over its size, and PiS's national total is 31,761 over its share × base (it is floored at zero in 21). At 3 %, 624,393 votes against 464,109. Seats stay sane: MN takes 12 of 12 and the chamber stays at 460. The night stays sound and equal to the chamber (probe). The 158-vote error at 2 % is declared and acceptable. But the head's words ("as the committee's national votes near the okreg's size ... the committee taking the okreg's seats as it fills it") do not say that the votes can exceed the okreg. Whether a played MN share can reach 2 % depends on the vote model. State the regime in the head, or put a cap to Elias. Add one diagnostic case at MN ≥ 2 % that holds night = chamber and the calls' soundness where the floor binds. Today no sweep reaches it.

### L2 - MN absent from the shares and MN at 0 give different okreg-21 counts
`PolishSejmAllocation.cs:127`. With MN absent, `carry` stays 0, so okreg 21 loses MN's 2023 votes (5.4 % of the okreg) with nothing to replace them. With MN at 0, the other lists take them back. Seats agree in the probe, and the base code behaved the same way. The case is unreachable in play, because `NationalElection.Run` writes every roster key and the roster carries MN. It is informational only.

### L3 - a refused or stale request is silent
`GameController.PolishNight.cs:81-85`. When `_pendingElectionVerdict` is set, or the day has turned, the request is dropped with no log line. A player's OPEN then does nothing and no log line says why. Log one `ELECTION:` line on refusal, as the other night paths do. Separately, `DrawPresidentBetweenItem`'s bool return is unused at `GameController.DocketV35.cs:102`, so the docket can show "Nothing is waiting on you" under the item. That is consistent with the other can-wait items (minister alerts, the holdover), but the return value is dead.
