## Medium

**M1 - The 2024 count is not staged by the least margin, though the pass says it is, and the Congress is re-counted on the wider swing too.** `UiScreenshotDriver.cs:6606-6619`, against the summary at 6007 ("re-split ... by the least margin that elects"). The first re-split is `m = margin + 0.0025 * step` from step 0: the played margin turned round, then wider. A played REP +3.0 count is first staged as DEM +3.0, a 6-point swing, even where DEM +0.5 would already carry the College. `HoldUsPresidentialCount` then re-counts the House and the Senate class on the same shares (5879), so the staged Congress is more lopsided than the least staging would give. That decides which later frames happen at all - divided or unified government, whether `usj10` needs the seat swap (6481-6489), the first budget, the debt-limit episode and the 2026 midterm's base. The spec asks for each outcome staged "as `-shotformation` stages a formation", which is the least answer. *Failure scenario:* a REP film whose played count is close shows a DEM trifecta that a 1-point staging would never have produced, and the reader takes it for the staged outcome's consequence. *Fix:* search upward from the tie (`m = 0.0025 * (step + 1)`, stopping at the first step that elects `winner` outright, with the bound kept), or keep the search as it is and correct both summaries (6007 and 6577) to say "the played margin reversed, then wider".

**M2 - Against a standing withhold, the journey proposes a raise first. The warm-up's rule, and K89/K90, let the limit bind.** `UiScreenshotDriver.cs:6281-6306`. The `proposals > 0 && usa.DebtLimitWithheldBy != null` test lets the limit bind only after one proposal has been made. When the hold first comes with `DebtLimitWithheldBy` already set, the President still tables `Pl119IncreaseUsd`. The bill is voted at once (K43), the withholder carries it down, and `ResolveDebtLimitBillDay` charges the failed vote through `ApplyBillResult` and the approval ledger ("Debt limit raise failed"). The warm-up in the same driver (3725-3733) checks `aiWithholds` first and lets the limit bind with no proposal. That is the least answer, and it charges nothing. *Failure scenario:* a REP-president film meets a DEM-withheld limit. Its approval takes a failed-bill hit that play's least answer never takes, and the midterm's economic vote reads it. *Fix:* test the withhold before the first proposal, as the warm-up does: `if (usa.DebtLimitWithheldBy != null && usa.DebtLimitWithheldBy != usa.PlayerPartyAbbrev) LetDebtLimitBind(...) else { proposals++; Propose... }`.
