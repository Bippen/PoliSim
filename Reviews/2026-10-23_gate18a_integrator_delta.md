# GATE-18 piece one: the integrator's reading of the delta after the second review (2026-10-23)

**Why this exists.** `Reviews/2026-10-23_gate18a_review2.md` (independent, NOT READY on M1/M3, arithmetic sound) reviewed `wave/gate18a` at 134d51c7. Two commits followed it: the Low fixes (4f210afa) and Elias's two noise rulings of 2026-10-23 (4044eb7d: K34 the noise scaled by sqrt(s(1-s)); K35 the noise at every election in every country). The second touches a money path, `SimulationManager.cs`, so the recipe asks the integrator to read that delta before the ledger row is written.

**Files reviewed:** `Assets/Scripts/Simulation/SimulationManager.cs` (the delta cc5457e4..4044eb7d, lines ~5754-5770), `Assets/Scripts/Elections/UsGroundGame.cs` (`NoiseSigmaPp`, `NoCampaign`, the `Effects` call), `Assets/Scripts/Elections/RegionalAggregation.cs` (`ApplyScaledNoise`, `HalfSpread`), `Assets/Scripts/Elections/ElectionDay.cs` (the `scaledNoise` parameter), `Assets/Scripts/Elections/ParliamentaryCount.cs` (`SigmaPp`, the seam), `Assets/Editor/PresidentialReferenceWorld.cs` (the `NoiseAtZero` scope).

**What the delta does, read line by line:**
- `RegionalAggregation.ApplyScaledNoise` draws one Gaussian per party in key order, as `ApplyNoise` does, and multiplies it by sigma x sqrt(s(1-s)) / 0.5, with s clamped to [0, 1] for the root only. A share of zero draws a Gaussian but adds zero, so every later party's draw is the one it would have been; the draw count per region is unchanged, which keeps the keyed seed's sequence aligned across worlds. The clamp at zero and the renormalisation are the old ones. A total of zero returns the input. Correct for K34.
- `ElectionDay.Count` gains `scaledNoise` (default false): W-D1's own harnesses keep §27's flat form, play passes true. `UsGroundGame.Effects` passes true. Correct, and the default keeps every pre-existing caller bit-for-bit.
- `UsGroundGame.NoiseSigmaPp` now reads `ParliamentaryCount.SigmaPp`, so the one `NoiseAtZero` scope holds the US count at sigma 0 as well. `PresidentialReferenceWorld.Hold` (E1's reference world, fitted to the poll at sigma 0) now opens that scope: its four users must stay byte-identical, which the named run checks.
- `SimulationManager` (the money path): after the campaign branch and the prediction branch, a US polling day with no ground game is given `UsGroundGame.NoCampaign(CurrentDate)` - every jurisdiction with zero contacts, base turnout votes cast of record over CVAP - so the count draws the keyed scaled noise and nothing else (K35). The guard `ParliamentaryCount.SigmaPp > 0.0` leaves a sigma-0 measurement exactly as before. A counterfactual world logs nothing on the failure path; a played world says why. No money is booked on this path: it changes which states are called, hence the electors, the Senate's states and the next House base, never a ledger figure.

**Risks, and how each is answered:**
1. *The trajectory sentinel.* If an AI-run USA reaches `HoldUsPresidentialCount` in the no-policy dump, the noise now applies there and the sentinel moves. The implementer argues the dump seats no player so the US count is never held; the integrator does not take that on argument - the dump `dump903b` against `us24dl` decides it, and a move would make this a BASELINE family with its own review.
2. *Forks and the shadow world.* The seed is `RegionalElectionNoiseSeedOn(country, day)` with no counted stream (review2 §1, and 4f210afa's L7 case), and 4f210afa's L6 keys the order, so a fork counts the same draws.
3. *Expectations that move.* Every US diagnostic that reaches a presidential polling day without a campaign now carries noise; the named run lists them and each is read, not waved through.

**Verdict:** the delta implements K34 and K35 as ruled, with no ledger figure touched; READY subject to the named run, the dump and both bars on the merged tree.
