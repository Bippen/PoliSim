## H-B (HIGH, new): the L-B pre-close answer makes every following ledger world part from the played world when a Fed term ends on a turn's last day

**Where:** `ShadowBaseline.cs:200` together with `SimulationManager.cs:3906-3907` (`AnswerFedChairWaitAsTheAi`). The premise it contradicts is at `GameController.cs:772-790` and `:807-814`.

### The played world's order (traced)

1. `GameController.Update`'s loop calls `_simulationManager.AdvanceDay()`.
2. `AdvanceDay` calls `AppointCentralBankHeadsWhoseTermsEnded` last (`SimulationManager.cs:556`). On the term day T, the player appoints, so nothing is seated and the Vice Chair acts. It then returns `daysSinceEpoch % DaysPerTurn == 0` (`:558-559`).
3. If T is the turn's last day, `AdvanceTurn()` runs at once, in the same iteration (`GameController.cs:789-790`):
   - `_impactLedger.AdvanceTurn(...)`, so every ledger world's `ShadowBaseline.AdvanceTurn`;
   - then the real close, with the Vice Chair's lean in the rate.
4. Only after that does the loop re-check `UpdateFedChairSelectionState()` (`:809`) and hold.
5. The player picks after the close. `Pull` schedules the lever on day 0 of the next turn (`DaysIntoTurn` = 0).

### What a following fork now does in the same turn

Take the tariff family, touched earlier, or a family first touched this turn (forked at `PolicyImpactLedger.cs:236`). Its `AdvanceTurn` runs `DoScheduled(int.MaxValue)`, where the pick is not yet scheduled because it does not exist yet. Then `AnswerFedChairWaitAsTheAi`, at `ShadowBaseline.cs:200`:
- nothing is pending;
- the term has ended;
- `FedAwaitsPlayersPick` is true.

So the AI's keyed nominee is seated, dated T, and `AnsweredAlone = true`. The fork closes the turn with the AI chair's lean; the played world closes it with the Vice Chair's.

Next frame the player nominates X. `Schedule(0, lever)` applies it at once in the fork, since 0 <= `_daysRun`, and lodges X over the seated AI chair. Until `VoteOn` (T + 89) the fork reads a seated AI chair against the played world's acting Vice Chair. On `VoteOn` both seat X.

The turn-N close has already parted. Every downstream figure (inflation, output, the next rate) carries the gap for the rest of the game, in every family's line, as if the family's withheld dials caused it. K46's flag is also set on every following world, so none is ever read as the played world again.
