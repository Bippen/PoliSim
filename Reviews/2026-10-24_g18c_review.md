- **Only the player's election reaches it.** The only runtime caller of `NationalElection.Run` is `RunPlayerElectionNow` (PlayerElection.cs:206). It returns before that line when `PlayerCountryId` is null. `HoldPlayerElection` and `GameController` (:6749) are its only entries.
  - **A no-player world never reaches it**, so the no-policy dump cannot move. That still has to be measured (UNITY RUNS OWED).
  - **AI-run countries** hold no chamber count through this path.
  - **The shadow baseline and the impact ledger's forks** carry `PlayerCountryId`, so they run the same deterministic allocation (ShadowBaseline.cs:324). Both arms of every comparison are counted the same way.
- **The Sweden branch only.** `counted` and `regionRosterShare` are read inside `case CountryId.Sweden` and nowhere else. Germany's and Poland's branches are unchanged. Germany's count now also keeps `Regional`, which is inert: only the Sweden branch reads it.
- **Calls without a count are unchanged.** Every Editor caller passes no count and takes `Allocate` as before. `AllocateRiksdag` returns false, writing nothing, when the count has no regions, or when it has neither a decision nor an electorate.
- **The bases.**
  - 4 %: the national base is `round(total / RosterShareOfVote)` on the day of record and the counted total on any other day. This is §913's reading. On a normalised count it is the same base as `Allocate`'s `max(total, 1e8/ρ)`.
  - 12 %: each valkrets reads `round(sum_r / ρ_r)` on the day of record, where `ρ_r = RecordFactors.RosterShareByRegion` comes from the same returns. On any other day it reads `sum_r`. ρ outside (0, 1) is read as 1 (NationalElection.cs:696).
  - `all` sums every list the count carries, so a list that is counted but not on the roster stays in the base.
  - This is the per-valkrets form of §913, DECLARED in the summary.
- **Seats per list** are mapped by `RegionKeys.IndexOf(Abbrev)`. `RegionKeys` is the count's ordinal-sorted key list, and the votes are indexed in that order. Correct.
- **Party totals against the national path.** Whenever no list takes seats by the 12 % route alone and no return goes unfilled, the party totals are the national totalfördelning's. The only exception is integer rounding of valkrets votes in the millions against `share × 1e8`. The diagnostic's part 4 asserts this on a polling day four years after 2026.

## (4) q25: the polls' mean

- **`PollingSystem.Mean`** (Polling.cs:185):
  - The window is `(asOf - w, asOf]` (:195), which is exactly what the summary says.
  - Each share is the plain mean. The sample is pooled, and the margin of error is the pooled sample's at the mean share. A house's lean is declared as not averaged out, and every tracker comes from one house (`setup.PublicHouse`).
  - It returns null when no poll falls in the window or when two polls have different party counts.
- **`CampaignRun.State.PublishedPolls`:**
  - The only constructor is `Begin`, which always creates it with `new List<Poll>()` (CampaignRun.cs:545), so it is never null.

  - It is not saved. `RestoreCampaign` rebuilds it, because it re-steps every saved day through `Begin` and `StepDay`, which is the same path the original run took. Replay is deterministic.
- **The only reader** is `SharesFromCampaign` in RunPlayerElectionNow (PlayerElection.cs:143). The cadence is weekly (`LiveCampaignSetup.PublicPollEveryDays = 7`), so the 28-day window reads four trackers.

## Findings

### Medium

**M-1 - Gate18Diagnostic.cs:224: an asserted clause outside the preregistered q18 rules.** `Reviews/2026-10-24_g18b_prereg.md` registers (a)-(d) and Poland's run-off, and says the out-of-sample figures "are never a pass condition". The new `Check(held.Counted.Riksdag != null && ... Method == SwedenTwoTier, "q3: ...")` is not among them.
- **Failure scenario:** a regression in the Swedish wiring, or a count that carries no valkretsar (for example the fallback when `AllocateRiksdag` returns false), makes "the gate holds" fail on a clause nobody registered. The gate's verdict then no longer means what was preregistered.
- **Why it is Medium, not High:** the clause tests wiring, not a measurement, and `SwedishRiksdagAllocationDiagnostic` part 4 already asserts the same wiring on its own count.
- **Fix, before the named run, one of:**
  - make it a REPORTED line, as the per-valkrets lines beside it are;
  - commit an amendment to the prereg on main before the run, in the K112 amendment-1 form.

**M-2 - The §913 factors were fitted on the last-tracker belief. q25 changes the handed shares wherever the idle game stages a campaign.**
- **The chain:** `RecordFactors`' Swedish FIT lines were fitted on `held.BeforeFactors`, which is `SharesFromCampaign(..., PublicPoll)` whenever the idle game ran a campaign (Gate18Diagnostic.cs:160 prints which). From 538ac9e0 those shares are taken on the mean (PlayerElection.cs:143-144).
- **Failure scenario:** in Sweden 2026's idle game a list polls near 4 %, so the mean lends a different amount than the last tracker did. The stored factors then apply to different shares, and (c)'s in-sample "+0.00" becomes a drift. If the drift exceeds 1.5 points, (c) fails.
- **Rulings that apply:** K107 rules that refit drift is reported, not failed. The patch tool, however, assumes the FIT lines were fitted on today's path.
- **Owed:** run Gate18 and compare the FIT lines with the stored factors. If they moved, re-patch through `Tools/chamber_factor_patch.pl` and record the move. q3 cannot move the factors, because the fit is on shares and not seats.

### Low

**L-1 - SeatConversion.cs:173-207: the return ignores 14 kap. 4 b §'s order.**
- **The statute:** where seats are returned in two or more valkretsar, the receiving parties take them in order of the largest comparison number.
- **The code:** it returns and reallocates one seat at a time, starting from the over party's weakest seat. When a receiving party's cap binds, the seat can land differently.
- **Reach:** no return happens on either record, and none is planted with two valkretsar.

**L-2 - SeatConversion.cs:190: `if (worstRegion < 0) { break; }`.** This branch runs when an over party's remaining fixed seats all sit in valkretsar with fewer than three fixed seats.
- It leaves that party over its total, so the chamber exceeds 349.
- Because it `break`s the whole loop, any other over party is never processed either.
- This is practically unreachable for a ≥4 % party (Gotland is the only valkrets with fewer than three fixed seats), and the statute is silent on it.
- **Suggested:** `continue` to the next over party, and say which reading is taken.

**L-3 - No check asserts the live q25 call.** If PlayerElection.cs:143 regressed to `PlayerCampaign.PublicPoll`, every diagnostic would still pass. Gate18 only prints `BeliefPolls`, and TacticalBeliefDiagnostic calls `TacticalBelief` directly.
- **Suggested:** assert `held.BeliefPolls > 0 && held.Belief` equals `TacticalBelief(...)` wherever a campaign ran. SwedishRiksdagAllocation part 4's harness is a natural place.

**L-4 - SeatConversion.cs:271: the year rule for an extra election.**
- An extra election early in 2026 (before Valmyndigheten's decision of 2026-05-05) would read 2026's table, which had not been decided yet.
- An extra election in 2023-2025 reads 2022's, which is the natural reading.
- **Suggested:** compare against the decision's date rather than the year, or declare the reading.

**L-5 - NationalElection.cs:673-681: a silent fallback.** If a future catalog renames a valkrets, `DecidedFixedSeats` returns null and the count falls back to the 310th-part rule over `SwedishRegions.EligibleAt`, with no log line. Part 4 catches this for 2026's names. A one-line warning would catch it in play.

**L-6 - Tools/riksdag_seats_prep.pl:75,79: `@jsonDigests` is dead.**
- The tool hashes the 29 JSON files and never compares or prints the digests.
- Its header says the digests are in `SHA256SUMS.results.txt`, but it never checks that file.
- **Checked here instead:** 40 of 40 OK.
- **Suggested:** verify against the sheet, or drop the variable.

**L-7 - The per-valkrets seats live only on `ParliamentaryCount.Held` (per world, never saved).**
- Nothing in play reads `count.Riksdag`.
- The night's cartogram still sizes its tiles by the derived 2022-roll column (ElectionNightScreen.cs:630, K-1c Design's).
- So q3's per-valkrets seats change the chamber only through the 12 % route and the return. This is a scope reading of "into the live count" and should be declared.

**L-8 - TacticalBeliefDiagnostic.cs:95: `SimulationRandom.Seed(777)` re-seeds the process streams.** `SeatConversionHarness` already does this. In a named batch, a check that runs after it and reads the streams without seeding would see a different state. CheckSuite's isolation may already cover this; if so, ignore.

**L-9 - Registration owed.** `SwedishRiksdagAllocationDiagnostic` and `TacticalBeliefDiagnostic` are not in `CheckSuite.cs`; the integrator adds both lines.
