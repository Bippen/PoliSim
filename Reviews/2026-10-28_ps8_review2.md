## New findings

### Medium

**N1 - `-shotabroad` films a Swedish game whose 13 Sep 2026 Riksdag election was never held.** `UiScreenshotDriver.cs:3486-3494`.
- **What happens.** Sweden's start is the run-up before `LatestElectionDay(Sweden)` = 13 Sep 2026. The walk then runs:
  - `AdvanceDay`;
  - `AdvanceCountryDayTick`;
  - `AdvanceTurn` on the turn boundary.
- **What is missing.** That list never calls `HoldPlayerElection`, `HoldElectionWithoutTheNight` or `AnswerWaitsAsTheAi`. Contrast `AdvanceDays` at `:3843`, whose §619 comment states the rule: "walking past that day would leave a game past an election never held - a state play cannot produce". `WorldCalendarDiagnostic.Day` calls `HoldPlayerElection` too.
- **What the frames would show.** On 4 Nov 2026 the player's Sweden still has its 2022 Riksdag and government. No 28 Sep round has been held. The run-up and campaign state was never resolved. The arrival budget window has been open since day one, though play's clock holds on it.
- **Failure scenario.** `abroad01_docket` is meant to show the USA item among the Docket's real contents. Those contents are a state no game reaches:
  - no budget or election items, where play would have resolved them;
  - possibly the campaign HQ, which the controller's `Update` can open on one of the walk's yielded frames.
  - A reviewer or Design reading the film takes that Docket for the game's.
- The doc comment's "filmed from a played game - never staged" is therefore untrue.
- **Fix:** hold the player's election on its day, as `AdvanceDays` does (`HoldElectionWithoutTheNight`), answer the budget window as play's AI rule would, and log both.
- No later shot is affected, because the run ends at `EndSweep`.

**N2 - The H1 clause can pass without exercising H1.** `WorldCalendarDiagnostic.cs:181-185`.
- **The escape.** The check is `opened || first.Expected` and then `if (!opened) return;`. A plant whose first ballot elects prints "NOT EXERCISED" and passes.
- **Why the plant may elect.** The plant is AfD 300, CDU 120, CSU 40, Linke 170 (majority 316).
  - The CDU's and CSU's declared lines (`DeclaredRedLines.cs:368-374`) are lines against AfD and the Linke.
  - A line that is the AfD's own against the Union is what `Tally` needs to stop the AfD voting for the nearest nominee. If the AfD has no such line, its 300 votes plus the Union's 160 elect the Union's candidate at the first ballot.
  - Without running it I cannot tell which happens, and the clause is built so that it does not matter to the verdict.
- **The FromStart runs.** `roundsOpened` is empty unless an AI Bundestag happens to hang, so those runs do not cover it either.
- **Failure scenario.** A regression that breaks `ContinueChancellorElection` (for example a round left in `Consulting` with `Asked` null forever, or the discharge dropped) leaves the diagnostic green.
- **Fix:** make an unexercised plant a FAIL, and choose seats whose first ballot is verified to fail. For example, hand the AfD a seat count no other party's vote can carry, or check `first.Partial` before going on.
