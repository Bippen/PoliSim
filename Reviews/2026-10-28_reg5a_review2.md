**M2 (the lead's margin from one sample): ANSWERED.** `SwingRegions.LeadMarginOfErrorPp` (`SwingRegions.cs:63-69`) is 100 · `PollingSystem.ConfidenceZ` (1.96, `Polling.cs:86`) · √((s_L + s_R − (s_L − s_R)²)/n). That is the multinomial variance of the difference: p_L(1−p_L) + p_R(1−p_R) + 2p_Lp_R = p_L + p_R − (p_L − p_R)².
- It returns 0 for n ≤ 0 and guards v > 0.
- `FromPoll` (`SwingRegions.cs:50`) uses it at `poll.SampleSize`.
- Every reader of the lead's error goes through `FromPoll`: `RegionalPolling.Readings` (`RegionalCampaign.cs:241`), `StateOf`, the intelligence line (`RegionalCampaign.cs:268`), the map's `TooCloseToCall`/`GapErrorPp` (`GameController.CampaignMap.cs:178/335/363`) and the screenshot driver (`UiScreenshotDriver.cs:7295`). No other code combines two margins: a grep for a `Sqrt` over `MarginOfError` finds only the diagnostic's own "independent" comparison.
- An averaged poll carries its summed sample (`Polling.cs:199-209`), so the formula is consistent there too.
- The worked case by hand: 0.335 + 0.28 − 0.055² = 0.611975; /600 = 0.00101996; √ = 0.031937; × 196 = **6.26 pp**. The old sum: √(3.776² + 3.593²) = 5.21 pp. The lead of 5.5 now sits inside its error, so the region reads TOO CLOSE TO CALL. The diagnostic asserts exactly this at 1e-12 and at |x − 6.26| < 0.01.

**L1 (the regional bound): ANSWERED, and (b) is not weakened.** `BoundsPp` now goes to `RegionalBoundsPp` (`CampaignPressureRegional.cs:272-313`) when the tally is regional.
- Per region it rebuilds exactly the inputs `RegionEffects` reads: the same `RegionalSlot` and `RegionalAttack` spreading, the same skip of a non-standing party, and coverage over the region's audience at reach one. It adds, per class, upper × cover(all) × e/all for the party's own effect, and upper × cover(all) × ae_t/all to each target t where t stands. That matches `AttackEffectPp`'s `onBallot`.

### Low

**L-r1 - The L3 declaration says more than the code does.** `NationalElection.cs:486-488` says the excess "stays in the region it landed in ... rather than reaching the other regions through the swing's re-solve". The national shares handed to the count still carry the campaign's full, unclamped movement. After the clamp and rescale, the region's prior moves the clamped party by less than that. The swing re-solve (`RegionalVoteModel.cs:285`, one swing per party from the priors' weighted national) then spreads that party's unrealised loss over every region where it stands.
- **Scenario:** an attack in valkrets A worth 3 points against a party holding 1 point there. In A the party drops to 0, and the other 2 points (times A's weight) come off it in every other valkrets through the swing. The rescale also takes A's 2-point excess from every party in A in proportion, including the gainer.
- **Fix:** reword the note to say "the row sum is kept; the clamped party's unrealised movement still reaches the other regions through the re-solve". Or, if the excess should truly stay in A, scale the region's movement row down before applying it.

**L-r2 - The AI's regionless door and the run's pick the largest region by different audiences.** `CampaignAi.cs:930-940` ranks `view.Regions[r].Audience`. That is the office-scaled `CampaignOffices.LocalAudience(audience, influence)` built at `CampaignRun.cs:1213-1219`. `CampaignRun.DoorRegion` (`CampaignRun.cs:1345`) ranks the staging's raw `setup.Regions[r].Audience`.
- **Scenario:** a party with a strong office in the second-largest region. Its view's audience there can exceed the largest region's, so a regionless door would be valued in one region and tallied in another.
- **Impact:** today it is unreachable, because the AI's door candidates always carry a region. It becomes live if any valuation (a player-facing estimate through `ContactValuePp`) is ever made for a regionless door.
- **Fix:** rank by the same field. The view could carry the raw audience, or `DoorRegion` could take the view's.

**L-r3 - The screen still advertises the pollster-less sample.** `LiveCampaignSnapshot.cs:227` hands the map `RegionalPolling.Sample` as its sample. `OfferLine` (`LiveCampaignSnapshot.cs:252`) prints "600 INTERVIEWS ... ±4.0 PP".
- **Scenario:** with a pollster on the roster, the poll actually fielded has 900 interviews and a ±3.3 margin. The map and the offer still print 600 and ±4.0, so the screen understates what the player bought.
- **Fix:** read the newest filed poll's `SampleSize` for the map, and `staff.Improve(...)` for the offer. Or declare the offer as the base price list.

**L-r4 - L4's redistribution moves the US national columns off zero.** `Centre` (`UsGroundGame.cs:135-141`) makes each raw column zero-mean nationally. After `WithPattern` hands Others' movement to the nominees in states without another candidate, the effective Democratic and Republican columns are no longer zero-mean. Their national offset is Σ_{no-others} w_r·O_r·share. Before the fix the dropped Others movement broke the Others column instead, so this is no worse. It is small, and it is nonzero only where a third candidate campaigns.
- **Fix:** declare it beside L4's note. Or redistribute before centring.

**L-r5 - The diagnostic's expected sample reads the final roster, not day 5's.** `RegionalCampaignDiagnostic.cs:350` takes `state.Staff[party].Improve(...)` after the run ends. `Improve` depends on `Active(Pollster)`, which is "on the roster and paid today".
- **Scenario:** a pollster who is paid on day 5 and lapses later. The poll was fielded at 900, but the check expects 600 and fails on a correct run. The opposite case passes a wrong one.
- **Fix:** record the expected sample on the day it is bought (the filed `Poll.SampleSize` against `Improve` at day 5). Or assert that the Swedish script hires no pollster.

**L-r6 (pre-existing, informational) - Poland's count ignores the regional shift.** The okreg names now match (L8), but the Poland/Italy branch at `ParliamentaryCount.cs:153-172` never calls `ShiftOf`. A future Polish staging's regional pattern would be dropped silently, with `CampaignRegional` false. This is consistent with the declared "unused until a Polish staging"; record it on that item.
