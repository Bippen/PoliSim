### M1 - Ruling I4's own revisit clause has fired and US-27 neither surfaces it nor declares the overlap it governs

`docs/RULINGS_2026-10-18.md:22` (I4): "Revisit against (c) once approval reaches the vote (A-LOOP below)" (also `docs/reference/US_ELECTIONS.md:603`). Approval reached the vote on §879 (A-LOOP q15, 2026-10-19), before this branch: `EconomicVote.RecordPartsOverTerm` sums the economy, approval and fiscal terms into what every live count reads. `HoldUsMidtermCount` (`SimulationManager.cs:5771-5776`) predicts the midterm poll with `RecordOverTerm(country, CurrentDate)` and `CountMidterm` (`UsCongressElection.cs:607`) then adds the fitted mean on top. The fitted mean (-3.755 pp) is the record's UNCONDITIONAL average swing, so it already holds the record's average approval fall from oath to midterm. The approval term is `0.16 x (net approval now - at the oath) / 100` (`ApprovalVote.cs:70`, `:211`), so a 20-point net fall adds about 3.2 pp against the president's party - the same order as the term itself. The first review's M2 names only "the economy's part". The diff contains no word on approval or on the revisit (searched). **Scenario:** a US game whose AI president's net approval falls 20 points by 3 Nov 2026 loses roughly 7 pp of two-party House vote at the midterm (term plus approval, before the economic vote), about twice the record's mean, and nothing on the record says this was a choice. **Fix:** this is Elias's ruling, not Code's. Put I4's revisit to him with the integration, by name. Print the record's components on the midterm's CONGRESS line (the approval and economy shifts on the president's party that day), so the overlap can be measured on the first run. State the options: keep (b) as built; (b) with the record's approval term held at zero on a midterm; or (c). Change nothing until he rules.

### M2 - The live term reads a SECONDARY column that the project's own record defines out of "the record's vote swing"

The bytes are clean (see below). The source class is the problem. The spec defines the record's vote swing as the Clerk's, and says the 1946-1998 swings are "BILLED - never estimated, never typed from an image, never taken from a secondary compilation" (`docs/specs/USA_STAGE_PLAN.md:2271-2276`). Read narrowly they stay true, since the fit is not the rule. But the live count now runs on that column (`Tools/us_midterm_fit.pl` reads `vs_vote_change`/`vs_vote_direction`; `UsCongressElection.MidtermTermPoints`), and those comments no longer say so. "From the record since 1946" can only be met with the secondary source. That makes it an owner's call, which the implementer DECLARED PROVISIONAL and asked. That keeps this at M, not H. **Scenario:** a reader of the record or the CSV header concludes the SECONDARY figures move nothing in play, and they set every midterm's swing. **Fix:** keep it on the ask, and put the alternative beside it, already generated (`UsMidtermTerm.ClerkN`/`ClerkMeanPoints`: n 6, -3.98, [-7.85, -0.10]).
