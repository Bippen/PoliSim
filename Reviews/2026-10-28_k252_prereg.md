# K252 - the debt stock's effective rate seeded at the implied rate: registered before its first Unity run (2026-10-28)

**Status of the predictions, said first.** The figures below come from the implementer's out-of-engine runner, `Tools/headless_hold/hold.sh`, run on `wave/k252`'s working tree. That runner runs this branch's own `DebtPathHoldDiagnostic` under .NET with a patched copy of UnityEngine.CoreModule. The figures are therefore measured by a second runtime, not blind forecasts. On this branch's base (babea922) the same runner printed every one of §940's figures to the tenth. The Unity run is the confirmation. Each figure is held to plus or minus 0.5, the tolerance for .NET's floats against Mono's.

**What changes.** `Country.EffectiveDebtInterestRate` is seeded in `WorldFactory` at `Generated.DebtRateSeeds.ImpliedRatePercent` (`Tools/k252_debt_rate_prep.pl`, with `--check`). Before, ruling R4's sentinel opened it at the issuance rate. The seed is the national accounts' own figure: interest paid in 2025 over the gross debt at the end of 2024. For the EU five that is Eurostat gov_10dd_edpt1, S13, D41PAY over GD. For the USA it is OMB's FY 2027 Historical Tables, Table 3.2 line 901 (gross interest) over Table 7.1 (gross federal debt), the model's own perimeter. The rollover toward issuance is unchanged: 1/AverageDebtMaturityYears a year, with each country's sourced maturity.

## AMENDED 2026-10-28 after the first measured run: the denominator corrected (review M1)

The first Unity run on main (d14a720f, prereg 84fa8d08) printed every figure above to the tenth. The review (`Reviews/2026-10-28_k252_review.md`, M1) then found that the seed divided 2025 interest by the END-2024 stock, while the model charges the rate on a stock that opens at end-2025 (§621). That overstated day-one interest by the year's debt growth. The ruling, a K-line at merge, makes the implied rate 2025 interest over the AVERAGE of the end-2024 and end-2025 stocks, the standard convention. Both stocks come from the same fetched files, and the tool refuses unless both years are present. The bands and tolerances are unchanged.
