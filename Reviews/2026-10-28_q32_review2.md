## The first review's findings

**H1 - FIXED.** `DebtEventRecord.StockWriteOff` is set only by the haircut (`Enter` passes `stockWriteOff: true`; every other `RecordEvent` caller takes the default false). Every reader the first review named now skips it: `FiscalOneOff.FromLedger` (so `FiscalTurnReport.BudgetBalance`, `OneOffs` and `OneOffLines`), hence `History.BudgetBalanceAnnual` and `FiscalVote.CloseYear` (both take the report's balance), and `FiscalVote.OpenRatio` (its own loop). The debt ledger keeps the event, so the close's audit (terms + events + clamp = observed change) still closes, and `StatTracePanel` still shows it as a dated event. `GetSettledPosition` adds the haircut back as `h.Billions / gdpNow * 100`. That is the right sign and the right denominator: `gdpNow` is the `ClosingStat.Gdp` closing, which is `NominalGdp`, and the stock is nominal billions, so the add-back turns `(D_now - D_prior) / Y_now` into `(D_now + H - D_prior) / Y_now`. It reads the Published closings, not the ledger, so nothing is counted twice. On alignment: `PublishDueFigures` runs first in the day, before `ReviewIfDue` and `SovereignCrisis.AdvanceDay`. A haircut taken on a 1 October review therefore falls after the prior year's Jul-Sep closing (last written on 30 September) and inside the next one. The window `[review - 1y, review)` agrees with the span the two closings cover, on every day of the year (see N3 for the one case where it does not). The save carries the list as a public field (format 66, not yet shipped, so no bump is owed). `Copy()` now copies the list instead of sharing it, and the preview clone always has a non-null `Crisis` (`?.Copy() ?? new`).

**M1 - FIXED.** `Enter` now requires `TriggerReviewedOn == today.Date`. Question (2), can a legitimate first crisis now be missed: no. `Country.Rating.Rating` changes only where `ReviewIfDue` sets `LastReviewDate`, which is the fiscal-year start or the first review. The quarterly refresh writes only `Outlook`. So the standing rating, and with it the trigger, can never reach CCC between reviews; there is nothing between reviews to miss. The order inside `AdvanceDay` (phase end, then the trigger re-read, then entry) lets a programme that ends on its anniversary review day re-enter that same day if the review reads CCC. That is the K141 reading. A default's exclusion ending around 25 August waits for 1 October, which is what the fix intends.

## The briefed questions

**(5) DebtPathHold.** `ModelAt` reads the opening state as the start's calendar year and record y as start year + y. For a mid-year start (Italy 21 Jul 2022, Germany 6 Nov 2024), both ends of the model's change sit a constant fraction of a year before the DSM's year-end readings. The window's length matches the DSM's, so on a path whose slope changes slowly the bias is a fraction of one year's slope difference. It is DECLARED in the header. Every guard fails rather than passes: a start outside 2022-2029, a century that does not reach 2035, a USA series that does not reach `to`. Each calls `Check(false, ... UNVERIFIED, not clean)`. One exception: the USA's `_sb.Append` indexes `cbo[from - CboFirstYear]` before its NaN guard. That index throws only for a start past CBO's last year, and the outer `try` turns the throw into a failure, so it is still not a silent pass. The h1 band for a start other than 2024 is DECLARED (conditioned on 2024), and the h2 band now uses the fan's own width rather than a width re-based to the start. Both are consistent with the header.

**(6) MoneyIdentity (d).** The new asserts that are real: H1 on the default's day (the write-off on the ledger, absent from `FromLedger`, and `OpenRatio` the same with and without the event); the first closed year carrying no haircut line; the planted 1000 bn arithmetic at the last 1 October review (a four-year run has a closing a year back by then, so `before` and `after` both hold values, and the delta must equal `1000 / gdpNow * 100`); M1's no-off-review-entry counter; and the exclusion-end check. Two parts are weaker than they read. `reportClean`'s `BudgetBalance == FlowBalance + OneOffs` is true by construction, so only its no-haircut-line half tests anything (nit 2). And the in-run rating reading is vacuous; see N2.

## New findings

**N1 (Low) - The AF-17 identity is still stated without the exception H1 created.** After H1, in a default year the debt's change equals minus the year's balance minus the haircut. Three places still state the old identity. (a) Player-facing: `StatsSlips` line 130 adds "THE DEBT'S CHANGE IS THE DEFICIT" to the one-offs anchor whenever the closed year has any one-off. Failure scenario: the USA defaults in a year that also books a cabinet one-off or a fund drawdown. The slip then tells the player the debt's change is the deficit, while the debt fell by 37 % of its stock against a balance near zero. (b) The `FiscalTurnReport.BudgetBalance` doc ("the closed year's deficit IS the debt's own change"). (c) `DomesticMoneyBasisDiagnostic` asserts that identity, the balance as terms plus all events, and one line per event. A default inside its four years would fail all three as a regression. It does not reach one today, so nothing is red now, but the assertion encodes an identity the model no longer has. Fix: carve out `StockWriteOff` in the three places, for example "the debt's change is the deficit, less any write-off", and in the diagnostic subtract the write-off events.

**N2 (Low) - (d)'s in-run rating check cannot be reached, by construction.** The default fires at the first review (day one), and no closings are seeded before the start (`PublishedData` restores only saved closings). A scheduled review with a closing a year back is therefore at least about a year after the start. But the gate also requires `entered >= review - 1y`, so the check never runs and the `read:` line always prints. What goes untested is the end-to-end alignment I verified by reading under H1: the closing written before the crisis step. Failure scenario: someone moves `PublishDueFigures` after `SovereignCrisis.AdvanceDay`, or the haircut into the macro step. The prior closing then records the post-haircut stock, the add-back counts the haircut twice, and the next review reads a deficit of about +100 % of GDP. The planted arithmetic still passes, because it tests only the loop. Fix: make the reading reachable by running a year before raising the debt to 290 %, so the default lands on a 1 October review with a year of closings behind it. Then make "not reached" a failure, not a `read:` line.

**N3 (Low) - The add-back window keys on the review date, the deficit on the closings.** The two agree only for a haircut taken on a quarter start. `ReviewIfDue` reads `GetSettledPosition` every day for the outlook, and the prior closing is the last value of the quarter containing `date - 1 - 1y`, which is later than `date - 1y`. Take a haircut on a mid-quarter day (only the first-day review can produce one). On the days whose window holds it while the prior closing already postdates it, the haircut is added back although the stock change never contained it. Example: a start on 3 Sep 2024 that defaults on day one; on 2 Jul to 4 Sep 2025 the outlook reads a deficit of about +100 % of GDP. Only the outlook moves, since the standing rating changes only on review days, and no real start rates CCC on day one, so this is diagnostic-only. Fix: test `h.Date` against the two closings' own end dates (the prior quarter's end and the settled quarter's end) instead of `date - 1y`, or declare the restriction to quarter-start haircuts in the DECLARED line.

**N4 (Low) - The format-66 line in `SaveGameService` does not name the fields this round adds:** `DebtEventRecord.StockWriteOff`, `SovereignCrisisState.Haircuts`, `FiscalPeriod.AccruedCrisisCut` and `FiscalTurnReport.CrisisCut`. No bump is owed, since 66 has not shipped, but the line is the record of what a v66 save holds.

**Nit 1.** `TurnPreview.CrisisCut` has no reader. K26's debt-limit cut has a slip line on the preview's spending anchor (BudgetV35 1440); the crisis cut is computed and documented but never shown.

**Nit 2.** In (d), `openRatioClean` removes the haircut event and re-adds it at the end of the list. The list order only matters to itemisation, and the event was the day's last anyway. The `reportClean` balance equation is a tautology (see item 6 above).
