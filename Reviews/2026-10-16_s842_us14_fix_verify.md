NEW DEFECTS

MEDIUM - UsDebtLimitCheck.cs:335-346, with the claim at :26, Tools/us_debt_limit_prep.pl:5 and :207, and docs/generated/US_DEBT_LIMIT.md:4.
- **What the test does.** It checks `prose.Contains(Group(part))`, a plain substring. A short figure is "found" in any longer number or date. em.margin_reached_mil (26), em.margin_last_mil (26), mspd.step.3 (0) and the measures' parts 19 and 20 are always matched by "2026", "2023-01-19" or "2025". A figure printed in the wrong place also passes.
- **Demonstrated.** Each of these prose edits passes with the body digest re-stamped:
  - "26 under on 2025-01-21" → 27, and both 26s → 27;
  - "ESF 20" → 21;
  - "CSRDF/PSRHBF 19" → 18;
  - "0 from 2023-06-30" → 1;
  - one occurrence of a date that is printed twice.
- **The real-world case.** A future edit of the prep script drops or garbles a short figure in the prose. The script re-stamps the digest, and this test, the only guard on the prose, cannot fire.
- **Fix.** Match on token boundaries, for example `Regex.IsMatch(prose, @"(?<![\d.,-])" + Regex.Escape(printed) + @"(?![\d]|[.,]\d)")`. Or narrow the claim to say what the test does.

## Disposition (24f5fdb7, integrated in §842)

- **MEDIUM, the prose test was a substring search:** a figure now counts only as a token of its own (`(?<![\d.,-])` + the figure + `(?![\d]|[.,]\d)`), and a figure the blocks hold N times must be printed at least N times. Re-run with the verifier's mutations, every edit above fails: 26→27 (one or both), ESF 20→21, CSRDF/PSRHBF 19→18, "0 from"→"1 from", and a bogus key. The committed document passes. Still not caught, and stated in the code: where a figure is printed, and a figure printed more often than the blocks hold it. The body digest holds both.
- **H.R. 5371's actions** are quoted whole, each tagged with the variant it comes from (roll no. 285 is the House floor actions variant). The gloss says so.
- **2 U.S.C. 631's empty [...] row** is deleted.
- **The key sets are closed:** the Data block holds exactly its 17 lines and the Figures block its 36, each once.
- **The derived labels** are formatted from the derived days: the reinstatement's heading, "a Saturday", "on or before", the projection's month and the 2023 episode's year. **Noted, not fixed:** the engine block's label "2 January 2025's opening", which needs a WriteBridge run.
- **em.exhaustion** must be one whole month, its first day to its last.
- **(i)(a)'s line** says what it tests: the seed is the same on every start (§621). (i)(b) is true by construction.
- **debt_limit.md** words the reproduction with the thirteen whole-million lines' rounding.
- **The Data block** is byte-identical to §838's, so the engine block stands without WriteBridge. The new body digest is dedf91d6.
