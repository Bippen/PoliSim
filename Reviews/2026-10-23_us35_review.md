## What was verified (and holds)

- **The lines come from the model.** The probe at `scratchpad/us35_review/probe` checks `UsCongressView.OverrideLine(M) = (2M+2)/3` against the least YEA with YEA >= 2(M-YEA) for every M from 1 to 535: 0 mismatches. That is the same count as `Congress.OverrideVote.Required`. Cloture calls `Congress.ClotureLine` itself. The House majority line, seated/2+1, is equivalent to `HoldsHouse`'s 2s > total. `UsBillPath.OverrideBase` is `Congress.OverrideCount`'s base formula, copied.
- **The ten days.** `TenDays` loops to `PresidentialDocket.LastDay`, so the count is not done twice. The diagnostic's (a) covers every presentment day of 2026-2027, including Saturdays, Sundays and the year end. Presentment on a Sunday reads "0" (that day is not counted). The law day is LastDay+1 and can fall on a Sunday, as the docket models it.
- **The debt chip against `UsDebtLimit`'s states.** The states are suspended, below, reached with the measures running (with or without a raise pending or on the desk), and binding (with or without the raise on the desk). The chip covers each of them and claims none the model cannot be in. "AT THE LIMIT · 0 DAYS" cannot occur, because `Binding` starts on the day `MeasuresDaysLeft` reaches 0. "ON YOUR DESK" needs `BudgetBill.Docket`, which only the player-president's docket sets. UsDebtLimitLiveDiagnostic (e) still has teeth: it pins the binding state's new words, the absence of the measures-running words, and chip == "LIMIT BINDS · CUT IN FORCE" with ink Cut.
- **"Never predicts the override."** The desk's Veto line gives the lines and the passage's yeas. The lines card gives the majority party's seats against each line. Neither says "would override".
- **`Read` is pure.** It builds new objects. `UsSenate.On` writes only its existing per-day memo, and `SeatSides` builds a new dictionary. Nothing new is saved.
- **Other countries.** `DrawCongressV35` returns false unless `Congress.Applies` holds. `BuildChamberPair` is gated by `RecordSeatsSecondChamber` (USA only). `BuildUsPath` and `BuildUsAnswers` are gated by `UsBillPath.Applies` and need a non-null desk. Poland's six-argument `BuildAnswer` passes a null desk. The role chip, the lapse chip and the tile chip are all gated to the USA. `CongressRowsScroll` returns the old 900 for every other country.
- **Hygiene.** The gate prints GATE OK. Every committed file is LF, consistent with the base. The five new GUIDs are unique. No new player literal contains a § or a hand-built possessive. The one added `<see cref` line is the edited summary of `DrawRoleChip`, which names a field, so it does not trip the comment stripper. The PartyInkDrawSiteCheck entry is accurate: the file makes no area-accent call.

## Findings

### H1. `UsCongressViewDiagnostic` is never run by any bar
`Assets/Editor/CheckSuite.cs` registers every other `Us*Diagnostic` (UsCongressDiagnostic at :229, UsDebtLimitLiveDiagnostic at :602, and so on), but `"UsCongressViewDiagnostic"` appears nowhere outside its own file. Every claim in the file header, and in `UsCongressView`'s header ("`UsCongressViewDiagnostic` holds every figure"), is therefore enforced by nothing. If `ReadClasses` drifts from `UsSenate.On`, or a station's mark changes, the simulation bar stays green.
*Fix:* add the row to the suite's table, then run it.

### M1. A veto overridden by the origin and sustained by the other chamber is reported with the wrong chamber's votes
`UsBillPath.cs:103-107`. `RecordVeto` writes the second chamber's vote with `OtherChamber = first`, and the plate shows that second record. `Of` sets `first = record.OtherChamber` (the origin, which overrode), and the SUSTAINED sub reads `Votes(first) + " for · " + first.Required + " needed"`.
*Scenario:* the House overrides 300 to 135 (290 needed), and the Senate sustains with 60 (67 needed). The plate reads "SUSTAINED · The House 300 for · 290 needed". It shows the chamber that met its line and never names the Senate's failure.
The diagnostic's (c) plants only an origin-sustained veto and a veto overridden in both chambers, so this case goes untested.
*Fix:* report the chamber whose vote failed (`second ?? first`), and add a planted case.

### M2. The desk's Veto line says an uncontested bill passed the House "with 0"
`SigningScreen.UsPath.cs:203`, `UsBillPath.Yeas` at `UsBillPath.cs:159`. For a passage with no sides, `Yeas` returns `r.Sworn`. A House division's `Sworn` is never set (`Congress.cs:395`: `new Division { Chamber = House, ... }`, recorded at :514), so it is 0. `OverrideBase` correctly falls back to the House as seated.
*Scenario:* an uncontested bill on the player-president's desk; US-22's continuing resolutions are the stated case. The line reads "The override follows at once: 290 of 435 and 67 of 100. It passed with 0 and 100." `Congress.OverrideCount` counts YEA as the whole base here, so the House figure should be 435.
`Yeas` is right on override records, because `RecordOverride` sets Sworn = Members. It is wrong only on House passages.
*Fix:* fall back to `OverrideBase` when there are no sides, and pin the case in the diagnostic.

### M3. The Docket row reads "LAW UNSIGNED IN 0 DAYS" on every bill's last day
`GameController.PresidentialStart.cs:327`, `UsBillPath.CountedDaysLeft` at `UsBillPath.cs:73-78`. The count is the counted days after today, so on the period's last day it is 0. The bill is still on the desk that day (`Expired` is today > last), and it becomes law the next day.
The probe measured this on all 730 presentment days of 2026-2027. Board 30b's own case: presented 30 Apr 2027, and on Wed 12 May the row reads "LAW UNSIGNED IN 0 DAYS · ... 13 May". "In 0 days" contradicts the date printed beside it.
*Fix:* count to the law day, or word the last day as "LAW UNSIGNED TOMORROW". The plate's slip, "DAY 10 OF 10 · 0 DAYS LEFT", is coherent as written.

### M4. The lapse chip's "restored" memo survives a load
`GameController.Desk.cs:592` and `:603-626`. `_lapseLastSeenOn` and `_fundingRestoredOn` are controller fields that no load, new game or country switch resets. The bound added in f94c4b50 (within 3 days, clock not moved backwards) stops the false negative, but not this false positive.
*Scenario:* in game A a lapse is on screen on 1 Oct. The player loads game B, saved on 2 or 3 Oct, in which funding never lapsed. The masthead shows "Funding restored · back pay paid", and the slip cites 31 U.S.C. 1341(c), for a lapse game B never had. `_fundingRestoredOn` has the same flaw: it is never cleared when `_lapseLastSeenOn` is.
The header declares only the opposite case (a load on the restoration day shows no chip).
*Fix:* clear both fields wherever the controller adopts a world (load, new game, `SelectPlayerCountry`), or key the memo on the world's identity. Nothing tests the lapse chip at all.

### L1. `MetaTextCheck.Roots` lacks the two new files that build player words
`MetaTextCheck.cs:73` lists each Simulation file whose strings reach a page (UsDebtLimit.cs was added under US-24's review, L4). `UsBillPath.cs` (every station's status and sub, all drawn on the plate) and `UsCongressView.cs` (the line names) are missing. No literal hits today, but nothing will catch one later.

### L2. A 50-50 Senate reads "1 short" while it is held
`GameController.CongressV35.cs:330-335`. On an equal division the VP's party holds the Senate (`MajorityOf`), but the majority line reads "DEM 50 · 1 short", against "OR HALF AND THE VICE PRESIDENT'S VOTE" in the sub. The game's own elections can produce a 50-50 Senate, as the record did in the 117th Congress.

### L3. The VP row prints an unequal division when a seat is vacant
`GameController.CongressV35.cs:340`. With 99 sworn, `sworn/2 – sworn - sworn/2` prints "49–50" (the probe confirms), which is not an equal division.

### L4. The class rows carry no information until US-34's inks land
`PoliSimTheme.Party` returns the Neutral for REP and DEM, and independents are drawn in the Neutral too. The 100 class cells are therefore one colour, and "drawn apart" is not visible. The bars and codes still tell the sides apart. The file header declares the dependency, so this is ordering: integrate after US-34, or film it once US-34 is in.

### L5. The plate's ten days give a Sunday no "today" mark
`SigningScreen.UsPath.cs:150`. A Sunday cell gets no edge at all, so when today is a Sunday no cell is amber.

### L6. The "ON YOUR DESK" sub says "ten days" on a bill with a deadline of its own
`UsBillPath.cs:128`: "· ten days, Sundays excepted" is fixed text. For a continuing resolution with `Deadline` set (`CountsTenDays` false), the plate omits the ten days but this station still claims them.

### L7. "LEAVES" for "THE PRESIDENT'S PARTY" when the party keeps the White House
`GameController.Desk.cs:51-55`. When the winner differs from the sitting president, the tag reads "LEAVES 20 JAN", even when the winner is the same party's nominee (a term-limited president succeeded within his party). The person leaves; the party's role does not.

- `DrawCongressV35` now calls `Read` on every OnGUI event: 91d57499 removed the per-frame memo, and the call re-sorts 100 seats each time. Cheap, but no longer "read once a frame".
- `UsBillPath.Of`'s not-on-desk branch reads any passed-both record as "SIGNED". This is correct for every route that reaches it today (`QueueNewlyResolvedDivisions` skips Motions, and the player-president's bills go to the desk), except the film's staged answer plate (`PresidentialStart.cs:66`), which passes no desk.
