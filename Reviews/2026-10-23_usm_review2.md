## Findings

### H1 - The US Congress's verbs row breaks IMGUI layout on every US Budget frame (`GameController.BudgetV35.cs:1147-1153`)
`Rect row = GUILayoutUtility.GetRect(width, h, ...)` is followed by `if (placed > 0 && x + w > row.xMax) break;`. In the Layout event `GetRect` returns the 1x1 dummy rect. So Layout breaks after every button and asks for one row per verb, while Repaint uses the real width and packs two or more buttons to a row. The number of `GetRect` calls differs between the two events.

**Failure, shown live.** The film's world is a REP player-president with the limit reached, so it gets four verbs (two raises, two suspensions). `real907.log` logs `GUILayout: Mismatched LayoutGroup.repaint` 206 times (and drys907.log 242). Every US Budget frame fails its identity check: `05_budget`, `05a_*`, `05b_*`, `05c_*`, `05e_*`, `05r_*`, `03d_budget_process_open` and `05s_budget_us_congress_verbs` itself, 28 frames. The film ends "done, 158 captured, 33 failed". `real906d.log`, the same US film on `main` without this branch, has 0 mismatches and "190 captured, 0 failed".

**In play.** Any player-president with the limit reached sees a broken Budget page. That is exactly the page K85's chip sends him to, and the clock will not move until he clicks there. The code came in with `5e16a70c` (the first review's range), and the film frame the first review asked for is what exposed it.

**Fix:** decide the row breaks from `width`, the argument, not from `row.xMax`. Then re-film at 1280 at least.

### H2 - The audit driver does not answer the K85 hold, so US audits softlock (`Assets/Editor/AuditDriver.cs:816`, `:586`)
`AnswerHolds` answers the Fed chair, the opening, the scandal, cabinet decisions, the meeting, the budget window, the declaration and the formation, but not `DebtLimitHolds`. The driver was not changed in the delta.

**Scenario:** `f5_us_rep country=USA party=REP days=800` (`Tools/audit/area3_elections.plan`, also in `p4_ga3_4.plan` and `p4_s4words.plan`).
- The USA opens on 12 Mar 2024 (n907/real907: "the law in force at the start (2024-03-12)").
- The suspension ends on 1 Jan 2025, and the limit is reached on 2 Jan 2025.
- The game's REP president takes the oath on 20 Jan 2025.
- The limit binds on 13 Jul 2025, about day 488. K85 holds and nothing answers it. After `Stall` (2000) frames the run ends with "SOFTLOCK ... nothing the harness answers holds the clock", exit 1.

`HoldState` (`:1147`) does not name the hold either. The DEM long runs, `r4_us_idle10` (3650 days) and `w_us_idle30` (10,950 days), reach the same state whenever the game seats a DEM president while the limit binds. Before K85 these runs carried the cut instead.

**Fix:** answer it in `AnswerHolds`, for example with `ProposeDebtLimitRaise(id, RaiseUsd, MinValue)` (fuzz could pick a size or a suspension), log it as a `HOLD` event, and add it to `HoldState`.

### H3 - K78's live check (n) is red: the planted House does not survive a step (`Assets/Editor/UsDebtLimitLiveDiagnostic.cs:452`)
n907: `FAIL (n) PS-6 K78: a DEM House against the REP President (planted) withholds the raise ... (NEVER WITHHELD; ... "NOTHING IS WITHHELD"; the slip: "")`, and `=== UsDebtLimitLiveDiagnostic: 1 FAILURE(S) ===`.

The same log shows why. On the planted step the day loop re-seats the game's House: "CONGRESS: USA - the House the game elected on 2024-11-05 seated on 2026-02-20: REP 219, DEM 216". So `Congress.HouseMajority` reads REP, which is the presidency's party, `AiWithholder` returns null and nothing is withheld.

The code path of `AiWithholdDay` reads correctly to me, but its only end-to-end test is red, and the bar is red with it. **Fix:** stage the hostile House where the re-seating does not undo it (the game's own `UsCongressElections` record, or plant after the step and call the tick directly). Then make the tooth fail if the plant does not stand.

### L1 - M2's residue: a suspension can still become law after its last day through the desk
`MayPropose` requires the last day to be after the vote, but the bill then waits on the player-president's desk, and `Suspend` (`UsDebtLimit.cs:337`) accepts any `through`.

**Scenario:** the measures run out on 31 Dec 2027, and on that day the player proposes "Suspend to 1 Jan 2028" (the year after the vote). It is voted at once and passes. He signs it on 5 Jan 2028. `Suspend` sets a last day already gone, and the next `Track` reinstates the limit at that day's debt, which is reached again the day after. Not likely, but the title then says something false. **Fix:** in `Enact`, treat a suspension whose day has passed as refused or as a raise; or require the last day to be after the vote plus the desk's period.

### L2 - The film's US world sits under a binding limit for 21 months that play can no longer produce
The warm-up drives the manager without the controller's gates (`UiScreenshotDriver.AdvanceDays`). So in real907 the REP player-president's limit binds from 13 Jul 2025 to the frames' day, 30 Apr 2027, with the cut running throughout. The 05r slip reads "reached 2025-01-02". Under K85 play would have held on 13 Jul 2025. Every post-warm-up US frame shows that state and the K85 chip. Worth answering the hold in the warm-up, as the audit should (H2).

### L3 - The format comment does not name `Country.ContinuingResolutionWithheldInOffice`
The field is new in `3716010a` and rides the save (`Country.cs:955`, and the clone at `SimulationManager.cs:7399`). The format-57 comment (`SaveGameService.cs:104`) lists the other K75 fields but not this one, and `main`'s renumbered 58 comment does not name it either. Integration note: `main` has since taken 57 for §906 (K41/K42) and carries this set as 58. The integrator is doing that, but the comment should name the field.

### L4 - K78 never meets a vote at a median lead of 0 (for Elias, no defect)
Every raise is voted on `BindsOn` or later: the government's bill, the player's proposal, K43 and the fork's AI answer. The AI's withhold is released on `BindsOn`'s tick before the vote. So K78 changes the slip's lines and the logs, and no division or figure. That matches the record (all three episodes let the act pass at the deadline) and the ruling, but Elias should know the rule has no tooth in the model as built.

### L5 - K85 gives a player-president no answer that lets the limit bind
The other waits can be declined. This one is left only by proposing, and a veto empties the slot and holds the clock again. The binding cut can therefore last only as long as the desk holds a bill. That reads K85 literally ("holds the clock ... like the other waits"), but whether "let it bind" should be an answer is Elias's call.

### L6 - The 05s frame's skip is silent
`UiScreenshotDriver.cs:748` logs "NOT staged" with `Debug.Log`, not as a failure. A future film whose day falls inside a suspension would drop the frame and still pass. This is minor, because the current film stages it.
