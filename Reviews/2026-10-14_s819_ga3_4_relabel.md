# Review - §819, the gameplay audit's Phase 4: GA-3-4, a government relabelled by its seats when a supporter withdraws or a partner leaves (2026-10-14)

One read-only agent, of the patch in the tree while area 3's recheck (`p4_ga34`) ran: the record's new `Relabel`, its two callers' new argument, the saved `OfRecord` flag and `EconomicVote.TookOffice`'s reading of it - every reader of the record's label, the arithmetic against the formation's and the invariant's, every mutation of a seated record, the save and the bars. Its report, verbatim:

## The report

HIGH 0 / MEDIUM 1 / LOW 5. For the game the fix is correct: only the label changes, and `TookOffice` gives the same answer on every path. The real cost is that the GOVERNMENT invariant can no longer see a minority left behind by a supporter's withdrawal or a partner's exit, so the open row GA-4-1's symptom disappears from the harness.

Checked and correct:
- **Callers.** SimulationManager.cs:2807, :2890 and :2998 all pass the country. Every Editor caller goes through `SimulationManager.WithdrawSupport` or `LeaveGovernment`, so the build does not break.
- **Readers of the record's `Outcome`:**
  - EconomicVote.cs:108 is handled by the fix.
  - GameController.cs:2232 tests "what-if", which is never relabelled.
  - CongressOfRecordDiagnostic.cs:162 is the USA, a presidency, which Relabel skips.
  - PlayerRoleDiagnostic.cs:81/126/128 are untouched cases.
  - AuditTrajectory.cs:56 and AuditDriver.cs:489 only print it.
  - ElectionNightScreen.cs:702/743, GameController.CampaignCoalition.cs:74-83 and UiScreenshotDriver.cs:4406/5378 map the enum on a formation View or Result, not the record.
  - No UI and no Tools script reads the record's label, so no word mapping is missing.
- **Arithmetic** matches AuditInvariants.cs:209-218, CoalitionFormation.cs:598-600, SeatedGovernment.cs:117-121 and `CoalitionMath.Majority` (total/2+1). Seats are keyed by roster abbreviation. A seatless or absent cabinet party adds 0. A party in both sets counts once; the invariant counts it twice, which is harmless.
- **No other mutation needs a relabel.** Seats change only at a count (ParliamentSystem.cs:96-105) or at the USA's record seating (SimulationManager.cs:4001-4010, presidency only). No supporter joins and no partner returns in place; every new government is a new record. Discharge, a carried motion and the count's day are either exempt or leave the seats alone.
- **Provenance.** `OfRecord` is true exactly when the label was "of record" before the first relabel. New records start false, and `Provisional` is untouched. So `TookOffice` answers the same for a government of record, a play-formed one, a stand-in, a caretaker and an older save.
- **Blast radius.**
  - The no-policy dump and the shadow set no player country, so no exit runs there (AiWithdrawals returns at :2991).
  - TrajectoryBaselineDump reflects `EconomyState` only.
  - `Version` is bumped once per exit, as before, and ChamberVerdicts reads no label.
  - The new field rides Newtonsoft (`MissingMemberHandling.Ignore`), and the round-trip compare is whole-string.
  - What moves: the saved label, the new saved field, the trajectory's outcome column and the driver's event text.
- **Comments.** The figures 103 of 349 and 324 of 736 match ledger row 115 and the seat tables. The one new backticked `Type.Member` claim, `EconomicVote.TookOffice`, resolves for CommentClaimCheck.

Findings:

1. **MEDIUM, CONFIRMED - AuditInvariants.cs:229-230.**
   - The invariant exempts "MinorityGovernment" as declared, and Relabel now writes that label after every exit that leaves a minority.
   - GA-4-1 (open, S3) was measured by this breach: SD+M+KD on 172 of 349 seats after L leaves, labelled MajorityCoalition for 1,393 days. After the fix those runs pass with no breach, though still nothing follows the exit. A recheck with area4_office.plan would read as resolved.
   - The inventory's premise at C_elections_office.md:237, "MinorityGovernment can only arise in Sweden", is no longer true: Germany, Poland and Italy exits now produce it.
   - Fix: annotate GA-4-1's row now. Give the invariant a non-failing tally of minorities left by an exit (the dated exit is in `Breaks`), so GA-0-16 stays measurable.

2. **LOW, CONFIRMED - GovernmentRecord.cs:39-42.**
   - The comment calls `OfRecord` provenance ("the record came from the record of governments") and says only "an older save carries false".
   - But AtStart (:293) never sets it, so a new game's government of record also carries false until relabelled. A future reader testing `g.OfRecord` alone would miss every one of them.
   - Fix: set `OfRecord = true` at :293 (`TookOffice` is unchanged, since it reads both), or reword the comment.

3. **LOW, CONFIRMED - comments the fix makes false.** AuditInvariants.cs:200-201 and :228 ("the label is not recomputed when a partner leaves"), and docs/audit/inventory/C_elections_office.md:239 and :301-303. Fix: update them, or mark them as history.

4. **LOW, CONFIRMED - SaveGameService.cs:53-65.**
   - This session's convention is a "Nor for GA-x" note for each fix that does not bump the save version; GA-3-4 has none.
   - An older save cut after an exit keeps its stale label, because nothing relabels on load. It still breaches GOVERNMENT when resumed, for example by Tools/audit/real_saves.ps1.
   - Fix: add the note.

5. **LOW, PLAUSIBLE (latent) - `Relabel` is public and does not bump `Version`.**
   - That breaks §642's contract at GovernmentRecord.cs:83-88: every in-place change goes through the record's methods and each bumps the version.
   - ChamberVerdictCacheCheck's Guard catches direct writes only, not a method call.
   - It is harmless today because no cache reads the label. Fix: make it private.

6. **LOW, CONFIRMED - no standing check proves the new `|| g.OfRecord` term in `TookOffice`.**
   - PreStartRecordDiagnostic.cs:139-166 plants labels only, so deleting that term would pass every check except an audit rerun.
   - Fix: add a plant with `OfRecord = true`, Outcome "MinorityGovernment", Germany, FormedOn 2024-11-06, PM party SPD, expecting 2021-12-08.

## What was done

- **1 (MEDIUM): fixed in the harness, and GA-4-1's row annotated.** The GOVERNMENT invariant still passes a declared minority, but one an exit left - its record's `Breaks` dating a withdrawal or a departure - is counted a day at a time as the note EXIT-MINORITY: logged once per government with its seats and the dated exit, printed in the run's summary as "notes: EXIT-MINORITY xN", never failing the run (`AuditInvariants.Note`, new). `Tools/audit/p4_ga3_4_post.plan` runs GA-4-1's three (area 4's office plan, unchanged) on the fixed code: PENDING_POST. The inventory's line 237 is marked as history.
- **2 (LOW): reworded** - `OfRecord` says what it is: set as an "of record" label goes, false on every record never relabelled (a government of record among them, its label still saying so); the provenance is the flag or the label, read together, as `TookOffice` reads it. The code is unchanged (the reviewer's alternative, setting it at the start, would leave an older save's governments as the exception to the same rule).
- **3 (LOW): fixed** - the invariant's summary and its D-14 comment say the label was not recomputed until §819; the inventory's lines 239 and 301-303 carry the history in place.
- **4 (LOW): written down** beside `SaveGameService.CurrentSaveVersion`: an older save's government of record still reads as before; one cut after an exit keeps the label the exit left standing, and nothing relabels it on load.
- **5 (LOW): fixed** - `Relabel` is private; its doc names the contract.
- **6 (LOW): fixed** - the diagnostic's (d) holds every branch of `TookOffice`'s gate to a plant that fails without it, and the gate gained one: Scholz's government planted relabelled (`MinorityGovernment`, `OfRecord`) from 2024-11-07 walks back to 2021-12-08, where without the term it would answer its own day. The date mirrors the "of record" plant beside it, so the two differ by the label alone. Not a new check (R-N5): the existing (d)'s own rule.
