## New findings

### Medium

**N1. `ItalianNight.Certain` is still not a lower bound, and I built a wrong call. (e) states a property that holds only on its own fixtures.**

Where: `ItalianNight.cs:356-392` (the allocation set), `:278` (the stamp) and `ItalianNightDiagnostic.cs:29-31` (the claim).

How the bound is formed: the set tries one competitor at a time. Each competitor's largest list either takes the whole uncounted vote, or one near-line list is given just enough to clear its line and the rest goes to the rival. Whenever two or more entities each need part of the uncounted vote to stay over a line, every allocation in the set drops at least one of them. That entity's votes are wasted, and the leader's share of the admitted votes rises. The true minimum keeps all of them in, so the set's minimum exceeds what the leader ends with.

Measured with the branch's own code (scratch harness; shares are the 2022 Camera vote with the planted lists fixed and the rest scaled; roster 1):
- **Wrong call.** Az-IV, ScN and a created party each at 3.01 %, FdI at 19.05-19.55 %.
  - The Camera is called at 146 of 147 for FdI+Lega+FI+NM, with 201-202 certain.
  - That coalition ends with 198-200 seats including abroad, under the line of 201.
  - At FdI 19.60 % the call is still right (201 at the end).
- **Two lists alone at 3.01 % (Az-IV and a created party).** At Camera 146: certain 249 against an end of 247.
- **A list alone at 3.01 % and CSX at 10.03 % (PD 5.0, AVS 2.95, +E 2.08).** At Camera 146: certain 303 against an end of 300. The coalition's 10 % line (lett. e) has no allocation in the set at all.

The declared limitation is honest only in part. "A mixture of several competitors beyond the near-line one is not tried" (`ItalianNight.cs:28-29`) names the mixture gap. But:
- It does not say the consequence: a call can be stamped for a coalition that ends without a majority.
- It does not name the lines the set never targets:
  - the coalition's 10 % (`Rosatellum.CoalitionThreshold`);
  - a coalition admitted only through its one member at 3 %;
  - the Senato's 20 % regional route for a list other than SVP (`ItalianCount.cs:592`).
- (e)'s doc says "at EVERY declared count the leader's certain seats never exceed the seats it ends with", as if that were a theorem.

Mitigation:
- The live night opens at its close (`GameController.ItalianNight.cs:118`, `ItalianNight.Total`), and the stamp is drawn only while `!s.Final`.
- So today a wrong stamp reaches only the film's staged states and the log line's `called at`. It becomes visible to the player the day the night steps.

Suggested fix: make the certain seats a sound floor rather than a minimum over chosen allocations. Both are cheap and need no Count re-runs.
- **Camera:** lett. f's quotient is `floor(sum admitted / pool)`, which is at most `all valid / pool`. So the leader, once its admission is certain on counted votes alone, holds at least `floor(pool × L / all valid)`. Here:
  - "certain on counted votes alone" means coalition ≥ 10 % with a member ≥ 3 %, members under 1 % struck;
  - `L` is its counted, unstruck votes.
  - Lett. h/i preserve entity totals.
- **Senato:** the same bound region by region: `floor(seats_i × L_i / valid_i)`, summed.

If the set is kept instead, add the three plants above to (e) as must-hold cases (each fails today) and restate the doc and the lane's slip as a heuristic.

## Checked and clean

- **`stopBelow` short-circuits correctly.** The walk stops at the first allocation whose `colleges + least` falls below the line. The full minimum can only be lower, so "not called" is exact. When it does not break, the full minimum is computed, so `CalledCertain` is the true set minimum. The short-circuited value lives in locals at `ItalianNight.cs:277` and is never displayed. `LeaderCertain` comes from the unshortened call at `:281`.
- **(e)'s shortcut is sound.** The full set's first allocation is the `rivalOnly` allocation: same leader, same rival, and `Largest()` gives the same rival list. So certain ≤ single always holds, and wherever single ≤ end, certain ≤ end. The harness walked the full set at every count on all eight fixtures: certain > single never occurred. The only divergence is when the rival has no list standing in a college. Then `rivalOnly` falls back to the empty allocation while the full set does not. That is unreachable, because the rival is chosen by counted college votes.
- **The rework leaves the count untouched.** `ItalianCount.cs`, `Rosatellum.cs` and the count's own path are not in the rework diff. `Certain` passes `estero = null` and allocates fresh arrays, so the re-runs stay pure.
- **The sweep still passes.** 2022 vote, planted case and six-vote sweep: no overshoot by the set; the single allocation overshoots only at the planted count.
- **The generator is byte-exact.** Regenerated from `PoliSim-captures/.../night.json` into scratch, it is identical to the committed file, and the digest line is the same.
