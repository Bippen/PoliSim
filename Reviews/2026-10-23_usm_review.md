## What was verified, and how

1. **The floor (K21).** `RaiseOf` (`UsDebtLimit.cs:143`) is `Max(size or RaiseUsd, ExcessUsd)`. `ExcessUsd` is 0 while suspended and never negative. `Raise` and `Suspend` both clear `DebtLimitReachedOn`, `DebtLimitCutFlowDay` and `DebtLimitWithheldBy`, and they reset the cut's total. So law disarms the next day's cut, even when it comes from a docket answer between ticks, and law clears the withhold. `Enact` sends a suspension to `Suspend` and everything else to `Raise`. A raise at the floor lands the limit exactly on the day's debt. The next day's `Track` reaches it again and opens a fresh 192-day span. That is DECLARED and asked, but a reader should know it (L4).
2. **The same-day vote (K43).** `ProposeDebtLimitRaiseNow` is reached only from the UI or from a counterfactual replay. Neither runs inside `ActOnDocketAnswers`, whose `try/finally` resets `_docketAnswersOnly`, so the early return in `ResolveDebtLimitBillDay` cannot swallow the vote. With `DaysRemaining = 1` the vote happens at once. A player-president's desk then holds the bill. Signed through the docket pass, `Enact` disarms tomorrow's cut, so `UsLapse.OutlayToday` pays the lapse's back pay on the first unarmed day. The diagnostic's (e2) holds both.
3. **No double tabling.** Every route checks the single slot: `MayPropose` refuses while one is pending, and `AnswerDebtLimitAsTheAi` returns where one is pending. So a world never holds two raises. Pre-emption is a different problem (M1).
4. **Titles across worlds.** At `3476e9ab` the played world's floored size is handed to the replay (`SimulationManager.DebtLimit.cs:118`), and `BillTitle(bill)` formats it in the invariant culture. So the title is the same in every world that accepts the replay.
5. **K26.** The clone is armed only on the clone (`SimulationManager.cs:6947`), and only when the played country's cut is armed. `PreviewTurnOnClone` is only ever handed `ClonePreviewCountry(...)`, and no AI path calls a preview. The preview's `ApplyRevenueAndSpending` overwrites `_debtLimitCutToday` (`:8951`). That is harmless, because the only reader (`:8785`) runs right after the real day's own write.
6. **K75 on an oath day.** `FormedOn` and `WithheldOn` are both dates. An out-party withhold given before the oath day lapses at the oath (`FormedOn > WithheldOn`). A withhold given on the oath day, after the tick that swore the president in, stands as the president's own party's. That is correct. Re-election is the exception (L1).
7. **K74.** I grepped every `ElectionMethod.NotImplemented` and `.Method` reader in `Assets/Scripts` and `Assets/Editor`. Every parliamentary reader in the play path now reads `CountsParliament`. The remaining `== NotImplemented` sites (`ElectionDayReachDiagnostic`, `HistoryRolledForwardDiagnostic`, `SaveLoadRoundTripDiagnostic`) test the result of `NationalElection.Run`, which never returns `UsCongress`. `AuditInvariants` handles the new method. One cosmetic miss is left (L3). Non-US records keep their methods, so their readers are unchanged.
8. **K29.** Both college forms weigh each jurisdiction by its votes cast in the prior year of record. `FromNationalShares` uses `prior[r].VotesTotal`, and `FromGameStates` uses its `weightsYear`, which `CollegeOf` hands `prior`. `ResummedShares` uses the same year and the same `VotesTotal`. ME and NE are state calls, so nothing is counted twice. `Share` (the carry) is unchanged, and `StatesOn` still re-derives from it. Newtonsoft round-trips the `NaN` default.
9. **The sentinel cannot move.** `TrajectoryBaselineDump`/`TrajectorySentinelCheck` set no player country and read no `DebtLimit*` field. Grep finds no export, approval or interest reader of the limit in `Assets/Scripts`. With no Congress of the game, the cut is never armed: `AdvanceDebtLimitDay` returns at `UsBudgetProcedure.Applies`. So the limit is bookkeeping only in the sentinel's world. Also, `Max(5e12, excess)` equals `5e12` exactly whenever the excess is smaller. K13, K27, K29 and K74 run only in a player's US world.

## Findings

### M1 - The counterfactual worlds table the raise before the player can (K23 with K49)
`SimulationManager.PlayerElection.cs:49` calls `AnswerDebtLimitAsTheAi` (`SimulationManager.DebtLimit.cs:182-186`) at the start of each ledger world's day. The premise is in `ShadowBaseline.cs:72`: "the played clock holds on every such wait, so the player's answer, given the same day, is always replayed first". **A binding debt limit does not hold the played clock.** Scenario: a REP player-president's limit binds on day B, and the player proposes on B+3. Every except-world and the no-policy world tables the AI's +$5.0 tn on the morning of B+1. In those worlds it reaches the player-president's desk, which no AI rule answers, so it becomes law about ten days later. On B+3 the played proposal's replay is refused ("A RAISE IS BEFORE CONGRESS ALREADY") and logs "found no match". So the forks' cut days, raise size and division titles all differ from the played world with no policy difference, and each family's line in the ledger takes that difference. Before this branch the government tabled on the same day in every world.

Options: make a binding limit with nothing proposed a clock-holding wait for a player-president, like the other waits; or apply the AI's rule only in the no-policy world. This is Elias's call, since K49's "the next morning" was ruled on waits that hold the clock.

### M2 - A suspension can become law after its own last day (K22/K25)
`UsDebtLimit.MayPropose` (`UsDebtLimit.cs:162`) only requires `suspendThrough > today`. But a suspension proposed while the measures run is voted at `BindsOn`, up to 192 days later (`SimulationManager.DebtLimit.cs:134`). The UI offers "Suspend to 1 Jan {next year}" (`GameController.BudgetV35.cs:1116`). Scenario: the limit is reached on 20 Nov 2025 and binds on 31 May 2026. On 1 Dec 2025 the player clicks "Suspend to 1 Jan 2026" and it is accepted. On 31 May 2026 a division titled "...suspended through 1 Jan 2026" passes, and `Suspend` (`:263`) sets a past last day. The next day's `Track` (`:218`) reinstates the limit at that day's debt, and it is reached again the day after. The net effect is a raise to the debt with no headroom and a fresh span, under a title that says something else. Fix: require the day after the vote (`BindsOn` where not binding), in `MayPropose` and in the offered days.

### M3 - The new Budget surface has no film evidence
The tiering rule makes this UI commit owe the dry film and one filmed width. The branch has no capture, and the surface appears only in a staged state: a player-president with the limit reached, a party holding a chamber against the presidency, or a CR due. A film that does not reach that state claims nothing for the verbs row (`GameController.BudgetV35.cs:1063-1167`), its wrapping at the 14 px floor or the K26 slip line. The integrator owes a film session that stages it, at 1280 at least.

### L1 - K75: a re-elected president's own-party withhold lapses at the second oath
`UsBudgetProcedure.cs:137` compares against `Government.FormedOn`. But every oath forms a new government (`UsPresidentialElection.Governs`, `:264`, needs `FormedOn >= TakesOffice`; see `SimulationManager.cs:5374`), even when the same party is re-elected. Scenario: REP holds the presidency, REP's own CR withhold is given on 15 Jan 2029, and REP is re-elected. On 20 Jan 2029 the withhold silently lapses, although the party never left office. Fix: compare against the party's unbroken tenure, or store the presidency's party at the withhold.

### L2 - K22: a withhold outlives the chamber that made it bite
`DebtLimitWithheldBy` is cleared on law, on release or when the party takes the presidency. It is not cleared when the party loses its chamber at a midterm. The slip then still says "a chamber it holds stops it". The effect is harmless (the raise passes), but the words are false. AI parties never withhold (DECLARED, asked). K22's "a hostile chamber can withhold" reads wider than that, which is Elias's to confirm.

### L3 - `Assets/Editor/ForkAnswersDiagnostic.cs:303` still reads `!= NotImplemented`
It runs on a non-US world, so it is harmless today. It is the one K74 site not moved to `CountsParliament`.

### L4 - At the floor the limit is reached again the next day, with a full new measures span
That is DECLARED in `RaiseOf`'s comment. It means a K21 floor raise buys 192 days. Also, the "excess" button's size is floored again at the vote's later excess (`GameController.BudgetV35.cs:1101`, `UsDebtLimit.cs:143`), so the division's title states less than the law enacts. K77 replaces both (addendum).
