## Findings

### M1 (Medium) - Poland's daily s641 constructive vote is switched on, unruled and unmeasured
Routing `TryAiMotion` through `IsConstructive` makes `TryAiConstructiveVote` run on every confidence day of a player's Poland (AdvanceConfidenceDay -> TryAiMotion), not only after an exit. K133 rules the three countries for COAL-1's procedure; nothing measures the s641 daily vote in Poland. Failure scenario: a player's Poland after a game-held election whose Sejm steps end in the Sejm's own cabinet (Art. 154 ust. 3) on a thin majority - the day the formation closes, the daily weighing can now install a PiS-led successor by Art. 158 that no Polish run could produce before COAL-1, and every Polish day pays a DraftSuccessor per candidate (the Germany figure was about 12 ms a day). No diagnostic walks a Polish term (CabinetPressureDiagnostic only steps budget days; PolishPresidentialStartDiagnostic does not step confidence days). Fix: either gate the Polish branch to `afterExitOf != null` until the daily rule is ruled for Poland, or add a measured walk of a player's Poland over a term and record it.

### M2 (Medium) - the player-president's party is treated as the player's in the new Polish paths (SP-7b, I14 (2a))
`WeighPartnerExits` skips `partner == country.PlayerPartyAbbrev`, and `AiMotionCandidates`/`DraftSuccessor` exclude and decline the player's party - but while `PlayerHoldsPresidency` that party is the AI's (CabinetSystem 512, FinancePartner 133, LeaveGovernment refuses with PresidentOfficeWords). Failure scenario: the player starts as President Holownia (TD) or Biejat (NL), whose party is a junior partner of KO's cabinet: K130 never weighs TD's exit and the player cannot leave either, so that partner can never exit. With President Nawrocki (PiS), PiS - the largest opposition party - can never move Art. 158, even after an exit, and is declined from every successor draft. Fix: treat the party as AI (`PlayerPartyAbbrev` only when `!PlayerHoldsPresidency`) in these three places, or declare the reading.

### M3 (Medium) - the constructive branch lifts the gate for every opposition party, not the declared opposition; the comment says otherwise
K131: "any DECLARED opposition holding a majority moves". The Riksdag branch filters movers by `RedLinedFrom`; `TryAiConstructiveVote(country, afterExitOf)` filters nothing but the departed partner, and the gate is lifted for all of them. `ExitOpensConfidenceProcedure`'s summary and the file header claim the declared opposition moves under Art. 67 and Art. 158 too - that is untrue for the code. Failure scenario: Germany, the FDP leaves SPD+Gruene+FDP; the CDU (no declared line against the SPD) moves and, with the gate lifted, installs its successor - the diagnostic's case (4) depends on exactly this. Either restrict the constructive movers to `RedLinedFrom` (and re-plant case 4), or DECLARE the reading ("under a constructive vote the motion is the successor's election, so any party that carries one moves") in the comment and the report to Elias.

### M4 (Medium) - the diagnostic never proves the gate is lifted, and it is not on the bar
Case (2)'s control and case (4)'s start chamber are printed, not asserted, and in both asserted cases the s641 gate would likely pass anyway (V would sit in the round's cabinet; the CDU prefers its own chancellery). Mutation: restore `after <= now + DefectionMargin` / the round-preference test for the exit path - every check still passes. The second clause of K131 is therefore unproven. Also `CoalitionExitDiagnostic` is not registered in `Assets/Editor/CheckSuite.cs` (ConfidenceDiagnostic and ConstructiveVoteDiagnostic are, lines 208-209), so no bar run exercises COAL-1. Fix: plant a case where the gate would refuse (the mover's payoff after <= now) and assert the motion is still moved after an exit and not moved without one; register the diagnostic.

### L1 (Low) - the minority break can state a falsehood
`ExitOpensConfidenceProcedure` writes "no declared opposition carries a motion" whenever `TryAiMotion` leaves the record untouched - including when it returned on the week-before-polling-day guard, on a pending extra election, on Germany's round guard, or when the only declared party with a tenth of the seats is the player's own (excluded as an AI mover). Scenario: the player as S (95) in a chamber where V, C and MP are each under 35 seats; an AI partner leaves and the declared side holds 177 - the record says no motion carries. Say why nothing was moved.

### L2 (Low) - the quiet window reads chamber polling days only
`NextPollingDayOrNone`/`LastPollingDay` read the parliamentary election; Poland's 2025 presidential polling days (18 May, 1 June) are not "a polling day" here. The reading is defensible but undeclared in the DECLARED list.

### L3 (Low) - "majority of the statutory number" read as seated members
Art. 158 counts the statutory 460; `Needed = Members / 2 + 1` counts seated deputies. Equal while every seat is filled (the md states it); a chamber with unfilled seats would lower the bar.

### L4 (Low) - an AI exit can hand a junior-partner player vacant chairs without K59's seating
`LeaveCabinet` re-apportions the departed partner's portfolios; if the player is another junior partner, new chairs become the player's and stand VACANT, while every install path calls `CabinetSystem.SeatOnEntry`. Scenario: player KD in M+KD+L, AI L exits, an L portfolio moves to KD and shows VACANT.

### L5 (Low) - a Polish budget held on the President's docket survives a government change
With Art. 158 now live, a constructive vote (daily or after an exit at the hold's budget vote, path 2020/2024) installs a successor while the old government's budget acts sit on the docket; `FinishPolishBudget` later applies them to the new government. Edge case; state it or clear the slot in `InstallSuccessor` for Poland.

### L6 (Low) - diagnostic hygiene
Seeds are re-seeded (777) and never restored (the sibling diagnostics' idiom; matters once registered in the suite, where ChamberVerdictCacheCheck's capture/restore is the model); epoch is restored by `EpochScope`, `PlantProjection` by `using`, CreatedParties and WorldClock are untouched. "One exit per vote" and its tie-break are untested (one partner is planted each time). Case (5)'s player-verb check passes on either of two outcomes.

### L7 (Low) - fetch_log time stamp
`fetch_log.txt` records `2026-10-27T00:00:00Z` - a midnight placeholder, not the fetch's time.
