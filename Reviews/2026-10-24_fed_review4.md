## (2) The (e3) follower probe (`FedChairConsentDiagnostic.cs:463-534`)

### What it does

1. **The control.** It builds a fresh US world (`UsWorld`, turn 0 day 0, so `lastDay` is the turn's day 90) with the player seated (`:471`), the chair's term planted to end on `lastDay`, and the Vice Chair's lean at +2.
2. **The first turn.** It drives days 1-90 in `RunDaysThrough`'s order: the morning answer, `AdvanceDay`, `AdvanceCountryDayTick`, and the election except on the last day.
3. **The follower.** It forks one world on day 10 (`insideTurn`, `dayTick`, `seatAsPlayed`, `AnswersWaitsAsTheAi`).
4. **The close.** It runs it in `GameController.AdvanceTurn`'s order (`:502-505`): the ledger's world first, then the real boundary, then `EnergyMarket.BeginTurn`, then the election held after the boundary.
5. **The pick.** Only then does it lodge the pick through the real verb (`:509`). It plants the same lodge in the follower with the same party and day (`:514`), which is what `Schedule`'s day-0 branch (`ShadowBaseline.cs:248`) does through the lever's replay closure (`SimulationManager.cs:3851`).
6. **The comparison.** It compares `FedStateOf` (chair, term start, acting flag, pending nominee and vote day, vote count) and the zone rate (`"R"` format, so exact). It compares at the close and on each day to `VoteOn`, and holds the played chair seated on `VoteOn`.

### Is the control a faithful stand-in for the played world? Yes, for the property at issue

- **The order that matters is kept.** The boundary runs before anything lodges, and the pick lands on day 0 of the next turn. That is the played world's order: `GameController.cs:789-790` crosses the boundary inside the same loop iteration, and the hold is re-checked only at `:807-814`.
- **The control is marked counterfactual (`:472`), and on the Fed path this changes nothing.**
  - `PlayerAppointsCentralBankHead` turns false in the control. But `AppointCentralBankHeadsWhoseTermsEnded`'s AI condition (`SimulationManager.cs:3949`) is still blocked by `FedAwaitsPlayersPick`, which does not read `IsCounterfactual`.
  - So on day 90 the control seats nothing and the Vice Chair acts, exactly as the played world does.
  - `CentralBankHeadAwaitsPlayer` (the hold) is not read by the manager's own day.
  - The verb gates on `PlayerMayIntroduce` and the pending check only (`:3843-3848`), so it lodges in the control as in the played world.
- **The control's morning answer never reaches the Fed.**
  - Before day 90 the term has not ended. Day 90's morning runs on day 89's date.
  - The next turn's mornings all find the nomination pending, because the lodge comes first.
  - Every other wait it answers (cabinet, foreign-policy, debt limit) is answered identically in the follower, which is also a counterfactual. That is the comment's stated purpose (`:465-466`).
- **What the control omits from `GameController.Update`** (`QueueNewlyResolvedDivisions`, `CheckPresidentialRound`, `CheckUsHouseChoice`, autosave, the shadow world) moves no Fed state or rate here, and the follower omits them too.
