## 2 · The floor, which is what the ruling asked to be fixed

`LevyScaleWith` floors the SUPPORT at zero (`Math.Max(0.0, share * own + dialCost)`) and the SCALE at zero past the whole levy. The held code's `LevyScaleAtDial` clamped the dial's cost at **the whole line** (`-Math.Max(0.0, own)`) while the support is only the line's **share**: the reading told the page the floor sat further down the dial than the book's own support reaches. It now clamps at `-Math.Max(0.0, share * own)`. **Checked both ways**: the ledger diagnostic's floor probe (`past the whole levy the floor did not hold`) passes, and the pass-through probe is unchanged and green.
