## Amendment r2 - after review 1 (Reviews/2026-10-29_k276_review1.md), registered before any measurement of the amended tree (2026-10-29)

It answers review 1's M1 and M2; it changes no band and no year, and it does not re-form the USA's (c), which failed at -3.43 % in 2040 (M3) and stays as registered until Elias rules (K279).

**r2-1 (M1) - the response's reference is the age the projection was made under.** The Ageing Report projects Sweden's and Italy's participation under the pension age linked to life expectancy for the whole horizon; the game's statute publishes only to 2032 (Sweden) and 2028 (Italy) and then carries the last figure (board 15c-r2). From the year after its horizon, an indexed statute's reference is the age the Ageing Report assumed: the 2024 Ageing Report's country fiche, Table 1 "Qualifying conditions for retirement" - Sweden, "Earliest age for a guaranteed pension - men and women" (the age the riktalder sets), Italy, "Statutory retirement age" (men and women alike) - linear between the fiche's years, held after its last, and never below the statute's carried figure. The fiche's figures are cross-checked by the prep tool against the report's Table I.1.3 (2030, 2050, 2070) within 0.05 years. Inside the horizon, and for a scheduled or fixed statute (Germany, France, Poland, the USA), the reference is the statute's age for the year, as before. **Consequence, DECLARED before measurement:** in the no-policy run Sweden (after 2032) and Italy (after 2028) carry a NEGATIVE response - their labour force is the projection's less the participation of the indexation the game's carried statute does not enact - so participation and the pension line read the same law. A player's bill is measured from the reference, so no step is counted twice.
