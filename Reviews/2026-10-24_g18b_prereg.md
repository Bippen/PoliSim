# GATE-18 piece two - the pass rule, registered before the first run of Gate18Diagnostic (2026-10-24)

Drafted by the implementer of `wave/g18b` (23e1137a); committed by the integrator before the gate's first run.

For each chamber election of record in `RecordFactors.Elections` (Sweden 2026 Riksdag, Germany 2025 Bundestag, Poland 2023 Sejm), on the idle game the diagnostic defines (the country's start, seed 777, the start's prime minister's party played idle, waits answered by K49's AI rule, the whole run under `ParliamentaryCount.NoiseAtZero`), the gate passes only if all of the following hold:

- (a) every stored factor is a finite number of zero or more, patched from a FIT line of this diagnostic's own log (`Tools/chamber_factor_patch.pl`);
- (b) the count of the polling day of record was drawn at sigma 0 and handed exactly the shares times the stored factors;
- (c) every party's counted share is within 1.5 points of the record's share over the roster's total;
- (d) where the record names the first non-caretaker, sourced cabinet that took office within `WorldClock.FormationWindowDays` after the polling day, the game's first government formed after that day (not seated from the record, its procedure complete) has that head's party as prime minister's party and exactly that cabinet. Where the record names none, (d) is printed NOT ON RECORD and asserts nothing.

For Poland's 2025 presidential election, on E1's reference world: every first-round candidate of record within 1.5 points of the PKW's, and the run-off electing the record's winner with his share of the two within 1.5 points of the record's.

The out-of-sample figures (the same count at factor one; the first round at factor one) are printed and are never a pass condition. A refit drift is reported, not failed. The gate's first run is expected to fail on (a).
