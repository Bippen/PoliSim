## Findings, most severe first

### 1. The four shares are not read by one test — CONFIRMED
`EnergyLedger.cs:151-171`.

The doc defines the share as what funds "Eurostat's renewable, capacity and other charges". Italy's cap. 2037 is excluded by a component-matching argument: it replaces a TAX_NUC charge, which is not in the book's policy levy.

I fetched Eurostat `nrg_pc_204_c` and `nrg_pc_205_c` for 2023; they are saved as `review_pap/es204.json` and `es205.json`. France's book "policy" component is 100 % TAX_CAP:

| France 2023 | TAX_CAP | TAX_RNW | OTH |
|---|---|---|---|
| Households | 0.0062 | 0 | 0 |
| Non-households | 0.0018 | 0 | 0 |

- Action 09 funds renewable electricity, whose bill component is zero in the book. By the Italian test, France's share is NONE, or action 13 (effacements) alone at 1.37 %.
- The French test is the opposite one: budget support in a category that Regulation (EU) 2016/1952 Annex II lists under RNW, CAP or OTH. I fetched Annex II; it reads:
  - RNW: "renewable energy sources, energy efficiency and CHP generation".
  - CAP: "capacity payments, energy security and generation adequacy; taxes on coal industry restructuring; … financing energy regulatory authorities".
  - OTH: "island compensation".
- Applied to the other three, the French test gives:
  - **Poland:** rozdział 10001 is 97.95 % of dział 100 (all dotacje in część 48). The budget act does not itself label it restructuring, so I rate this PLAUSIBLE.
  - **Sweden:** appropriations 1:2, 1:4, 1:5, 1:10 and 1:11 total 2 859 451 tkr, 36.0 % of UO21. `se_uo21.txt:3567` and `:4869` describe 1:5's kraftlyftet as investment support for "utökad elproduktionskapacitet … leveranssäkerheten".
  - **Italy:** cap. 7666 (30 M, 2.86 %) and cap. 7660 (6.3 M, 0.60 %).
- **Scenario:** Poland's and Sweden's cells read 0.000 because the test was narrowed for them. France's 18.4 % stands because it was widened.
- **Smallest fix:** state ONE test in the doc comment and apply it to all four. Which test to use is the owner's ruling.
