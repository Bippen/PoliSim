- **Why t1 and t2:** under the new start, 1 January falls on day 92 of the turn window; under the old start it fell on day 365. That is days 92 against 365 for the 2027 steps, and days 457 against 730 for France's 2028 step.

- **Participation first moves at t3** for France and Germany. That is when the old start's boundaries begin to fall in the previous calendar year (2028-12-31).
- **Sweden and Poland at t4 is spillover, not a statute prediction.** Euro-area unemployment and the Taylor gap move at t3, the euro rate at t4, and with it the currencies. The USA also moves through its currency at t4. Italy's participation moves only at t5, by spillover. USA participation never moves in 20 turns.

## 3. The century debt moves are seed noise under §599 — REFUTED

**The figures use the wrong ratio.** 242.3 → 255.3 is nominal debt over real GDP (21916.1/9045.06). The game's ratio is `EconomyState.DebtToGdpRatio = GovernmentDebt / NominalGdp` (`EconomyState.cs:369`). §599 itself quoted "% of nominal GDP" (24.20 → 24.12).

Recomputed at t100, debt as % of nominal GDP (pp4 → ep1):

| country | seed 777 | seed 424242 |
|---|---|---|
| Germany | 31.43 → 33.04 (**+1.61**) | 33.61 → 33.71 (**+0.10**) |
| France | 24.12 → 23.99 (**−0.13**) | 33.91 → 31.13 (**−2.78**) |
| Italy | +0.09 | −0.01 |

The claimed figures were +13.0, +0.8, −0.9 and −20.6. On the author's own ratio France 777 is −0.97, and Italy +0.63 exceeds the stated 0.6.

**§599's premise, a direction that flips between seeds, is not met.** On the game's ratio:

| country | seed | turns with the same sign | mean over 100 turns |
|---|---|---|---|
| France | 777 | lower on 99 of 100 | −1.16 pts |
| France | 424242 | lower on 99 of 100 | −1.96 pts |
| Germany | 777 | higher on 94 of 100 | +0.66 pts |
| Germany | 424242 | higher on 95 of 100 | +0.19 pts |

**Turns 1–5 are identical across seeds to about three decimals, so this is deterministic:**
- France: −0.033, −0.045, −0.273/−0.275, −0.218/−0.217.
- Germany: −0.012, −0.017, −0.020, −0.116, then +0.098/+0.101.

This is a systematic effect of the statute calendar arriving about nine months earlier in game time. Only its t100 size depends on the seed. The attribution to "the AI ministry's threshold rule" is plausible for Germany (§596's mechanism), but no ministry-off probe measured it.

## 7. Save bump justified — CONFIRMED

The loader refuses any other version (`SaveGameService.cs:160`, `:320`). A v23 save would load wrong in two ways:
- **A v23 play-protocol save** (2029-05-07, turn 3) has 949 days since the new epoch. It would fire its election turn 3→4 on 2029-09-30. The campaign record's 2029-12-31 would never equal `TurnBoundary(4)` = 2030-09-30, so election night drops the campaign (`GameController.cs:6162`).
