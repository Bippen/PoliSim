**Findings**

1. **MEDIUM - CONFIRMED - the audit's HQ captures now draw the previous frame's snapshot.**
   - Where: AuditDriver.cs:1047-1067 (when `hqWasOpen`, it calls no OpenLiveCampaign and goes straight to PumpGui twice), together with AuditHarness.cs:221-247 (the pump runs before LateUpdate) and GameController.cs:2790-2799 (no refresh in the draw any more).
   - Failure: area2b_presented.plan's header says Sweden opens the HQ on day 182 and Germany on day 53. So `e1_se 200:HQ` and `e1_de 60:HQ` capture an HQ that is already open; the same applies to area10_ui.plan's `200:HQ`/`60:HQ` and area4b_created.plan's m2_* `200:HQ`.
     - At delta 0.25 and 3x, the frame's Update steps day 199→200, then the tick pumps the HQ built in the previous frame's LateUpdate.
     - The `e1_se_day200_HQ` label table therefore carries day 199's figures: "D TO POLLING DAY" one higher, and the chest, queue, poll and notes from the day before. Before the patch the in-draw refresh made it day 200's.
     - Area 2's presented-against-stored reading, and GA-4-4's HQ poll figure, would be off by a day with nothing in the log saying so.
     - The same skew can reach dry-film live-HQ frames while the clock runs (e7b, UiScreenshotDriver.cs:4723-4725); that is harmless there because the dry film only measures layout.
   - Fix: call the controller's own LateUpdate (`Call("LateUpdate")`, which is idempotent) immediately before every `PumpGui()`. That means AuditDriver.Tick:290, and in CapturePages before the open check at 1051 and the first pump at 1065. The pump then draws the frame the game would, including the desk on the drop frame.
