## Hunt answers

**1. Legal accuracy against the stored sources**
- **Quorum: correct.**
  - `PresidentialVeto.Quorum` = (460+1)/2 = 230, which is "co najmniej połowy ustawowej liczby posłów".
  - Every member is present, as every vote in this model reads.
  - `Carried` = Members ≥ 230 && For > Against.
- **"More for than against": correct.** The test is For > Against, and abstentions are not counted.
- **The 45 days: the window is right; the last Sunday is a choice.**
  - Art. 98 ust. 5: "nie później niż w ciągu 45 dni od dnia zarządzenia skrócenia kadencji Sejmu". Day 45 is the order + 45, the order's own day not counted. In 2007 that is Monday 22 Oct, and the record respects it.
  - The constitution requires only a day no later than day 45. KW art. 4 § 1 adds that it must be a day off work.
  - **The last Sunday is not required.** It is the 2007 practice, applied by ruling E2's principle. That is one precedent, and it was itself an Art. 98 ust. 3 self-shortening, under ust. 5 "odpowiednio".
  - The code says this in prose (SejmFormation.cs:218-224) but carries no DECLARED mark. The spec says "(the 2007 practice)" (L3).
- **Kodeks art. 195 § 1: the start day differs (L2).**
  - For a President's shortening, the Kodeks counts "od dnia ogłoszenia postanowienia Prezydenta Rzeczypospolitej o skróceniu kadencji Sejmu", which is promulgation, not the order.
  - The model counts from the order. That is the constitution's start, and the earlier one, so its day satisfies both texts.
- **The first sitting: correct.** Art. 98 ust. 5's second sentence gives "nie później niż na 15 dzień po dniu przeprowadzenia wyborów", and Art. 109 ust. 2 excepts ust. 3 and 5.
  - The code takes +15, the last day, DECLARED as E2 took Art. 109's.
  - The 6th Sejm sat on 5 Nov 2007, day 15 after 21 Oct. That is consistent, but not sourced on disk.
- **The new Sejm's own term: correct.** Art. 98 ust. 6 → ust. 1: four years from its first sitting.
  - `OrdinaryAfter` re-anchors on `FirstSundayOfTermWindow` of the +15 sitting.
  - The rule gives 9 Oct 2011 from a 5 Nov 2007 sitting, which is the record. The branch could add that as a check.
- **Art. 155's days: as the text reads.**
  - The President's 14 days run from the lapse of ust. 3's window: 28 days from a Sejm vote taken on its window's first day.
  - The confidence vote falls on day 14.
  - The shortening falls on the day of the refusal.
  - Art. 98 ust. 4 (the Marshals' opinions) and Art. 228 ust. 7 are stated as not modelled.
