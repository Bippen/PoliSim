# K274 - the levers of the AI USA's reaction: the decision rule, registered before the variants are measured (2026-10-29, review 1's M2 and M3)

Drafted by the implementer of `wave/k274` after review 1 (`Reviews/2026-10-29_k274_review1.md`) and committed on that branch BEFORE any lever variant below is run. The first prereg (`Reviews/2026-10-29_k274_prereg.md`) stands unchanged: its coefficient, its (A), its (B) and its band are not re-chosen here. The integrator commits this text into the plan beside the first, before its own run.

**What is measured.** As in the first prereg: `Tools/headless_hold/frf_play.sh <root> on none 100 <seed>` at seeds 777 and 424242, the USA's row each year. The coefficient stays Bohn (1998) Table I column (1), **0.054**. Three lever variants, each on its own source root:
- **(i) as built at 79520d8b** (K274c): a tightening cuts EVERY spending line by one uniform percentage, Social Security included, inside the ranges, and puts what the ranges leave on the household rates; a loosening lowers the rates the rule raised, then gives the lines back.
- **(ii) every line but Social Security, then the rates**: (i) with `SpendingCategory.SocialSecurity` left out of both the cut and the give-back (and out of the total the percentage is taken on). Ground: 2 U.S.C. 641(g), on disk (`ElectionsData/usa/raw/records/govinfo_uscode_2_641_reconciliation.html`), bars any reconciliation bill that "contains recommendations with respect to the old-age, survivors, and disability insurance program established under title II of the Social Security Act", and Social Security is not appropriated - so no budget act an AI president lays changes it; the game already holds the US pension line to a statute (MF-14, 42 U.S.C. 415(i)). The other mandatory lines move by reconciliation (direct spending, 641(a)), and the rates by reconciliation, so they stay levers.
- **(iii) discretionary lines only, then the rates**: the retired US rule's line scope (`67e9ae77`: `includeMandatory: false`) with the remainder put on the household rates - review 1's "discretionary-plus-rates" variant. Measured for Elias's ruling only.

**The decision rule, DECLARED before measurement.**
- **Code's recommendation is (ii)**, on the grounds above, fixed now. It is BUILT if it meets the first prereg's **(A)** (no US debt crisis in a century) at BOTH seeds; otherwise (i) stays built and (ii) is reported with its miss.
- (iii) is printed, never chosen by this measurement: a choice between (ii) and (iii) is Elias's (a strikable K-line).
- **(B) is judged as registered** - year 30 against CBO's 2056 figure, 175.076 %, band [150.076, 200.076] - for whichever variant is built. **Printed beside it, not judged:** the same year-30 level against CBO's 2055 figure, 171.785 %, band [146.785, 196.785] (the task's own wording names "CBO's 2055 figure"; the registered calendar rule - opening year 2026 plus 30 - reads 2056). The registered reading is not switched after any measurement.
- Printed for every variant, both seeds: the debt ratio every ten years, the crisis count and first crisis year, the peak, revenue and the primary balance at years 30 and 100.

**Not measured here.** The bill path (the player's USA under an AI head, review 1's M1) does not run in frf_play (its player always governs); its statement is held by `UsFiscalReactionDiagnostic` (e) in the engine, with no acceptance range to register.
