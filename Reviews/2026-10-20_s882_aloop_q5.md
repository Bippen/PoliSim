## H findings

**H-1. The adverse gate moves Economy salience on four favourable booms.** `EventSalience.cs:122-123` (`Moves`): `GdpShockPercent < 0 || InflationShockPoints > 0 || ApprovalEffect < 0`.
- Four booms carry a small positive inflation shock, so they count as "adverse" and jump Economy by +0.1026:
  - Consumer Confidence Surge (pool index 6: g +1.0, i +0.2, a +2)
  - Tourism Boom (18: g +1.0, i +0.1, a +2)
  - Stock Market Rally (23: g +0.6, i +0.1, a +1.5)
  - Export Boom in a Key Partner (27: g +1.2, i +0.2, a +2)
- This contradicts the declared reading in the same file (":810 a boom, a breakthrough or a bumper harvest raises none") and the class doc. That is 4 of the 25 Economy-moving events.
- The diagnostic cannot see it, because check (1a) computes its expectation with `EventSalience.Moves(e)` itself (EventSalienceDiagnostic.cs:113-114), so the test is tautological.
- **Fix:** drop the inflation clause. In today's pool the events adverse only by inflation are exactly these four booms; every real inflation shock also has g < 0 or a < 0. Or gate on the event's net sign. Then add an independent oracle, for example "no event with g >= 0 and a >= 0 moves anything".

## M findings

**M-1. The fitted jump is anchored at the post-wave, but the game lands it on the event day.**
- The tool measures J as the post-wave share minus the pre-wave share, and measures decay time from the post-wave's date (aloop_salience_fit.pl:484, `$dt += ... - $post{$_}[2]`). So the fitted curve reads "J at the post-wave, decaying after".
- The game lands J on the event day and starts decaying the next morning (EventSalience.cs `Land` plus `AdvanceDay`). At the date the survey actually measured J, the game holds only part of it:
  - Economy: war on 24 Feb 2022, EB97 midpoint 128 days later. Game 0.1026 x e^(-0.004425 x 128) = 0.058, about 57 % of the measured jump.
  - Security: event-to-post-wave gaps of 64, 118, 157, 48 and 84 days, mean about 94. That leaves about 47 % of J.
- Either set J0 = J x e^(lambda x Delta) per unit, with Delta measured from the event's date (both dates are in the header), or state the reading. Neither is done, and the header's units section does not mention it.

**M-2. Security is "generated" though the series does not resolve it, and the war event borrows the terror fit.**
- The jump's interval is -0.070..+0.186 and lambda's is -0.436..+0.452. One unit carries it: BE +0.24, the attack in its own capital, whose pre-wave straddles Paris. The other four units are -0.01, +0.01, +0.02 and +0.03.
- The generate rule (`jump > 0 && edge == 0`, :512) ignores the jump's interval. Health, whose interval excludes zero, is inert, while Security, whose interval spans zero, is live.
- Plan §6 (3) says a class without a resolving analogue is [AUTHORED-DRAFT] on the list.
- The terrorism item stands in for EB105's "security and defence" (a mismatch the header states). "War on the Doorstep" (Security; EventSystem.cs:445-454) therefore jumps Defense by the terror figure, while its analogue line cites the Economy item's rise, which is exactly the Economy class's own fitted event.
- Recommendation: either make Security inert or [AUTHORED-DRAFT], or require the jump's lower bound to be above 0 to generate. Retag or re-word the war event, and consider whether it should move Economy, since it carries i +1.0.

**M-3. On main, q24's persuasion normalisation cancels the shift in the general message.**
- The branch predates cb3facc3. A trial `git merge-tree` is textually clean, and all four staging sites still apply the shift in place before `Begin` or `WithRecordShift` (both pass the same array). `ElectorateMean` is computed on the fly, so it reads the shifted array. TrueSalience is therefore fed the same way.
- But under q24 a contact's effect is CACE x quality / norm (CampaignPersuasion.PerContactPp). The general message's relevance is mean(salience) x mean(match) over the contested issues, the same issue set for every party, and the norm is the prior-weighted mean of that.
- A shift therefore scales every party's general relevance and the norm by one common factor, and it cancels apart from Clamp01. The event reaches the campaign only through issue messages (raised issue up, the others down through the larger norm), the AI's issue choice (CampaignRun :1133-1157) and MeasureIssues.
- Diagnostic check (4b) checks only the array. After merging, re-run on the merged tree and add one check that a shift moves a campaign quantity (an issue message's effect, or the AI's chosen issue), or declare the reading that only relative effects survive.
- Coverage note: Sweden's wave measures Climate, Crime, Defense and Education, so only Security reaches Sweden's campaign. Germany's measures Economy and Immigration.

**M-4. No save step, against the ruling-A precedent.**
- `Country.SalienceShift` is additive (Country.cs:536-540). A v47 save cut by main's build loads with a null shift, while the new build's continuous run from the same start would hold one. The two runs then part, in the AI's bill votes and the campaign's relevance, for up to about a year.
- Steps 43-46 (TaxTermStock, VoteApprovalMarks, FiscalVoteRecord) and 42 ("conservative, not forced") were taken on exactly that argument.
- The stake is small, since the shift decays with a half-life of about 5 months. Either take the step or write the no-step argument into the CurrentSaveVersion comment, as US-8 did.

**M-5. The diagnostics are weak where it matters.**
- (1a) is tautological on `Moves`; see H-1.
- (1c) passes with an inert crime event: "Gang Violence Wave" moves nothing (Crime is not generated) while its analogue cites the measured +4.
- (4a) tests only Defense on Sweden.
- (4b) tests the array, not a campaign effect; see M-3.
- Nothing ties a fired event in a *played* run (the fuzz set) to the logged `SALIENCE:` line, as plan §6 (4)'s pass list asks.

**M-6. Every adverse economy event jumps by the Ukraine-war figure.**
- 25 Economy-tagged events, including minor ones such as Wage Round Overshoots (g -0.2) or Infrastructure Collapse, take the full +0.1026. That figure was fitted on the largest inflation shock in 40 years, with a pre-wave already in the 2021-22 inflation surge.
- For Sweden, Economy sits at the 0.10 floor, so one event doubles the opinion cost of cutting the SocialSecurity, IncomeSecurity, Labor, UBI and similar lines (StanceModel.cs LineBands and ProgramBands to IssueId.Economy).
- The plan declares this as an ask. It belongs on the asks list with a band-scaling option. The game is about 25/52 x 0.4, roughly 19 % of country-years.
