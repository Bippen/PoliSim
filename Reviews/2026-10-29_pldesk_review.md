## Findings

### High

**H1. `PolishDeskVerbsDiagnostic` is never run - it is not on the bar's table.** Assets/Editor/CheckSuite.cs:210 registers `CoalitionExitDiagnostic`; nothing registers `PolishDeskVerbsDiagnostic` (a repository-wide search finds only its own file and a doc comment in PolishDeskRecord.cs:12). Every claim the commit message rests on it - the gates' words, 231 carries and 230 fails, the install through the record, the Docket's month, the strip's one day, K196's four boundary days - is asserted by a check no bar executes. Failure scenario: a later change makes `CanMoveNoConfidence` drop the quiet-window test or `Needed` slip to the seated majority; every bar stays green. Read on its own merits, each part would fail while false (part 4 checks both the refusal at day 90 and the taking-up at day 91 on each side; part 2 checks For/Needed/Members and the title exactly), so the fix is one row in `CheckSuite` and a bar run showing its PASS line.

**H2. The film frames pl1-pl4 cannot reach on Poland's standard session, and their absence fails nothing.** `CapturePolishDeskVerbs` (UiScreenshotDriver.cs:2623) stages "the smallest junior partner" of the sitting cabinet. Poland's default start is `WorldClock.StartDate` → the pre-campaign start before 15 Oct 2023, where the government of record is Morawiecki's with cabinet `{ "PiS" }` alone (WorldClock.cs:522), and `CaptureDeskByRole` before it moves no day. So the staging finds no partner and logs "no junior partner to stage ... skipped, not missing" (UiScreenshotDriver.cs:2642) - in the default case, which is the only case the main sweep runs unless a later start is chosen. No pl frame exists under `PoliSim-captures`. The frames are also never `Expect(...)`-declared (cf. :3560, :6485), so TL-1 (`EndSweep`) cannot see them missing. The consequence is that the UI item's new surfaces - the constructive row in the Sejm's words, the LEAVE slip, the Docket's two items, the strip's chip - have no frame and no overflow/containment measurement, which CLAUDE.md's UI tier owes ("the dry film and one filmed width"). "Skipped, not missing" is not honest here: on the standard start it means "never filmed". Fix: stage the KO+TD+NL cabinet on a planted day outside the quiet window (as the diagnostic does), or run the frames on a start that has one, and declare pl1-pl4 with `Expect` so a skip fails the film.

### Medium

**M1. K196's quiet days on the PLAYER's Art. 158 motion are an unruled rule, recorded nowhere but a code comment, and they make the desk contradict itself.** K130 rules "an AI coalition partner leaves ... never within 90 days of a polling day"; K196 reads K130's days on both sides; K197 files the player's verbs as "a follow-on UI item". None of them restricts the player's motion, and Art. 158 has no such bar. The branch adds one (SimulationManager.PolishDesk.cs:71) and declares it only in the summary comment; docs/RULINGS_2026-10-23.md gains no K-line, against the standing instruction that each of Code's recommendations is recorded as a K-line Elias can strike. Practical weight: with the 2025 presidential rounds (18 May / 1 Jun) the player cannot move Art. 158 from about 17 Feb to 30 Aug 2025, and again from about 19 Jul 2027 before the 17 Oct 2027 Sejm election - roughly a sixth of the term. The asymmetry is the sharper defect: inside that window the player may still LEAVE (not held, by the same declaration), `ExitOpensConfidenceProcedure` → `TryAiMotion` then lets the AI's declared opposition move Art. 158 the same day (its only date guard is the week before a polling day, SimulationManager.CoalitionExit.cs:110), and the LEAVE slip promises "THE DECLARED OPPOSITION MAY MOVE ART. 158 THE SAME DAY" (ParliamentRows.cs:1281) - while the player, now in that opposition, is refused for 90 days. Germany's identical constructive vote is not held at all. Either record it as a K-line with this asymmetry stated (and decide whether the AI's post-exit motion is held too), or drop the test and leave the player's motion to Art. 158's own rules.

### Low

**L2. **L3. The film's staging restores the party key, not the government.** The `finally` (UiScreenshotDriver.cs:2683) puts `PlayerPartyAbbrev` back, but the partner's leaving, any AI successor installed by the exit's procedure, and the player's own motion stay in the world, and FORK-2's `Answered` records them as the player's answers under the staged key (an except-world replays `LeaveGovernment` with the film's own party and is refused there). Contained today because the frames are the sweep's last act before `EndSweep`; a frame later added after them would inherit a changed government. Worth a comment or a restore like :2120's.

**L4.** The Sejm's reason order: for a seatless Polish party inside the quiet window the reason given is the quiet days, not "YOUR PARTY HOLDS NO SEAT". Harmless, noted so nobody reads it as a regression.
