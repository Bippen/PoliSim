# Review - §829, the gameplay audit's Phase 4: GA-2-13, the income tax's yield memo keys everything the yield reads (2026-10-14)

A workflow ran the review (`review-ga213-memo-key`, run `wf_269c7068-b5b`, seven read-only agents): three reviewers read the change to `Assets/Scripts/Data/TaxSchedule.cs` - the memo's key, `TaxSchedule.OverridesKey`, the yield's exact rate key - each through one lens (the key's completeness; determinism and the no-policy sentinel; robustness, cost and the comments), and every finding went to its own skeptic, told to refute it if a concrete reason exists. The reports follow verbatim, as the workflow returned them; then what was done.

## The lens: the key's completeness (`key-completeness`)

**Summary.** The new key covers every input that can move during play. Price level and real wage are keyed by value, sub-row rates by their full content, and the lever rate as the full float. The pyramid is keyed by reference: every path that replaces it installs a freshly allocated array, and nothing anywhere in Assets writes into a live pyramid's elements. The other inputs are static (the statutes, the exchange rates, the earned income credit's constants), immutable after world creation (each cohort's income median and sigma), or read live outside the cached value (IncomeTaxSeedAer). The one input left unkeyed is the line's seed rate (TaxLine.RateSeed). It is fixed before the first memoised read and every caller carries the same value, so no wrong result is reachable today. But the new summary claims the key is "EVERYTHING THE YIELD READS that can move", and the seed rate falls outside that.

## The lens: determinism and the sentinel (`determinism`)

**Summary.** The fix is sound and makes the memo give the same figure as a fresh computation, whatever order the calls come in. The no-policy sentinel will not move. The real turn reads no income-tax yield between CohortDemographics.CommitYear and IndexSpendingLines. Every reader (revenue, household burden, Gini, participation) runs daily in AdvanceDay. The boundary's own tax step reads lever points, not the yield, and the AI ministry and the Finance partner read only TaxBases.Base. So in the dump the old and new memos reset at the same read (day 1, after the index has moved the level and wage) and produce the same figures. The one stale-pyramid read that really existed was display-only: the Budget page's preview of a sub-row draft, at a boundary-fresh state, on its own clone memo. The only finding is a low one: the new comment's wording says the stale read happened in the commit-to-index window, which is not where it happened.

**The sentinel, predicted.** It does NOT move. The 'ga111' digests hold for the first 20 turns at seeds 777 and 424242, and no baseline family is needed. The reason: between CommitYear and IndexSpendingLines the turn reads no yield, since every reader is a daily step in AdvanceDay. So before the fix the memo never served last year's pyramid to anything that writes state, and both versions reset at the same read (day 1 after each boundary, once the index has moved the price level and real wage). In this run the overrides key is always \"\" and each memo sees exactly one lever rate per generation, so the other two key changes are inert too. One residual case: a country whose price level and real wage were both bit-identical across a year (inflation held at its 0 floor all year and the real wage at MinRealWageIndex) would have carried the old memo across a commit. That is not reachable in 20 turns from the seeded states. This prediction is a static trace; the cheap bar's sentinel run will confirm it.
