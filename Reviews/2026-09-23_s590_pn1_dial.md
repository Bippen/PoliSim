# Adversarial review: PN-1's dial (§590), staged diff pn1d.diff

Read-only review. Line numbers are from the working tree at review time. GameController.cs is `MM` (it has unstaged edits on top of the staged diff, and its line numbers moved while I was reading), so GameController locations are given by function name first.

## DEFECTS

### D1. The Budget impact estimate does not change when only the pension dial moves (a missed hand-list)
- **Where:** `GameController.BudgetDraftSignature` (about line 10507), used as the cache key by `DrawBudgetDraftFiscalImpact` (about line 10473).
- **What:** the diff adds `PensionAgeSet`/`PensionAge` to `DraftFingerprint`, but not to `BudgetDraftSignature`. That second hand-list over `BudgetBill`'s fields carries its own warning: *"the moment a field is added to that class and not to this, the estimate silently stops updating for it."*
- **Scenario:** the player drags the pension age from 67 to 69 and touches nothing else. The signature does not change, so `EstimateBudgetBill` is not re-run and the "Revenue / Spending / Net" lines keep the previous figures (zero if nothing else is drafted). The estimate would have been non-zero: `EstimateBudgetBill` → `PreviewTurnOnClone` → `IndexSpendingLines` takes the driver step. If the player then touches any other draft, the estimate jumps and includes the pension step, so the change looks like it came from the other lever. The film's `_pension_dial_drafted` frame shows the same stale panel.
- **Fix:** append `draft.PensionAgeSet` and `draft.PensionAge.ToString("R", Invariant)` to `BudgetDraftSignature`.
- **Adjacent, already there before this diff:** `BudgetDraftSignature` also leaves out `BracketRates` (F4-4), so the same fault already applies to the bracket sub-rows.

### D2. France and Italy: the dial cannot reach the statute's own figure, so "return the statute" cannot be drafted and a stray drag cannot be undone
- **Where:** `LedgerRow.Draw` snaps a dragged value to `step = StepFor(unitsPerPixel) * grain` (LedgerRow.cs:313). With `grain = 1` and a track of at least 100 px, the step is 0.1 year. `DrawPensionAgeRow` then snaps to the month (`Mathf.Round(result*12)/12`).
- **Arithmetic:** starting from the 0.1-year grid, the month snap gives x.0, x.1→1 m, x.2→2 m, x.3→4 m, x.4→5 m, x.5→6 m, x.6→7 m, x.7→8 m, x.8→10 m, x.9→11 m. **Months 3 and 9 cannot be reached.** Below 100 px the step is 0.5 year, and only 0 m and 6 m can be reached.
- **Statutes affected** (PensionAgeStatute.cs:101-109): France 62 y 9 m (2026-27), 63 y 3 m (2029), 63 y 9 m (2031-32). Italy 67 y 3 m from 2028, carried with no end date after that (`DatedTo = 2028`). Germany's even months, the USA's 66 y 10 m, Sweden and Poland are all reachable.
- **Scenario A (undo impossible):** Italy in 2030 with an override of 66 standing. The player drags back towards the law's ghost mark. The nearest reachable values are 67 y 2 m and 67 y 4 m, each one month from 67.25. That is more than the half-month tolerance in `BuildBudgetBillFromDrafts`, so the bill carries a new frozen override instead of `-1`. The statute cannot be restored with the dial.
- **Scenario B (a phantom draft that cannot be cleared):** France in 2026, no override, the knob at rest on 62.75. Any drag lands on 62 y 8 m or 62 y 10 m, and the dial cannot land back on 62.75. The draft can therefore never clear itself ("a draft back on the standing figure is no draft"). There is no control that discards drafts. The next budget bill carries a one-month override that freezes France off its statutory path for good. Because of D3 the chamber does not resist it and it costs no approval.
- **Fix, either of:**
  - pass `grain: PensionAgeMonth` to `LedgerRow.Draw`. Its step becomes 1/120, 1/24 or 1/12 year, all of which reach every month after the month snap.
  - add a detent: if `|snapped - statuteAge| < PensionAgeMonth`, snap to `statuteAge` exactly (and do the same for the standing `age`).
- Also add a check that asserts every statute figure is reachable by the row's snap for every country and year.

### D3. The pension age is invisible to the chamber and to approval. Raising it to 70 passes for free.
- **Where:** `ParliamentSystem.GetBillDirection` (:166) and `GetBudgetBillConcern` (:878) do not read `PensionAgeSet`. `WouldBillPass(Country, BillConcern)` (:317) returns true for an empty concern. The approval hit in `ApplyBillResult` counts only tax hikes. `MandatorySpendingChangeThisTurn`, the approval channel for mandatory lines, carries only `ApplySpendingLineChanges`' dollar changes, never the index step.
- **Scenario:** a bill whose only change is Sweden's age from 67 to 70. The concern is empty, so it passes unconditionally ("a draft introduced unchanged asks the chamber for nothing") and approval does not move. At the next turn boundary the largest mandatory line falls by the driver step. By my estimate, which I did not run, that is about 5 % per year of age (a fifth of a five-year cohort band). Lowering the age to 60 is just as free.
- **Why this counts as a defect and not only as the deferred participation response:** DS-3 cites the carbon rate's precedent, and the carbon dial *is* weighed in the chamber (`standing.PointsOf(...)` in the tax loop). The pension dial is currently the only budget-bill lever that costs nothing politically.
- **Fix, or rule it as a stated limitation:** load the age move onto the transfer axes that `BudgetLineAxes(SocialSecurity)` already uses, with the implied percent change of the line (`Level(new)/Level(old) - 1`), and add a cut entry to `concern.Cuts` when the age rises.

## LIMITATIONS (real, but arguably by design; state them or rule on them)

### L1. The payment readout jumps the wrong way between passage and the next index
- **Where:** `PensionPayment.AverageBenefitPerYear` (PensionPayment.cs:52-58), drawn by `DrawPensionPaymentRow`.
- **Scenario:** the bill passes on day X (`AdvanceBudgetBillDay`). From that moment `heads` counts at the new age, but `LineBillions` does not change until the next 121-day turn boundary indexes the line. Raising the age therefore shows the benefit per pensioner and the replacement rate going **up** (for example 67→69 gives about 1/(1-0.10), roughly +11 %) until the boundary brings them back down. The player raised the age and the readout says pensions got more generous.
- **Fix:** read the heads at the age as of the last index (store it, or store it beside `DriverReference`), or state the lag on the row.

### L2. The row's NEXT projection leaves out a passed or drafted age step
- **Where:** `SpendingLine.ProjectNextYear` (SpendingLine.cs:54) carries `LastDriverRatio` forward.
- **Scenario:** after the age is raised, the pension line's NEXT still projects last turn's ratio. The step lands only at the boundary. The effects panel does show it, because the preview indexes the clone.
- **Fix:** when an override stands, project `Level(now)/DriverReference` instead of `LastDriverRatio`.

### L3. The override freezes the age for good, unlike the carbon precedent
- **Where:** `CarbonRateStatute.AdvanceYear` keeps stepping after a passed bill. The German schedule overwrites the bill's rate and Sweden's indexes it: "a passed bill's figure for the coming year wins".
- **What:** `Country.PensionAgeOverride` persists until another bill. The doc states this as a choice, but DS-3 says "on CarbonRateStatute's precedent". Worth a one-line ruling.
- **Related edge:** once the statute catches up with an override (for example Germany reaching 67 in 2031 under an override of 67), the dial cannot draft "return the statute", because the draft equals the standing figure and is dropped. The age stays frozen and the row says SET BY A PASSED BILL. The fix is a "return the statute" affordance: a click on the ghost or a small toggle that sets `PensionAge = -1` even when the two figures are equal.

### L4. "Return the statute" is decided at introduction and applied at passage
- **Where:** `BuildBudgetBillFromDrafts` decides the `-1` against `AgeInForce(id, CurrentDate.Year)` when the bill is introduced. Passage comes `DaysRemaining` later and may fall in the next calendar year.
- **Effect:** the `-1` then means "follow the law", which is probably what the player wants. A figure-valued bill carries the introduction year's number. Minor; worth stating.

### L5. The pension draft is the only Budget draft not saved with the UI layer
- **Where:** `RestoreUiDrafts` nulls `_pensionAgeInput`, and `UiDraftState` (SaveGame.cs:106-140) has no field for it. Every other nullable dial draft, including `SpendingNominalDrafts`, is in that layer.
- **Effect:** save and reload with a pension draft pending, and the draft is lost.
- **Fix:** add `public float? PensionAgeInput;` to `UiDraftState` and capture and restore it. The change is additive, so under ruling A no save-version bump is needed.

### L6. No check exercises the bill route
- **What:** `RangeCaptionCheck.PensionAgeSign` writes the field directly. Nothing tests any of these:
  - that `ApplyBillResult` sets the override;
  - the clamp;
  - that `-1` returns the statute;
  - the one-off index step at the boundary;
  - that the preview/estimate clone leaves the live country untouched;
  - that a non-default value survives a save round trip.
- `LeverLivenessCheck` enumerates `PolicyDecision` only, so this lever, which lives on `BudgetBill`, has no liveness guard. `PolicyImpactLedger.Families` has no family for it, so its effect cannot be attributed to a family and lands in the interaction line. I did not trace the forks end to end, but the forks are save round trips, so they carry the override, while the no-policy shadow does not.

## NITS

- **N1. Policy Web text wrong under an override:** PolicyWebRenderer.cs:1391-1395 prints "Between decisions the pension age moves as the law moves it: …", which is false once an override stands. Its comment ("the age reaches nothing in the model yet") has been stale since §520. Print the override's figure and "set by a passed bill" when `IsOverridden`.
- **N2. Stale doc:** `DrawPensionAgeRow`'s doc (the paragraph at about line 11843) still says "⚠ The row is NOT a lever … the override state … is drawn the day the driver gives the row a bill."
- **N3. Film pin mismatch:** under `_pensionRowYearForFilm`, `DrawPensionAgeRow` ignores the override (`overridden` is forced false), but `DrawPensionPaymentRow` still honours it through `AgeInForce(Country, year)`. The two rows can disagree in a pinned frame. Film-only today, because the driver nulls the pin before setting the override. Separately, the row rewrites `_pensionAgeInput` against the pinned year's age, so a live draft can be silently cleared by a pinned frame.
- **N4. Range caption bands anchored on 65:** the bands name 65 "Customary" for every country, so Sweden's standing 67 reads "Extended". The signs are correct against the neutral at 65 and the check passes; the wording is only cosmetic.

## AREAS CHECKED CLEAN (no finding)

- **Readers of the age (1):** every money reader now goes through `AgeInForce(Country, …)`:
  - `SpendingDrivers.Level` (StatutoryPensionAge);
  - `SpendingDrivers.ShortLabel`;
  - `PensionPayment.AverageBenefitPerYear`, and through it `ReplacementRate`.

  Nothing else reads the age:
  - `PensionersMillions` has two callers, both covered.
  - The labour force (PN-3) reads the 15+ population at the state's rate and never the age.
  - `FederalRetirement` and `Veterans` stay on 65+ by design.

  The `AgeInForce(CountryId, …)` calls that remain are intentional statute reads:
  - the row's law/ghost marks;
  - the bill's "statutes" test;
  - the film's `lawAge`;
  - `PensionAgeDiagnostic` and `PensionPaymentDiagnostic`, which run on fresh worlds where the override is -1, so they are unaffected.
  - The `SimulationManager` hit at :2752 is `SeedYear` only.
- **Driver ratio (2):** `DriverReference` is written only in `CaptureStructuralBases` (at the seed) and in `IndexSpendingLines` (SimulationManager.cs:4196-4198). It is persisted on `SpendingLine` and never re-seeded on load. A mid-turn override gives exactly one step at the next boundary (`Level(new age)/Level(old age at last index)`), with no double count. `SeedAmount *= factor` carries the step, so the seed band follows it. A pinned line absorbs the step (the reference advances before `continue`), which is consistent with what pinning means.
- **Save (3):** `Country` is serialized with Newtonsoft default public-field serialization. There is no contract resolver and no `DefaultValueHandling`, so the field round-trips, and an old save keeps the initializer's -1, which is the old behaviour. The same holds for `BudgetBill.PensionAgeSet`/`PensionAge` on a pending bill. Ruling A (additive) means no version bump. `LawCompositionDiagnostic.Snapshot` will now include the float but laws do not touch it. `LeverProbes` excludes it (the "Override" pattern). `PreviewParityDiagnostic.CloneAudit` will mark it and the clone copies it.
- **Preview leak (4):** `ClonePreviewCountry` copies a float value type, and `ApplyBillResult` on the clone writes the clone's field only. `PreviewTurnWithBudgetDraft` and `EstimateBudgetBill` leave the live country untouched. AI bills never set `PensionAgeSet`. A failed bill returns before the new line.
- **LedgerRow return value (5):** on Layout/Repaint `GUI.HorizontalSlider` returns the value passed in, and `Draw` returns `interactive ? result : draft`. No phantom draft appears when nobody touches the row. No statute lies outside 60-70 (range 62 y 9 m to 67 y 3 m), so the slider's drag clamp never fights the standing figure.
- **RangeCaptionCheck (6):** `PensionAgeSign` builds its own `WorldFactory.CreateDefault()` world (as `RetailInterventionSign` already does), puts the override back, and touches no static cache. `SpendingDrivers.Level`/`PensionersMillions` are pure reads of the cohorts. The drawn-dial enumeration finds the literal `DrawRangeCaption("Pension age", …)`. The band/sign table matches `NeutralBand` (65 → band 4, "Customary").

## What was done with each finding

- **D1 (the impact panel's cache key) - FIXED.** `BudgetDraftSignature` carries `PensionAgeSet` and `PensionAge`; the bracket schedule, missing from the same list since F4-4, is added with it.
- **D2 (months the dial could not reach) - FIXED.** The row passes `grain: PensionAgeMonth` to `LedgerRow.Draw`, so the drag snaps in months (half-months where the track is wide, then the row's own month snap): France's 62 y 9 m and Italy's 67 y 3 m are reachable, and a draft back on the law's figure returns the statute (-1).
- **D3 (the chamber read no pension move) - FIXED.** `ParliamentSystem.SpendingPercentChangesOf` yields the bill's age as the pension line's implied change - the ratio of the two headcounts at or above the ages, the driver's own arithmetic - so the vote, the line's axes and the cut-exposure path weigh it as the same change asked as a percentage. The approval cost counts tax rises only, for every spending move; left as it is and stated.
- **Limitations - STATED in §590 and PN-1's row, not built:** the payment readout's one-boundary jump; `ProjectNextYear` without the age step; the override freezes the age (the carbon precedent keeps stepping); the return-to-statute choice read at introduction, applied at passage; the draft not in `UiDraftState`; no check exercises the bill route (a named gap).
- **Nits:** the Policy Web sentence now says when a bill's age stands (FIXED); the row's doc says it is a lever (FIXED); the film year pin reads the statute on the age row only (the film's own device, stated); 65 as "Customary" in every country - the catalog's neutral is the track's middle by the family's rule, stated.
