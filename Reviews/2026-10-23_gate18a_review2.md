## What was verified

**1. The count.**
- **At σ 0 with no campaign it returns the shares handed.** Probe, Sweden's 29 valkretsar on the 2026 returns: the worst |counted − handed| is 1.26e-7 of a share, which is a vote's rounding per region. It can move a seat only at an exact divisor tie. Germany has the same form. Poland returns the handed shares exactly, by construction (L1).
- **The noise draws on no counted stream.** It is drawn on `new System.Random(SimulationRandom.RegionalElectionNoiseSeedOn(country, day))`, a pure hash of `MasterSeed`, a salt, the country and the day (`ParliamentaryCount.cs:142-148`). `MasterSeed` rides the save (`SaveGameService.cs:148,307`).
- **Forks and the shadow world count like the played world.** The count is pure but for the readout, and `RunPlayerElection` puts a counterfactual's readout back (`KeepRegionalReadout`). Identical inputs therefore give identical counts in every world.
- **The noise form's small-party bias** is quantified under M2.

**2. The CACE unit.**
- **The stored PDF says what the constants say.**
  - p. 16: "the fraction of opened doors, respectively 45.5% and 35.9%".
  - p. 17: "CACEs of, respectively, 0.018/0.359 = 5.01%".
  - Table 4 col. 1 (p. 29): "0.018 *" with "(0.008)", and "Mean dep. var. 0.747".
- **The upper bound is right.** (0.018 + 1.96 × 0.008) / 0.359 = 9.3816 points.
- **The game's "door contact" is read consistently as an opened door.**
  - The 5b effect applies the CACE to `RegionalMobilization.DoorContacts`.
  - Those contacts are `GotvModel.Contacts` at 0.25 volunteer-hours each.
  - [CP20] p. 13 measures 7.2 minutes per opened door, travel included, by canvassers working in pairs. That is about 14.4 person-minutes, or 0.24 person-hours, per opened door.
  - So the price is a price per opened door, not per knock. The ~2.8× misread is not present.
  - 5a's persuasion also reads these contacts at a per-contacted CACE ([KB18]), so the two halves share one unit.
- **Probe:** doors equal to 20 % of C's supporters in every valkrets (113 023 doors) move C by +0.0705 points. The same by hand is +0.0705. The bound is 0.1419. Read as knocks (the ITT), the move would be 0.0253.

### L

- **L1. Check (3) for Poland cannot fail.**
  - Where: `ParliamentaryCount.cs:148,159`; the check at `GroundGameCountDiagnostic.cs:151-152`.
  - At σ 0 with no doors, `counted` and `still` are the same call, so Poland's shares come back as `national` exactly.

  - Fix: label the check as an identity, or hold the okregi's vote-weighted sum against the handed shares separately.

- **L2. Poland's counted shares are not renormalised after `Math.Max(0, …)`.**
  - Where: `ParliamentaryCount.cs:159`.
  - When a list's handed share plus its noise move goes below 0, the shares sum above 1.
  - `PolishSejmAllocation.Admitted` then tests the 5 % and 8 % lines on shares that do not sum to 1.
  - It is rare because a tiny list's move is biased upward (M2), but it is unguarded.

- **L3. Poland's base turnout of 1 would silently zero any Polish door.**
  - Where: `ParliamentaryCount.cs:118`, with the clamp in `GotvTurnout.cs:104`.
  - A reached supporter's turnout of 1 + CACE clamps at 1, so doors would add nothing. `HighestPartyTurnout = NaN` (`:174`) hides this.
  - It is latent, because no Polish campaign is staged. A future Polish staging must carry an electorate first.

- **L4. The unit is right but the words disagree.**
  - Where: `GotvTurnout.cs:17-19` declares a contact to be an opened door. But `GotvModel.cs` (the class head "doors knocked", the `DoorContacts` summary "the doors a party's operation has knocked") and `CampaignAi.cs` (the `OwnDoorContactsByRegion` summary "knocked so far") still say knocked.
  - Also, [CP20]'s CACE is per voter in a household whose door opened, while the game counts one door as one supporter. Relative to the source, the game understates the effect by about the number of voters per household. It does not inflate it.
  - Suggest one sentence in `GotvModel` matching `GotvTurnout`'s declaration, with the cost-parity argument (0.25 h against 2 × 7.2 min).

- **L5. Check (5) cannot detect the AI drifting from the count.**
  - Where: `GroundGameCountDiagnostic.cs:239-245`.
  - It compares `DoorTurnoutValuePp` with a re-typing of `ShareValuePp`, never with the count's own move.
  - The probe's −0.6 % to +12 % gap is the real figure. A check against `ParliamentaryCount` with the doors added would hold the claim "the count's own".

- **L6. A party's noise draw is tied to its position in the key order.**
  - Where: `ParliamentaryCount.cs:98-101` and the loop in `ElectionDay.Count`.
  - Each party gets its draw by its position in `shares.Keys` (Dictionary insertion order), not by its key. A played world (the campaign's key order) and its shadow (the prediction's) share the draws only because the two orders coincide today (`LiveCampaignSetup` builds on `TryCompatibility`).
  - Failure scenario: an entrant appended in a different position in one world gives the two worlds different noise for the same party, and the impact ledger reads noise as policy.
  - Sorting the keys, as Poland already does, would remove the dependence. Sorting changes the digest.

- **L7. The diagnostic never tests "consumes no counted stream".**
  - The claim is in the class head and the review. It is true by reading, but no check compares `SimulationRandom` draw counts before and after a count.

- **L8. `EnthusiasmValue` now prices a share point.**
  - Where: `CampaignAi.cs:830`.
  - This [AUTHORED-DRAFT] per-personality weight was a turnout-attribute point against a persuasion point. Now it multiplies a door's share points, so the same share point is worth 0.4× (Establishment) to 1.6× (Grassroots) depending on the channel.
  - Declared, but it is a reinterpretation of the weight's unit. It should be on the calibration list.

- **L9. The 56 comment reads as if a v55 save outside a campaign loads.**
  - Where: `SaveGameService.cs:104`. It says "A v55 save cut after its polling day loads to the chamber that count seated".
  - `Load` refuses every v55 save (`:219`). The sentence argues the case for that save; it does not describe what happens to it. Suggest "would load".
  - K8 names saves cut inside a campaign. The blanket refusal is ruling A's standing practice and is acceptable.
