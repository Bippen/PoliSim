**N2 (the invariant) - ANSWERED.** `AuditInvariants.cs` 418-430 now holds the identity `net == seedResidual`, with a tolerance of 1e-6 x max(1, |seed|, |chain|).
- *Float noise.* Where no link binds, `net` is `c + (s - c)` or `c - (c - s)`, which is two roundings. The worst error is about 2 ulp of max(|c|, |s|): about 2e-12 MW at 5000 MW, against a tolerance of 5e-3. That is nine orders of headroom. No other zone's `UnservedMw` is non-zero in the no-binding branch, so the sum adds nothing. It cannot false-fire.
- *Sign and size errors.* If `ChainCurtailedMw` is written as `gap` (the wrong sign), the miss is `2(c - s)`. If it is not written, the miss is `c - s`. For a 1 MW surplus at |c| ~ 1000 MW, the miss is 1-2 MW against a tolerance of 1e-3. A wrong-signed or missing `UnservedMw` booking fires the same way. It no longer misses a sign error.
