Surrounding code read: ShadowBaseline.cs (whole), PolicyImpactLedger.cs (AdvanceTurn / BeforeLever / AfterLever / LinesFor), SimulationManager (AdvanceCountryDayTick, AdvanceDay tail, AdvanceTurn top, TryOpenBudgetProcess, TableGovernmentBudget, PlayerGoverns, PartnerStepsAtBoundary, TryRollForeignPolicyMeeting, DaysIntoTurn), SaveGameService (CreateSaveGame, Deserialize, RestoreInto), GameController (Start, Update day loop, AdvanceTurn, SaveToPath, CarryTheShadow, CarriedOrForkedShadow, ForkTheShadowAtTheStart, RestoreFromSave, OpenWorldAt, SelectPlayerCountry, CheckElection), GameController.Statistics (shadow graph and the "Your policies" card), CreatedParties.TryRegister/Clear, UsVeto/UsLapseDraw counters, both new diagnostics, and PlayerLeverLedgerDiagnostic.

## Verdict: SHIP WITH FIXES

FORK-1 does what J8 asks, and I found no leak into the played world. The fork's `AdvanceCountryDayTick` runs inside the generator swap. The veto and lapse draws are keyed, so the UsVeto/UsLapseDraw counters only count; they don't select a draw. I found no static that the tick writes and the played world then reads. EnergyMarket's turn state is read only at the boundary, and the boundary sets it at its top. The order inside the loop (day, then tick, then levers due that day) matches `GameController.Update` plus a lever pulled from OnGUI.

AF-12's mechanics also look right. Carry and FromCarried swap the generator correctly, the per-date cache cannot go stale for the no-policy world, and the start-fork fires only at turn 0 on the epoch date. A re-seat during a running game and a load both bypass it correctly.

There is one blocking problem, H1, and it comes from how the two items combine. AF-12 now seats the player in the no-policy world, but that world still runs no day tick. Several sim paths are gated on the player's country, and for that country they now run nothing at all. "Without your policies" therefore stops being "no changes": for a player in opposition, the AI government never lays a budget in it. Fix H1 and stage the CheckSuite registration (M3) before committing. The rest can follow.

## H1. The AF-12 no-policy world seats the player but runs no day tick, so the player's country gets no budget, no funding horizon and no Speaker's round in it

**Where:**
- `Assets/Scripts/UI/GameController.cs:900` (`ForkTheShadowAtTheStart`: `new ShadowBaseline(_simulationManager, _world, PlayerCountryId, insideTurn: true)`, no `dayTick`).
- `GameController.cs:888` (the load fallback, the same call).
- `Assets/Scripts/Simulation/ShadowBaseline.cs:278-298` (the carried constructor never sets `_dayTickFor`, so a restored world never ticks).
- `Assets/Scripts/Simulation/UsLapse.cs:51` ("The no-policy shadow runs no day tick; FORK-1 left it as it was").

**Mechanism, verified in the code.** Before AF-12 the game's shadow was `new ShadowBaseline(masterSeed)`, with `PlayerCountryId == null`. Every player-gated path was inert there, and the player's country was governed like any AI country. `SimulationManager.AdvanceTurn` sets `aiGovernment = true`, and `ministryByBill` (`SimulationManager.cs:5837`) is false, so `AiFinanceMinistry.Apply` writes the book directly at every boundary.

After AF-12 the shadow is a fork whose `_sim.PlayerCountryId` is the player's country (restored by `RestoreInto`). For a player whose party does not lead the government (the US DEM player of 2025, or any opposition pick in the party picker), `ministryByBill` is now **true**. The ministry's rule then reaches the book only as the government's budget bill. That bill is tabled by `TryOpenBudgetProcess` (`SimulationManager.cs:3122-3130`) and adopted by `AdvanceBudgetBillDay`, and both run only from `AdvanceCountryDayTick` (`:342-357`). The shadow never calls that.

So in the AF-12 world, the AI government of the player's country never lays or passes a budget, for the whole game. The same applies to everything else in the tick for that country:
- the US funding horizon and lapse (`AdvanceUsFundingDay`);
- the Speaker's round and confidence days (`AdvanceConfidenceDay`);
- Poland's partner tax act, tabled at the boundary (`PartnerTaxAct`) and resolved through the law-bill countdown;
- every pending bill's countdown.

**What the player sees:**
- The "without your policies" line on every economy graph (`GameController.Statistics.cs:394`) drifts away from a played world in which the player did nothing. On debt and spending the drift is large and grows every year.
- In the impact ledger, `divergence = real − none` with a non-ticking `none`, while since FORK-1 every except-world ticks. The whole tick-driven gap (the AI's budgets, a lapse, the Speaker's round) cancels out of every family's line and lands on the INTERACTION line (`PolicyImpactLedger.cs:231`). The ledger labels that line "the part that belongs to no single dial", but here it is not caused by any policy.
- So J8 fixed attribution inside the families, and AF-12 moved the same error into the headline divergence.

**The repo's own evidence.** `ForkDayTickDiagnostic` case 2 *asserts* that a fork without the tick, taken from Sweden's start, differs from the played world (`seC.Fork != seC.Real`, ForkDayTickDiagnostic.cs:145). The AF-12 shadow is exactly that kind of fork, taken one day earlier.

**Why the diagnostics miss it.** `NoPolicyShadowCarryDiagnostic` runs no day tick in its played world (it says so at lines 34-36) and uses a *governing* M player. Neither the opposition branch nor the tick is exercised, and its "no changes" check (item 2) holds for any shadow, because a shadow is never handed a decision. `ForkDayTickDiagnostic` builds its `None` from the seed (line 246), not as AF-12 builds it.

**Fix:**
1. Pass `dayTick: true` at GameController.cs:900 and at :888.
2. In the carried constructor, set `_dayTickFor = _sim.PlayerCountryId`. The field is readonly, so assign it in that constructor; the carried world is always the game's no-policy world.
3. Correct UsLapse.cs:51 and ShadowBaseline.cs:63-67 ("Null for the no-policy world"), which will then be wrong.
4. Add a case to `NoPolicyShadowCarryDiagnostic` that runs the played world's tick with an *opposition* player and no player act, and asserts that the shadow equals the played world before the first polling day. That is the done-when a reader assumes q12 already meets.

If Elias reads "governing with no changes" as excluding the AI government's budget, this needs his ruling. Even then, the built state matches neither reading: it is not the AI-governed world from before AF-12, and it is not the played world's own calendar.

## M1. After a load, the ledger's family lines count from the load while its divergence counts from the start

**Where:** `GameController.cs:962-966` (the ledger rebuilt empty around the carried `_none`); `PolicyImpactLedger.cs:237` (`HasAnything => _except.Count > 0`); `GameController.Statistics.cs:518`.

**Scenario:**
1. The player raises income tax in turn 1, saves in turn 5 and loads.
2. `_none` is now the carried world from the start, so the divergence includes the turn-1 tax rise.
3. `_except` is empty, so the "Your policies" card shows "—" while every graph shows the full gap.
4. When the player next touches Taxes, the fork is taken from the loaded game, which already contains the turn-1 rise. Taxes gets only the change since the load, and the effect of the turn-1 rise moves to INTERACTION.

Before AF-12, both sides counted from the load, so the ledger was consistent even though q12 now rules that wrong. AF-12 carried one side only.

**Fix:** either carry the except-worlds too, or show the split on the card. Carrying needs a place in the save for the levers they have scheduled but not yet applied, because `Carry` throws while `_scheduled.Count > 0`. The alternative is a line on the card ("the families since the load of …; INTERACTION carries what came before"), which is Elias's call. At minimum, state the limitation in the AF-12 notes; today nothing mentions it.

## M2. FORK-1's "a fork with no policy difference equals the played world" holds only while the player answers nothing; ticking forks now leave a foreign-policy meeting pending for good

**Where:** `ShadowBaseline.cs:176`, through `AdvanceCountryDayTick` → `TryRollForeignPolicyMeeting` (`SimulationManager.cs:1243-1255`, a single slot).

**Scenario:**
1. A governing player holds Foreign Affairs.
2. The played world rolls a meeting, and the clock holds until the player answers. `ResolveForeignPolicyMeeting` applies the option's approval and debt and clears the slot, so the daily rolls (ForeignPolicy stream draws) resume.
3. Every ticking fork rolls the same meeting on the same day, but nobody answers it there. Its slot stays full, so it never rolls again: its ForeignPolicy stream stops and every later meeting's effects are missing.
4. From the first answered meeting on, every except-world differs from the played world, and the difference is booked to every touched family. This is the M4 shape J8 set out to close, with a different trigger.

Before FORK-1, forks rolled no meetings at all, so this is not a regression. It is the remainder the diagnostic names (ForkDayTickDiagnostic.cs:47-49). But the diagnostic's harness `Day()` (lines 253-274) never holds the clock the way `Update` does, so its "played world" is not the game's in exactly this case. The commit's claim is broader than what it proves.

**Fix:** treat a player's answers (a meeting, a cabinet decision, a withhold or release) as AF-11 `PlayerLever`s replayed in every except-world, or at least clear the fork's pending slot on the played world's resolve day. File it as FORK-1b with this scenario.

## L1. A failed or set-aside carried restore leaves process globals from the carried save, and leaks its host

**Where:** `ShadowBaseline.cs:278-298` and `GameController.cs:873-886`.

**Mechanism:** `RestoreInto` writes globals before any check can refuse the carried world: `CreatedParties.Clear()` and re-register, `OwnNominees`, and `SimulationManager.SetEpoch(...)`. The check that sets it aside (the seat comparison, `carried.PlayerCountryId == save.PlayerCountryId`) runs only *after* the restore. If `RestoreSaveState` throws, the constructor throws after `_host` was created, `carried` stays null, and the hidden GameObject is never destroyed.

In normal play the carried values equal the save's, so nothing visible happens. It matters only for a corrupt or spliced save, which is exactly the case this path exists for.

**Fix:** validate the deserialized `SaveGame` before `RestoreInto`: `PlayerCountryId`, `EpochDate == save.EpochDate` and `CurrentTurn == save.CurrentTurn`, which also covers L2. Destroy `_host` in a catch.

## L2. The carried world's turn and epoch are never checked against the save

**Where:** `GameController.cs:881`. Only the seat is compared. A carried world at another turn would be adopted and would advance one turn per boundary, offset from the played world for the rest of the game, with the graphs misaligned. A one-line check costs nothing; fold it into L1's validation.

## L3. Saves roughly double in size, and the first save of each turn serializes a whole world on the main thread

**Where:** `ShadowBaseline.Carry`, GameController.cs:838.

**Detail:** the world is stored as a JSON *string* inside an indented JSON document, so every `"` is escaped and the parse runs twice on load. The per-turn cache (`_carriedOn`) is correct, but an autosave right after a boundary always pays the full serialization. The diagnostic measures the time but not the hitch at the boundary.

**Fix:** consider embedding the snapshot as an object (`JRaw` or a nested `SaveGame` with `[JsonProperty]`) instead of a string. Not blocking.

## L4. Counterfactual worlds now print the player's log lines

**Detail:** with the player seated in the shadow (AF-12), `AdvanceDay` runs `AdvanceCampaign`, `HoldPresidentialRound` and `HoldUsPresidentialCount` there. "CAMPAIGN:", "RECORD:" and "PRESIDENTIAL: USA - …" therefore print a second time, in a burst after each boundary, carrying the counterfactual's own figures. With FORK-1, every except-world also prints "BUDGET:" and "ROLE:" lines.

**Who this affects:** listeners such as `UsPresidentialElectionDiagnostic.cs:309` count `== 1` and parse the first match. They don't build a controller today, so they are unaffected, but any film or harness that greps the play log will meet the duplicates.

**Fix:** a `SimulationManager.Quiet` flag set on counterfactual managers, or a "[shadow]" prefix on their lines.

## L5. `NoPolicyShadowCarryDiagnostic` has weak spots

- Its identity fingerprint (lines 292-307) reads only EconomyState floats, tax lines and the policy rate. It does not read the government, pending bills, the campaign or each world's streams, so a carried world that lost manager state still passes over two turns.
- It never asserts that the *played* world is unchanged by `Carry`, the start-fork or the load. There is no draw-count or fingerprint comparison against a run without the shadow, which is the "played world byte-identical" property.
- Its cost verdict is a single wall-clock sample per side and gates the bar (line 222). The margin is probably wide at turn 4, but a cold JIT or a busy machine can flip it. Suggest the median of three samples, or making the verdict a report and the identity checks the gate.

## L6. `_dayTickFor` reads `realSim.PlayerCountryId` while the fork seats the `playerCountryId` argument

**Where:** `ShadowBaseline.cs:125` against `:130`. In the game these are equal: `SelectPlayerCountry` and `RestoreFromSave` set both, and the ledger passes `_playerCountryId` from the same source. In a harness that passes a player without setting it on the manager, the fork seats one country and ticks none, or another. Use the argument, or assert the two are equal when `dayTick` is set.

## Checked and clean

- **The generator swap.** `Carry` and the carried constructor follow GA-1-3: `CaptureLive` before, `Reinstate(real)` in `finally`, and `_own` taken only once swapped. The real save is made *before* `Carry` in `SaveToPath`, so its draw counts are the played world's.
- **The cache.** It cannot go stale for the no-policy world. Its date moves only in `AdvanceTurn`, it is never scheduled a lever (`AfterLever` skips it), and every new shadow is a new object. The created parties and own nominee inside it are pre-game choices (`CommitPartyCreation` and the nominee flow run only from the picker).
- **When the start-fork fires.** It fires once per selection, at turn 0 on the epoch date, after every seat is taken (`GameController.cs:2194`). It does not fire on a load, on a mid-film re-seat (turn > 0, or the date past the epoch), or in a running game. Removing the seeded shadow from `OpenWorldAt` changes nothing in the played world: WorldFactory draws nothing, and the `EnergyMarket.ResetTurnState` it repeated had already run.
- **The counters.** `UsVeto` and `UsLapseDraw` only count (the index is keyed), so the extra counts from forks are harmless outside diagnostics, which reset them.
- **Day order inside the turn.** A fork made at the boundary has `DaysIntoTurn == DaysPerTurn` and runs no day. A lever's fork starts after day k's tick and the lever, and runs from k+1. Both match `Update`.
- **`ForkDayTickDiagnostic` has teeth.** Each case has a non-ticking control that must differ, and the played world is compared across both runs. A tick leaking into the played world would fail `usT.Real == usC.Real`.
