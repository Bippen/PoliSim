## 1. The check (EnergyReliability.cs 127-172, SimulationManager.cs 4068)

**Precedence.** `mw > 0 ? A : B ?? C` parses as `mw > 0 ? A : (B ?? C)`, so builds still go through `CannotPlaceWhy` only. A retirement goes through `CannotOrderWhy`, then `RefusalFor`. `RefusalFor` returns null for `mw >= 0`, for a label the queue refuses, and for nothing left to retire. **No build or non-retirement is ever refused.**

**Correctness.** `retire` is `EnergyFleet.Place`'s own cap. A fossil label meets the ministry's closed form, `AiEnergyMinistry.PeakBlockSpareMw` (private to public, no other change), with the queue's pending coal and gas retirements taken off the flexible fleet. Every label then meets the trial clearing. "Without" lands every queued retirement and every landed order, with queued builds not credited. "With" adds the order. Steps pressed one at a time are each tested against the queue the earlier presses built, so splitting a retirement into steps cannot get round the check. Demand (`DispatchDemandMw`, `ZoneConsumptionBlockMw`) is static and builds are never credited. An order that passes today therefore cannot start leaving load unserved later, apart from the hydro-shift bisection's price dependence, which is negligible.

**Side effects.** `TrialUnservedMw` copies the orders (`o.Clone()`, `Landed` set only on the copies) onto a stand-in `Country` and clears inside `EnergyFleet.For(stand)`. The `Ambient` stack is pushed and popped by `using`. The clearing reads the turn's water value and deficit share and writes neither. The only state it can write is lazily filled caches: `Adders`, `SeedSpread`, `WaterValueAtSeed` and the new `SwedenSeedUnbalanceMw`. Each is computed under `RecordOnly` at the seed, so the cached value does not depend on when it is first asked for. The re-entrancy flag returns null to the reference's own clearing (gap 0 there), and `ProbeLinkCapacityScale` is saved and restored in a `finally`. No RNG stream is touched. **It is deterministic and has no side effects.**

**Cost.** One press costs one `Clear` (the closed form) plus two trial clearings. Each clearing is three blocks, or four zones for Sweden, plus up to `ShiftRounds` hydro bisections. That is microseconds to a millisecond, paid on a click or once a day per label in the audit driver.

**Other paths.** `EnergyFleet.Place` is called directly in three places. `AiEnergyMinistry` 250 is not covered by the ruling, and `Live = false` anyway. `AuditDriver.Styles.cs` 419 and `UiScreenshotDriver` 835/853 place builds only. The audit driver's retirements now press the verb (408). The impact ledger's except-world replays re-run `EnergyFleet.Place` without the check, but they replay an order the check already accepted. A save holds only orders that were accepted, and a v66 save is refused. **No bypass found.**

## 2. The output channel

**Units.** `UnservedGwh` sums each block's MW over its hours / 1000 to get GWh. GWh x EUR/kWh = 10^6 EUR, so `/ MillionsPerBillion` gives billions of EUR, and `x UsdPerMarketCurrency` (1.0813 for the five, 1 for the USA) gives billions of book USD. The VoLL is carried to 2023 by each row's own index, so the result is at the seed's prices, as `EconomyState.GDP` is. `OutputVollPerKwh` throws if a row's currency is not the market's. **The units check out.**

**Timing.** `SetYear` runs once per boundary inside `EnergyLedger.AdvanceYear` (`DaysPerTurn = 365`). The no-layer branch zeroes the rate, and when the clearing has no unserved load the rate goes back to 0 at the next boundary. The daily overlay is a level, not a flow: the annual rate is taken off the annual-rate GDP and put back the next morning, so nothing accumulates. The order against `UsLapse` is LIFO: Restore runs energy then lapse, Apply runs lapse then energy, Lift runs energy then lapse, and Lower runs lapse then energy (6877-6928). The restore is float-exact: `wedge = before - after` is exact by Sterbenz while `loss < before/2`, so `after + wedge == before`. The rate and the wedge are separate fields, so a rate changed at the boundary leaves yesterday's wedge exact. **There is no double count, and the overlay comes off exactly.**

**Saves and the preview.** Both fields are public on `Country` and travel with the wholesale serialisation. The load refuses any version other than 67 (`SaveGameService` 230, 421). `ClonePreviewCountry` carries both. Like the lapse, the preview never runs the daily overlay.

## 4. q27 ClearSweden (EnergyMarket.cs 532, 570, 575-585, 606-622)

The reference and a turn on the record's fleet go through the same code. `Scale` is `(record + 0)/record`, which is exactly 1.0, and `RecordOnly` gives Delta 0. So the gap at the seed is exactly 0.0, and **rounding cannot make the control run non-zero.** Diagnostic (e) asserts this to the bit. A landed build and an equal retirement cancel exactly in `LandedMw`. Booking: a deficit-binding link books `-cumulative - cap` at zone k and raises the running sum by the same amount. The end gap is then the seed balance less the raised sum. The total unserved is `seed - (sum of the four zones)`, so nothing is counted twice. `UnservedGwh` reads the same `UnservedMw` fields for every country. The trial uses `ClearAt` at the country's level and probe, the same path the boundary takes.

## 5. The diagnostic

Only the currency and price-year shape tests have teeth, and the generator's own run (§3) is what actually ties the data to the sources. (b) would fail on the base, where nothing is refused. It tests the wiring by comparing the verb's sentence with `RefusalFor`'s, which is legitimate. (c) would fail on the base. **M2 (Medium) - The euro bridge overstates the non-euro countries, Sweden by about 23 %.** `EnergyReliability.cs` 58 and declared reading (iii) at 33. It is declared, but THREE UNITS asks for the conversion to go through the national unit: EUR(2015) to national at the 2015 rate, national HICP, then the 2023 rate (the layer already holds ECB 2023). This needs two sourced 2015 rates.

**M3 (Medium) - The clearing test goes further than "the AI ministry's reliability check", and its consequences are not on record as K-lines.** `EnergyReliability.cs` 143-147 and 21-24. The ministry's check is the fossil peak-block closed form. The added test refuses any label, in any block, at a tolerance of 1e-6 MW. Two consequences. (ii) Sweden's demand includes the exogenous exports (`ZoneExternalExportMw`), its fossil is not dispatched and its hydro is fixed, so every MW of Swedish nuclear, wind or solar is refused until builds land. Diagnostic (e) asserts this. In reality the first MWs retired cut exports, not domestic load. These are defensible readings of "the veto errs toward the lights" and of q27, but they are Elias's to strike. Like the other readings in §7, they need K-lines at integration; the only "ask" written down is in `fetch_log.md`.

## 7. The declared readings

The household VoLL is excluded from GDP. That is defensible: CEPA's domestic figure values lost leisure, and the non-domestic figure is value added per kWh. The data carry it. The USA's small C&I is excluded. That is defensible but conservative: $258/kWh against $12.7, applied to every non-household kWh. It needs a K-line, since K145 names the ICE without choosing a class. Sweden's reference is the seed balance. That is the only reference under which the control run stays exactly unchanged, and it is consistent with q27. See M3 (ii) for its consequence. Five countries have no sourced statute and say so on the sentence, which is honest. The EnWG text is stored byte-exact, and the reading of it (system-relevant means unserved somewhere) is declared. None of these contradicts a ruling. M3 asks only that they be recorded where Elias can strike them.

H1 is a one-line registration, and the q26/q27 core is otherwise sound: the refusal logic, the side-effect-free trial, the units, the overlay order, the sources and the q27 booking all hold. Register the diagnostic, then fix M1 or declare it (with the invariant). M2 and M3 can go as K-lines or a follow-on if Elias prefers.
