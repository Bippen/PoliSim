### Medium

**M1. USA (c) 2055 passes by 0.02 pts headless, and K290 could not register it if the engine tips it over.**
- **Where:** `Assets/Editor/ParticipationCohortDiagnostic.cs:391` (the `Band` call) and `:384` (`attributedToPopulation`).
- **The figure.** Headless, USA 2055 reads 1.0649 against 1.0977, **−2.98 %** against ±3 %.
- **Why it is fragile.** `anchors.sh`'s own header says an out-of-engine figure is "a prediction to the printed tenth at best", and −2.98 % is −3.0 % at the tenth.
- **Why K290 would not catch it.** Its split is rate −0.33 % and population −2.67 %. Neither part is outside the band on its own, so even if the line were registered it would FAIL, not register.
- **Failure scenario.** The integrator's simulation bar runs in Mono and prints `(c) USA 2055 … (−3.01%)`, and the bar goes red on a line no ruling covers.
- **What to do.** Do not register 2055 pre-emptively; that would fit the rule to an outcome. If the engine run tips it, report it as a stopping result to Elias.
- **A design note for the record.** K290's rule needs one part to miss alone, so a miss that is "mostly population" but sits just over the band can never be registered. That is conservative (it fails rather than hides), but it should be written down with K290.

### Low

**L1. K290 bounds the rate part but not the population part or the total, so a later population regression would hide inside the registered line.**
- **Where:** `Assets/Editor/ParticipationCohortDiagnostic.cs:384`.
- **What the gate checks.** It requires |population part| > 3 % and |rate part| ≤ 3 %. It has no ceiling.
- **Failure scenario.** A change to US migration or mortality drops the model's 15+ growth to 2040 from ×1.0618 to ×1.03. The (c) line goes to about −6 %, its population part to about −6 %, and it still prints REGISTERED MISS (K290). Nothing in (c) flags it.
- **What it cannot hide.** A real participation miss larger than the band: the rate part is held to the same ±3 %. That answers the brief's "must not hide a real participation miss".
- **Fix.** Register while the population part stays near its measured −3.34 %, for example within [−4.5 %, −3 %] ([AUTHORED]), or cap the total at twice the band. K218 has no ceiling either, so this is an improvement to suggest, not a breach.
