### The random-stream comparison
- **The passing path moves nothing.** `CaptureLive` takes the seed and the very stream objects (:50). The probe loop's `RestoreState` and `For` build new objects only (:66-72). `Reinstate(live)` then puts back the same objects at their own positions and drops every stream the probe created (:76).

- **The planted break and draw:**
  - The break is added and then removed by `RemoveAt(Count - 1)` (:168).
  - The Parliament draw is undone by the rebuild at `c0`. A stream absent from `c0` is left uncreated, which is the same as position zero.
  - Both are seen: `breaks (...)` and `streams: Parliament n -> n+1 draws`. The clause bites (:169).
  - **Nothing planted survives into the projection's own measurement.**

## (2) M3 - Gate18's none-branch (`Gate18Diagnostic.cs:286-322`)

### Sweden
- `roundOf` latches the open round whose `ReadsOn` is the polling day. `RoundOf` drops a concluded round, and the latch keeps the reference, so the round's end is still seen.
- `OpenRoundAfterElection` opens the round on day one. A conclusion needs later days (the ask, the draft at +7, the vote), so the latch always sees the round open first.
- `BreakOff` (:5570-5581) sets `Concluded` on the fourth rejection, with no install. `EndedWithout` holds, the loop stops, and `seen` names the rejections.
- An install concludes the round with `FormedOn > poll`, so `Formed` holds.

- `InstallSejmCabinet` moves the same `SejmFormation` object onto the new record, so the latch survives the install.
- `Conclude` with no cabinet gives `!Open` and `FormedOn <= poll`, so the none-branch passes on the procedure's own end. See L-d for the one case it misnames.

### Germany
- Germany 2025 asserts nothing on `Partial`, and asserts `formed` where the projection is elected at the first ballot.
- The none-branch is reachable for the Bundestag without `Partial` only where the order is empty, or where every ask declines and `continue`s (FormationProjection.cs:66, :93-96). The procedure then continues to phases 2-3, and it ends with none only through `PluralityBallot`'s two `Concluded` exits (`SimulationManager.cs:5189`, :5223).
- Such a case would now print THE WINDOW ENDED or a concluded none. Either is honest, and neither is reachable on Germany 2025's roster.

### Stepping

### A1 - an ask for Elias: which share does q22 mean?
The ruling's words are "a created party with no campaign stays below the threshold whatever its origin". The gate now passes only if the **higher** of two shares stays below the line:
- the expected share (the idle prediction);
- **one** noisy count of it.

That gate is a strict superset of the expected-share gate, so it is never weaker. But it is not a bound over the noise:
- **It is one realisation.** The noise is keyed on (777, the country, `EpochDate`), and the created party always sits at the same ordinal key position ("NEW"). So every grid point and every Splinter reads the same per-region noise numbers. The ~100 measurements are one sample of the noise, not many.
- **Elias's options:**
  - **(a)** q22 means the expected share. Then the counted half is a reported extra, and it can fail on a seed although the ruling holds.
  - **(b)** q22 means every count in play. Then one seed proves nothing, and the gate would need a bound: the cap, plus the noise's tail at the cap's share.

Today's form is the conservative reading of (a). Until Elias rules, the integrator should treat a FAIL here that comes only from the counted share as a question, not a defect.

## (4) L1 - the Sejm's own-election branch, against the procedure (`SimulationManager.FormationProjection.cs:196-209`, `SimulationManager.Sejm.cs:32-176`)

### The days
The procedure steps daily (`SimulationManager.cs:4378`; `AdvanceSejmFormation` runs every step due on `CurrentDate >= NextOn`). The projection's days match it on every path:
- **Designated, not of record.** The steps run:
  1. `AwaitingSitting` on the sitting, with `NextOn` = sitting + 14 (Sejm.cs:72);
  2. `Designated` on sitting + 14, with `NextOn` = sitting + 28 (:82);
  3. `AwaitingConfidence` on sitting + 28: refused, it sets `NextOn = CurrentDate` (:94), so `SejmElects` runs the same day;
  4. the appointment on +`AppointmentAfterSejmElectionDays` (2) (:127).

  The projection's `electsOn` = sitting + 2 × `StepWindowDays` and `appointedOn` = `electsOn` + 2 are the same days.
- **None designated, not of record.** `SejmElects` runs on sitting + 14 (:64-65), which is the projection's 1 × `StepWindowDays`.
- **Of record.** `SejmElects` waits for `record.SejmElected` (:94) and the appointment for `record.SejmAppointed` (:127). These are the projection's `ofRecord ?` arms.
  - On 2023 the confidence vote and the Sejm's election fall on one day (2023-12-11), so the step runs on the record's day.

### The prime minister's rule
- `SejmElects` sets `f.PmParty` from `FromView(view, electsOn)` (:124).
- The install then re-derives the government: `InstallSejmCabinet` takes `FromView(view, appointment day)` wherever `SameKeys(view.Cabinet, f.Cabinet)` holds (:151-156). It always holds here, because both come from one `Projection(country, pollingDay)`.
- The projection does the same through `Installed(..., designated: false, ...)` with `appointedOn`: the same view, the same date, so the same prime minister and supporters. **Same days, same rule.**
