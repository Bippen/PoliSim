### Defects
1. **SimulationManager.cs:1411-1413 and 1210/1258-1262 (the UI's twin is GameController.BudgetV35.cs:964-966).** The budget act is decided and recorded on the whole bill, rates included. `PolishTaxAct` then votes the rates a second time. Under D4 the rates are not part of the budget act. Scenario: in Poland the player drafts income tax −2 and Defence +5 %. A party that opposes the cut votes AGAINST the budget act, and its reason names taxation. If that tips the count, the veto-proof budget act fails and the spending is lost, when only the tax act should have fallen. Fix: where `Applies && ChangesTaxRates`, decide and record the budget act on `bill.WithoutRateChanges()`, and do the same for the slip's headline. If Elias means the rates to be voted twice, that is his ruling to make, and the record should say so.
2. **SimulationManager.cs:1298-1299, the title.** Every line is printed as `"0.0"` plus `" %"`.
   - The carbon tax is priced per tonne (`TaxLine.IsPerTonne`). After an "Implement CarbonTax" bill, Poland's title would read "carbon tax 0.0 % to 120.0 %".
   - The AI government's raise of the household rates can be 0.01 to 0.04 points (`RaiseHouseholdRates`, `MinRatePoints` 0.01). The title then reads "income tax 12.4 % to 12.4 %".
   - Fix: use `"0.0#"` (or the row's own format) and the per-tonne unit for the carbon tax.
3. **SimulationManager.cs:1265.** `CreditAdopted(country, government)` credits the Finance partner's whole step even when `PolishTaxAct` withheld the rates. The partner's tightening puts whatever is left over on income tax and VAT (FinancePartner.cs:253-271). When the ministry has already claimed every spending line, the free scope is 0 and the whole step lands on those rates. Scenario: Poland under an AI head, with a partner holding Finance. The step lands as a rise in income tax or VAT, PiS opposes it, and the veto stands. Nothing moved, yet `FinanceStanceApplied` advances, so the next year's step is too small. Credit only the part that landed.
4. **GameController.cs:6524-6529, election night's "standing budget".** The scan takes the newest passed, non-motion fiscal division that carries effects. A signed tax act, or an overridden veto vote, is now newer than the budget act on the same day. The plate then cites "Tax act: …" or "Vetoed by the President …: Tax act … - overridden" as the standing budget. The heuristic is older than this change, but now every Polish budget that moves a rate and whose tax act stands hits it.

Comment-only:
- SimulationManager.cs:1417: the approval ledger's "Budget bill passed (tax hike)" was always a mislabel. It now also logs a budget whose rates were vetoed as a tax hike, with a delta of 0.
- GameController.BudgetV35.cs:1037: the tax-act line also shows under a WOULD FAIL headline, where no tax act is voted. Suggest the prefix "IF THE BUDGET PASSES".
