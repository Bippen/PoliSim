### M1 - the M1 fix took the ground swing out of the House base's SHARE but not out of its SEATS, so the ground game's seats compound across cycles
- **The count.** `UsCongressElection.cs:377` sets `uhat = b.U + delta + ground`. The seats are `rep = RB + P(cT, uhat) - P(cB, U)`, so the stored `Rep` includes the ground game's seats (`:386`, together with `Share = uhat` and `Ground = ground`).
- **The next base.** `GameBases` (`:327`) reads `U = s.Share - s.Ground` but `RB = s.Rep`. The anchor pair (RB, U) is no longer consistent: RB is the seat count at share U+g, while U is the share without g.
- **Scenario.** Take a state with ten contested seats, U = 0.52 and RB = 5.
  - 2024: a ground swing g = +0.04 gives uhat = 0.56 and rep = 5 + P(10, 0.56) - P(10, 0.52) = 5 + 6 - 5 = 6. Stored: Rep 6, Share 0.56, Ground 0.04.
  - 2026 midterm (no campaign, delta = 0): U' = 0.52 and rep = 6 + P(10, 0.52) - P(10, 0.52) = 6. A share-consistent base gives 5.
  - 2028 with g2 = -0.04: rep = 6 + P(10, 0.48) - P(10, 0.52) = 6, against 5 for a consistent base.
  - The +1 seat stays in every later RB.
- **Consequence.** Each cycle's ground game and its σ 1.2 regional noise add a rounding-sized seat offset to every multi-seat delegation, and the offsets walk randomly. They drift toward whichever party's ground game is systematically stronger: the drift the first review's M1 named, moved from shares into seats.
- **The comment.** `UsCongressElection.cs:78-79` claims "a cycle's ground game and its noise never pass into a later House count". That is false for the seats.
- **Fix.** Either:
  - store the base seats without the ground swing as well (for example `RepBase = clamp(RB + P(cT, b.U + delta) - P(cB, b.U))`, with `GameBases` reading it beside `U = Share - Ground`); or
  - keep `Share` with the ground swing and correct the next count's `vBase` by the national mean of the ground swings, so that only the relative pattern carries, as the presidential side does through `FromGameStates`.
- Whichever is chosen, DECLARE it, add a two-cycle tooth to (h), and correct the comment.

### L2 - the regional noise can create "every other candidate" votes where none stands on a ballot of record (US-6c, J3)

- **Fix:** skip the noise on a column whose derived share is 0 (or exclude it from the noise), or DECLARE it beside L6 of the first review.
