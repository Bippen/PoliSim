# Review - §802, PS-6 US-10: the veto and the filibuster on the record (2026-10-06)

One adversarial reviewer, a single agent, read the US-10 build:
- `Tools/us_veto_fetch.pl`, `Tools/us_veto_prep.pl` and its CSV, `Tools/us_veto_backtest.pl` and its document, `Tools/us_veto_mutations.sh`;
- `Assets/Editor/UsVetoBacktestCheck.cs`;
- `ElectionsData/usa/veto_record.md` and the plan's new lines.

It ran both tools read-only into temporary paths and checked measures against the BILLSTATUS XML and the roll calls. It edited nothing. Its report follows, wording kept and layout lightly reformatted, then what was done.

## The report

**Two high, two medium and two low findings.** The verdict "(a)" survives, but the numbers in the ASKED line are wrong and the "No STOP" call is fragile.

**Checked and correct:**
- Both tools reproduce byte for byte.
- The presidents at each boundary: H.R. 39 and S. 84 on 2017-01-20 are Trump's, and no 116th or 118th decision is misassigned.
- All 25 vetoes and their kinds.
- The override arithmetic and results.
- The en bloc roll 179: its question text does name S.J.Res. 37.
- The cloture rounding.
- B1's 45 of 166.

**1. HIGH: the prep takes an earlier, replaced agreement as a chamber's final one in about 20 measures** (`us_veto_prep.pl:78-99`). When the real last agreement uses wording the matchers miss, the code falls back to an earlier passage of a different text, and nothing checks for this. The missed wordings:
- **House:**
  - "Pursuant to the provisions of H. Res. 1061, the House agreed to the Senate amendment …" and "House agreed to Senate amendment … pursuant to H.Res. N": H.R. 4366 and H.R. 2882 (118th); S. 1301, H.R. 7776, H.R. 4373 and H.R. 1082 (117th); H.R. 1520 (116th); H.R. 6, H.R. 302 and H.R. 2266 (115th); H.R. 4 (119th).
  - "On motion the House agree…" with no "that": H.R. 3401 (116th).
  - "…suspend the rules and recede … and concur": H.R. 7147 (119th).
  - A garbled Clerk line: H.R. 1917 (117th).
- **Senate:**
  - "Senate agreed, under the order of …, having achieved 60 votes in the affirmative, to the House amendment" fails, because only one aside is allowed: H.R. 1437, H.R. 7776, H.R. 2882 and S. 4367.
  - "House Amendment" with a capital A fails: S. 709 and S. 3791.
  - "Senate receded from its amendment and concurred with an amendment" fails: H.R. 1 (115th).

Two of these change the rule counts:
- **H.R. 3401 (Trump, first term):** the 230-195 first passage is taken instead of the 305-102 concurrence.
- **H.R. 4366 (Biden):** the July 2023 MilCon-VA vote is taken instead of the March 2024 omnibus agreement through H.Res. 1061, which should name nothing.

Corrected, (a) pooled is 22 of 33 (66.7 %), F3 and (b) name 34, and 13 measures are vetoable (Trump 9, Biden 4). The fix: add these wordings, plus a structural guard: if the chamber that acted last did so "with an amendment", the other chamber must have a later agreement.

**2. HIGH (affects the verdict): "No STOP" rests on parties voting differently in the two chambers** (`us_veto_backtest.pl:102-120, 181`). In every one of the 15 vetoable measures, the president's party opposed in the House and supported in the Senate, or went unrecorded there. The plan's risk 4 says "each party voting as one".
- If a party takes one position in both chambers, nothing it opposes gets through.
- Without the expedited procedures, no party had 60 seats.
- With them, the opposing party never held a Senate majority in the matching Congress.

So the count is 0, which is exactly R-US17's STOP. The reading behind 15 (or 13) should go to Elias as a question, not be printed as "No STOP".

**3. MEDIUM: the cheap-bar check fails after a fresh checkout.** `us_veto_measures.csv` has no `.gitattributes` line, and `core.autocrlf=true`, so a checkout writes CRLF line endings, the digest moves, and the check fails. Fix: add the line, as was done for `usa/house_*.csv`.

**4. MEDIUM: the check leaves parts of the document uncovered and holds two empty checks.**
- It never recomputes the bloc section, the (c) table, the "Every veto" table, or which verdict is chosen. It only checks the verdict is one of three prefixes.
- The override check compares the prep's own two-thirds formula with itself, so it can never fail.

**5. LOW: two CRA disapprovals are classed as plain joint resolutions.** `^"?Disapproving the rule submitted by` misses "A joint resolution disapproving the rule submitted by…" (S.J.Res. 18 and 28, 119th), so "it sends 1510" should be 1512.

**6. LOW: wording.** The ASKED parenthetical leaves out the D.C. disapproval.

## What was done

- **1:** every wording above is read, any number of asides and either capital included. The structural guard is installed, and on the same day the chamber agreeing to the other's amendment is taken as the later one, since the Senate's actions carry no time. The guard found five measures:
  - H.R. 1370, H.R. 133 and H.R. 1520 were same-day orderings.
  - H.R. 1917 was the garbled line.
  - S. 1301 was "House agreed" with no "the".

  All five are now read. A Senate "Motion to Recede … and Concur with Further Amendment" joins the concurrence questions (H.R. 1, 115th, roll 323). Seven new roll calls were fetched. The figures are now the reviewer's: (a) 22 of 33 (66.7 %); F3 and (b) 34; 13 vetoable chamber by chamber (Trump 9, Biden 4).
- **2:** taken. The document now prints the plan's premise first: each party one stance in both chambers, with each Congress's seats. Under it a bloc Congress sends 0 of the 34 measures the president's party opposed. **The verdict is STOPPED, and R-US17 is re-asked.** The chamber-by-chamber reading is printed beside it, marked as the reading declared before the first run. This changes the verdict after a result was seen, so the record says so plainly and Elias is told.
- **3:** `ElectionsData/.gitattributes` gains `usa/us_veto_measures.csv -text`.
- **4:**
  - The self-comparing override check is gone; the prep keeps its own override test against the roll calls' stated results and the Senate's lists.
  - The check now recomputes the bloc Congress under both readings.
  - It now chooses the verdict again from the record, with B1's yardstick read from the Sejm's CSV.
  - The (c) table and the "Every veto" table remain recomputed only by the tool, declared.
- **5:** the CRA pattern is unanchored; 1,512 are sent.
- **6:** the D.C. disapproval is named.
- **Also:** the backtest's CSV reader was quadratic on decoded UTF-8 (`substr` per character), at 120 s or more a run. It now reads an array of characters, at 0.6 s.
- **Also, found by the guard's own mutation case:** in build mode the measures' problems were reported only after the roll calls were read, so a misread final agreement died first on its unfetched roll call. The measures' problems now stop the run before any roll call is read. The suite has twelve cases, the guard's among them.
