# The adversarial review of PN-3b — the wage bill's base off the 20–64 window (§577, 2026-09-22)

**What was read.** `Assets/Scripts/Data/TaxBases.cs` as this change leaves it (the `WageBill` driver, `Level`, `Base`, `Ratio`, `Of`), `Assets/Scripts/Data/PotentialOutput.cs` (`Population15Plus`, `LabourForce`, `LabourInput`), `Assets/Scripts/Data/Country.cs`'s `CaptureStructuralBases`, `Assets/Scripts/Persistence/SaveGameService.cs`'s version gate, `Assets/Editor/LabourInputWindowDiagnostic.cs` (section 4, re-formed here from PN-3b's premise into its guard) and `Assets/Editor/TrajectorySentinelCheck.cs`'s re-based baseline. The family `traj_pn3b` (seeds 777 and 424242, horizon 100) was dumped on this change's own code and diffed against `p6pap2` with `Tools/traj_diff.pl`.

**The ruling** (Elias, 2026-09-22): *"PN-3b, the wage bill's base — the same 20–64-count-times-15+-rate seam PN-3 closed; its own family, measured first, explained per country, on the new loop."*

## 1 · The seam, measured before the change

`TaxBases.Level(WageBill)` was `SpendingDrivers.Level(WorkingAge20To64) × the state's participation rate × (1 − U) × the real wage`. The state's participation rate is a **15-and-over** rate by construction (`ParticipationRateTable.StructuralRate` sums band × sourced rate over the population aged 15+), so the base multiplied a COUNT on one base by a RATE on another and an ageing pyramid moved both the same way — the double count PN-3 (§522) retired from potential. Measured at year 25 before the re-form, the base's labour part against the input potential already reads: **Italy −12.68 %, Poland −12.34, France −7.37, Germany −7.11, the USA −3.45, Sweden −3.14.**

After: the base IS `PotentialOutput.LabourInput × the real wage`, one expression, and the diagnostic's section 4 now **asserts** that the base's ratio equals potential's input ratio (it fails on a drift past 0.01 %) and prints beside it what the window it left would have read. The two can no longer drift apart, which is the point of routing through the accessor rather than repeating its three factors.

## 2 · The seed, and why the save format had to move

`Country.CaptureStructuralBases` writes `RevenueBaseSeeds[driver] = TaxBases.Level(driver, this)` at world creation, so a NEW world captures the pyramid's level and the base's ratio is exactly 1 at the seed. **A save from before does not**: it carries the WINDOW's level as the wage-bill driver's seed, and the re-formed level read against it would price every wage-taxed base at the ratio of the 15+ population to the 20–64 cohort on the first day. That ratio was seen during this pass, by accident and usefully: while the diagnostic still divided by the window's seed it read the base at **×1.49 of the window's** at year 25. `CurrentSaveVersion` is therefore **22**, with that reason on the constant; the loader refuses a format it does not read, so no save can silently take the jump.

⚠ **Two instruments ride this base and they are the largest**: `IncomeTax` and `PayrollTax` (`TaxBases.Of`). Nothing else changes driver.

## 3 · What I went looking for and did not find

- **A circularity.** `PotentialOutput.LabourInput` reads the cohorts, the state's participation rate and unemployment. It reads nothing from `TaxBases`, so the base cannot feed itself through potential.
- **A second copy of the old arithmetic.** `SpendingDriver.WorkingAge20To64` still exists and is still read — by the spending drivers, where a 20–64 count is what a line actually follows. It is no longer read by a tax base.
- **A country without a pyramid.** `Population15Plus` falls back to `State.Population`; the seed and the level go through the same function, so the ratio is still 1 at the seed and the fallback cancels.
- **A stale claim.** Three were left saying the base reads the window (`PotentialOutput`'s note, the diagnostic's head and its section-4 note). All three now say what is true; `DocumentClaimCheck` and `CommentClaimCheck` pass.

## 4 · The family, explained per country

`traj_pn3b` against `p6pap2`, seed 777, at turn 100 — **372 of 462 field-readings moved**, which is what a base under the two largest tax lines does. Five states take the same shape and the sixth does not:

| country | fields moved | budget | debt | GDP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| France | 63 of 77 | −5 334.76 → −3 547.77 (**+33.50 %**) | −19.75 % | +1.61 % |
| Italy | 64 of 77 | −1 619.94 → −1 293.13 (**+20.17 %**) | −6.82 % | +2.60 % |
| Poland | 65 of 77 | −11 363.88 → −10 150.17 (**+10.68 %**) | −10.23 % | +2.01 % |
| Sweden | 64 of 77 | −3 366.71 → −3 227.62 (**+4.13 %**) | −3.88 % | +1.70 % |
| Germany | 59 of 77 | −19 433.92 → −18 927.25 (**+2.61 %**) | −2.26 % | +1.55 % |
| **the USA** | 57 of 77 | −975 182.5 → −998 812.6 (**−2.42 %**) | **+2.34 %** | +2.73 % |

**The five**: a bigger taxed wage base is more revenue on the income and payroll lines, so the deficit narrows and the debt path falls with it — largest where the ageing bit hardest (Italy and Poland's seams were −12.7 and −12.3 %, France's −7.4), and the order of the budget effect follows the order of the seam, not the size of the state.

⚠ **The USA runs the other way, and the cause is its fiscal rule, not its base.** Its government consumption does not merely rise — it stops being CUT: on the old path it falls 4 053 at t20 → 3 122 at t40 → **2 056 at t60** before recovering to 3 821 at t100; on this one it reads 4 023 at t20, 4 338 at t40, 4 191 at t60 and 7 787 at t100. The AI finance ministry's US caps and sequester (§387, §388) consolidate against the deficit, and a larger taxed base means the consolidation does not bite as deep; the rule then spends the room rather than banking it, which is why the deficit is 2.4 % wider and the debt 2.3 % higher while GDP is 2.7 % higher and the outcomes the spending buys improve sharply (students per teacher −35.2 %, homelessness −19.5 %, road quality +5.8 %). **This attribution is read off the series above and the ministry's rule, not off the ministry's own log** — it is the only thing in the model that moves US government consumption in that shape, and it is stated here rather than asserted by a check.

## 5 · The sentinel

Re-based to `pn3b` with both digests in this commit (`c6f43e5e…` seed 777, `f0cd44a3…` seed 424242). The four recorded FT-10 cells all **improved** — France household 20.728 → **19.454** (t48), France industry 5.396 → **5.064**, Poland household 2.740 → **2.414**, Poland industry 2.793 → **2.461** — and the bounds pass caught its own consequence: France's household ceiling of 21.0 now stands more than a point above its measurement and was lowered to **20.0** by the slack rule, which is the ratchet working in the direction it is meant to. The breach count is unchanged at 4.

## 6 · What this does not do

It does not touch the participation response of the bands a pension age crosses — PN-1's open half, which would reach this base through `ParticipationRateTable` if it ever lands (trigger T-11). It does not re-seed anything else: the other four drivers are untouched, and `TaxBaseTable.BaseShareOfGdp` (the sourced share at the seed) is unchanged, so the base's LEVEL at the seed is still the sourced share of the seed's GDP — only its path away from the seed changed.
