## Medium

**M1. The strip's clock chip says the clock is running while the budget window holds it.** GameController.TopStrip.cs:152 sets `paused = holds.Count > 0 || _gameSpeed == GameSpeed.Paused`. But `holds` comes from `CollectHolds(includeBudgetProcess: _bannerIncludesBudget)`, which leaves the budget window out on the Budget page (GameController.cs:5931). Failure scenario: Sweden, the annual budget window holds the clock, the speed is 1x, and the player opens the Budget page to draft. The strip draws a paper `1×` chip whose slip reads "The days run at this speed", while Update returns at `BudgetWindowHolds()` and no day moves. Before K178 that page drew no banner outside the USA, and the USA's masthead had no clock chip, so this false state is new. It is player-visible on the most common hold in the game, and it contradicts the "why has time stopped" guarantee. Fix: decide `paused` from the full hold predicate (the `isTimePaused` reading, or `CollectHolds(true).Count > 0`), not the banner's filtered list.

**M2. The non-US Desk's first row shrinks by the strip's height and the calendar card's month grid can clip silently. Not verified on film.** GameController.Desk.cs:783 shortens `rowWorld` by about 43 px at 1280 for every non-Congress country. The calendar card puts `DrawCalendarMonthGrid` inside a `GUILayout.BeginArea(body)` (Desk.cs:1095-1099). The grid's height comes from the month's rows (GameController.cs:3653-3658), and BeginArea clips what overflows without a guard. Design's "the calendar card's empty foot absorbs it" was measured on one film month. Failure scenario: a six-row month (August 2026 starts on a Saturday: 5 lead blanks + 31 days = 6 rows) with `DeskProvenance` on (the dense line takes another wrapped line). The last week row is cut off with no overflow report. Owed: a Sweden film at 1280 on a six-row month with provenance on, or a measured reserve that falls back to the full row when the grid does not fit.

## Low

**L1. Keyboard input reaches the page under the plate.** GameController.Crisis.cs:57-69 swallows only pointer events. A slider or text field that held `keyboardControl` before the crisis day still takes arrow keys or typing under the scrim. A control holding `hotControl` (a drag in progress) loses its MouseUp to the swallow and stays grabbed until the next release. The project's own comment (GameController.cs, the saves-screen note) explains why IMGUI modals elsewhere are screen swaps. Fix: on the plate's rising edge, set `GUIUtility.keyboardControl = 0` and `GUIUtility.hotControl = 0`, and swallow KeyDown over the page.

**L2. Two RETURN items on 32a are missing from the plate.** "The head's slip carries the sources" has no slip on the plate head at all (Crisis.cs:163-175). The haircut row's "the stock before and after" is absent too (Crisis.cs:268): only the sum written off is shown. Neither is declared.

**L3. "so far" over a trailing-year reading.** Crisis.cs:564-565 prints "▲ x over, so far" / "▼ x under, so far". The reading is the trailing year to the last closed quarter (K-d), which for most of year 1 is mostly pre-programme months. The figure line dates it honestly ("the year to ..."), but the verdict's "so far" reads as programme-year-to-date. Suggest "on the year to <date>".

**L4. Desk chart labels spill into the right column.** In `DrawTargetChart`, the running figure's label for year 3 starts at x+barW+8 = 294 px and runs about 40 px. The "3 %" reference label starts at `area.xMax - 2`. With `chartW` about 293 at 1280 and the right column starting 26 px after it, the year-3 label overlaps the right column's rows by about 15 px (Crisis.cs:414, 421). The plate's ladder is 7 x 43 = 301 px in a 300 px column, which is trivial.

**L5. The staging can leave a staged preview cached.** The Budget visit for 05u runs `if (PolicyInputsChangedSinceLastPreview()) RecomputePolicyPreview();` (BudgetV35.cs:56), and the Desk's effects card does the same. The preview's clone copies the staged programme (official lending, so issuance prices without the premium). The cache is keyed by turn, draft and role, not by Crisis, so a preview computed during staging would survive the restore into later Budget films. In the current sequence 01d settles the moved drafts first, so this is probably a no-op today. Cheap fix: clear `_hasCachedPreview` in the `finally`. Also, 05u does not reset `_budgetProcessCenterScrollPosition`, and on 01g the haircut row is shown beside a Debt-to-GDP that was not written down (declared in the summary, but the film Design compares against will disagree with play).

**L6. A real crisis in a film world leaves the plate over that day's shots.** Driver warm-ups advance with `sim.AdvanceDay()` directly (for example UiScreenshotDriver.cs:2391, 4206) and never acknowledge. A trajectory that crosses CCC paints the plate over every shot taken on its opening day. This is rare, but nothing reports it. A `SHOT:` warning when `CrisisPlateHolds` is true outside 01g would catch it.

**L7. Diagnostic gaps and mild tautologies.** (a) tests `CrisisPlateOwed` on planted states but never the integration the plate rests on: that a review day reading CCC through the model's own `AdvanceDay` leaves `StartedOn == CurrentDate`. It also misses the controller wrapper's load reset and `!_isGameOver`. (f)'s pin does not cover the `isTimePaused` addition or the Desk row shrink. (b) `heldOk` and (c) `far` build their expected strings with `TopStrip.Day`, the formatter under test. Each section otherwise fails while false (checked by mutation reasoning: dropping the date test, the count test, the face or the fold count each trips a section). The planted worlds are fresh per case and the hosts are destroyed, so no leak between cases.

**L8. France against the RETURN.** The RETURN asks for chip 4 to carry the censure motion (art. 49) and for "cohabitation in France and Poland". The model holds no censure motion (`TakesMotions` is the Riksdag and the Bundestag only) and no French presidency, so both are correctly absent, but neither is declared. "Relative majority" for a minority cabinet is a term stretch (K-f). Germany sits in `TakesMotions` while `ConfidenceChip`'s doc says the constructive votes never wait. That is harmless only as long as the Bundestag path never sets `NoConfidenceOn`.

**L9. Wording.** The hold chip counts the plate and election night as "decisions waiting" (TopStrip.cs controller :164). The Desk default card's "so far" cut and the CrisisLines slip read the run-cumulative `CutBillions`, so a second default would include the first's cut.
