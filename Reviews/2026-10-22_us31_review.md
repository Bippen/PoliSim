## Findings

- **H1 - the US-9 generated block on the card is stale, so `UsCampaignGeographyCheck` fails.**
  - **What is wrong:** the commit changes two lines that `UsCampaignGeographyCheck.WriteReadings` writes into the card:
    - `Assets/Editor/UsCampaignGeographyCheck.cs:303` adds "This is US-9's staging, which declares no targeting rule; since US-31 ...".
    - `:428` changes "its one reader, is wired to nothing" to "was wired to nothing ... since US-31 the count the game makes ...".
  - The card was not regenerated: `docs/reference/US_ELECTIONS.md:722` and `:750` still hold the old prose.
  - `Run` compares the whole block, text included (`onDisk == expected`, `UsCampaignGeographyCheck.cs:~100`), so the bar reports "the model card's US-9 block ... is STALE".
  - **Knock-on:** `UsCampaignDiagnostic` (m) reads its figures from that same block (`Us9CardReading`). Its regex still matches the old block, but the regenerated block must be checked again before (m) is trusted.
  - **Fix:** run `-executeMethod PoliSim.EditorTools.UsCampaignGeographyCheck.WriteReadings` on the integrated tree and commit the regenerated block. The ceiling sentence's figures should not move: the (b) and (c) arithmetic is unchanged, and only the prose differs.

- **M1 - the House ground swing is stored into the next House base, so its national part carries into every later House count.**
  - **What is wrong:** `UsCongressElection.cs:373` adds each state's ground swing to `uhat`, and `:382` stores it as `StateHouse.Share`.
  - `GameBases` (`:324`) reads `U = s.Share` as the next count's base, but `vBase = gameBase.V` is the poll's V, which holds no ground game.
  - So the next count's swing `delta = vT - vBase` leaves the ground swing inside every state's base: the 2026 midterm, and through it 2028.
  - The national mean of the ground swings plus the regional noise therefore builds up across cycles as a random walk, with drift wherever one party's ground game is systematically stronger. The House is pulled away from the poll's V.
  - **Contrast with the presidential path:** that path does not drift. `FromGameStates` re-solves the uniform swing to the new national shares, so only the relative pattern carries.
  - **Contrast with US-27:** US-27's M2 was consistent because `VBase` matched the stored shares; here it does not.
  - **Fix:** keep the ground swing out of the base. Either:
    - store it per state (for example `StateHouse.Ground`) and have `GameBases` read `U = s.Share - s.Ground`, or
    - store on the contest a `V` consistent with the counted shares (each base's `U` minus its own ground swing, which comes to the same thing).
  - In both cases the seats still carry the ground game. DECLARE the choice, or ask Elias if carrying it is intended. (I3 says the swing "enters", not that it persists.)

- **M2 - the AI's offices, which are much of its ground game, still stand at the largest audiences.**
  - **What is wrong:** the rule redirects only the local-act candidate regions (`CampaignAi.cs:623`).
  - Each AI party's offices are staged by `OfficesFor` (`LiveCampaignSetup.cs:292` → `:567`), which sorts by audience, so they open in CA, TX, FL and NY.
  - An office door-knocks every day (`offices[p].Day(gotv, ...)`, `CampaignRun.cs:666`), and since US-31 those contacts are exactly what `UsGroundGame.Effects` counts. The bulk of an AI's mobilization therefore lands in safe states, where it moves no electors.
  - That only partly meets the plan's "the AI targets by a DECLARED rule ... in place of the largest audiences". Diagnostic (l) passes because it counts only logged local acts.
  - **Fix:** in `TryUsa`, place the AI parties' offices by the rule's order, read on the staging's prior through `TargetingFor` (the same derivation; no poll has been published yet, so the prior is the public fact). Or DECLARE offices out of scope in the item, the card and the hand-back, and ask.

- **L1 - a rule that contests nothing leaves the AI with no local act.**
  - **What is wrong:** `RuleRegionsOf` (`CampaignAi.cs:1044`) returns an empty list whenever `OrderOn` is empty, and `regionChoices` is then empty for a non-reactive party.
  - `OrderOn` is empty in two cases:
    - every jurisdiction is at or past `SwingRegions.FullScaleGapPp`;
    - `WeightsOf` returns all zeros because the count could not be made (E1 factors NaN, `TryField` fails, or the poll's party count differs; `UsGroundGame.cs:236-243`).
  - In both cases this is silent.
  - **Fix:** fall back to `LargestRegions` when `RuleRegionsOf` yields nothing, and record the fallback.

- **L2 - the targeting derivation reads no nominees.**
  - **What is wrong:** `UsGroundGame.cs:241` calls `Count(..., null, 0, previous, previousStates, ...)`, while `Hold` passes `UsTermLimit.NomineesFor(country, date)`.
  - In a year with a field of record and the player's own nominee (US-33), the count scales that party by the future factor and the rule by the factor of record. The AI then reads a different derivation from the one the count makes, contrary to the comment's "the count's own derivation".
  - **Fix:** pass `played != null ? UsTermLimit.NomineesFor(played, pollingDay.Date) : null`, read once at staging.

- **L3 - a campaign whose ground game cannot be read is counted silently as derived.**
  - **What is wrong:** `SimulationManager.cs:5741` sets `groundGame = UsGroundGame.Of(...)`. Where `Of` returns null (REP or DEM not staged, or the mobilization's region count differs), `Hold` gets null and the count's line says nothing.
  - By contrast, the `Effects` failure path does write its reason into the line.
  - **Fix:** log a warning (or append the reason to the line) where a campaign ran but `Of` returned null.

- **L4 - diagnostic (m)'s card tolerance leaves no room for the count's rounding.**
  - **What is wrong:** `UsCampaignDiagnostic.cs:530` requires `|us9.Low - cardLow| <= CardRoundingPp` (0.005), which is exactly half the card's last digit.
  - US-9 measured the ceiling's move as counted margin minus the base count's margin, both rounded to whole votes on VEP. `Effects` measures counted minus the derived preference, on CVAP.
  - The two differ by the base count's whole-vote rounding: up to about 4e-4 points in the smallest jurisdictions, which is where the range's low end sits. A true value within that distance of a rounding boundary fails spuriously.
  - **Fix:** tolerate `CardRoundingPp + UnworkedPp`, or measure (m) against an idle `Effects` (the same rounding on both sides).

- **L5 - stated: the diagnostic's party filter relies on both parties standing everywhere.**
  - Diagnostic (l)'s per-day picks filter by `setup.StandsIn(0, r)` (party 0's ballot), while `RuleRegionsOf` filters by each party's own.
  - This is correct today: the USA stages REP and DEM only, and (c) holds both on every ballot. A staged minor party would make (l) fail spuriously.
  - **Fix:** filter by the acting party's own `StandsIn`.

- **L6 - stated: the floor at zero gives "every other candidate" a small upward bias.**
  - W-D1's `ApplyNoise` floors each noisy share at zero. On the small "every other candidate" share (about 1 to 2 %) at σ 1.2 this gives a mean bias of about +0.1 point, which moves margins only through re-normalisation (below 0.2 % of a margin).
  - This is W-D1's declared form, so nothing is owed. It is noted because it now reaches play.
