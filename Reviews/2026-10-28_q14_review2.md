### N-1 (Low): the law card states a natural-rate cut the economy will not deliver
GameController.cs:8317-8334 (and the rows at :8508, :8671) print the law's structural delta as written: "Natural rate of unemployment (pp) -1.2". Under K199, France's room below its NAIRU is 7.5 - 7.358 = 0.142, so France gets about an eighth of a point from that act. Germany and the USA have 0.35 and 0.37 of room. Follow-on: a per-country "effective here" figure, or a line naming the floor. Not a blocker.

### N-2 (Low): the NEXT YEAR projection cuts a pension line the guarantee will hold
`SpendingLine.ProjectNextYear` (GameController.BudgetV35.cs:808, the total at GameController.Statistics.cs:98) multiplies by (1 + inflation) at any negative print. In a deflating year the USA's or Germany's pension row shows a nominal fall, and the boundary then books none (K201). Failure scenario: a slump at -1 % shows the US Social Security row falling 1 % next year, and the next budget shows it flat. Fix: route the projection through `PensionNominalGuarantee` (prices = 1 + inflation/100).
