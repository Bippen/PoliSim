# K273 prereg: the support fraction f, committed before its first Unity run (2026-10-29)

**Honest note.** These rules were drafted after the implementer's out-of-Unity reproduction (`Tools/k273_support_sweep` on `wave/k273`), which the brief asked for. Each rule is justified on its own terms, not by where it lands. What this prereg fixes before Unity is the in-engine acceptance: the bars may not re-choose f.

1. **The grid.** f ∈ {0.05, 0.10, …, 0.95}. "Partly" excludes 0 (support not scored, the rule K273 replaces) and 1 (a supporter weighted as a member).
2. **The acceptance.** An f passes a record when `CoalitionFormation.Form` on the game's reader seats exactly the record's cabinet:
   - Sweden 2022: record seats, `ElectionVintage.Sweden2022` declarations, giving M+KD+L with SD supporting;
   - Germany 2025: record seats, polling-day reading, giving CDU+CSU+SPD;
   - Poland 2023: record seats AND the game's count (PolishDeclarationsDiagnostic (h), seed 777), polling-day reading, giving KO+TD+NL;
   - Italy 2022: the Camera of record AND the game's count on the 2022 vote (Camera, and the Senato for mandate 2), giving FdI+Lega+FI+NM.
3. **The choice.** The midpoint of the passing interval. The edges are where one seat's swing flips a record; the midpoint is furthest from both.
4. **When no fraction passes all four.** A record that fails at every grid point is REGISTERED as a miss: its check prints REGISTERED MISS (K273) and lifts itself when the condition holds. f is chosen by rule 3 on the remaining records. If those share no interval, f is not set and the item returns to Elias.
5. The constant is [AUTHORED-DRAFT] and is struck with K273.

**Applied (out of Unity).**
- Poland fails at every grid point (record seats and count) and is registered under K267 and K273.
- Sweden passes 0.05-0.55; Germany and Italy pass the whole grid.
- The intersection is [0.05, 0.55], so the midpoint is **f = 0.30**.

**What the Unity bars must show.**
- At 0.30, no government of record or seed changes in any of the six countries.
- PolishDeclarationsDiagnostic prints F7 as REGISTERED MISS (K267, K273) with KO+TD+NL 65.666 against KO+TD 68.154 at the played count.
- The other formation checks pass at their acceptances.
- FormationSweepDiagnostic's mismatch shows score figures and orderings only, with the same governments, before it is re-pinned in its own commit.
- If any government of record moves in-engine, K273 is held and returned, not re-fitted.

**Alternatives measured and not adopted** (the same harness, tools only): a party's hold-out on its own Gamson payoff (plain, partnered-only, positive-majority). Every one lifts Poland (NL refuses to carry KO+TD when it could sit in a cabinet) and breaks Sweden 2022: stripped of supporters, a lone-party minority cabinet survives under negative parliamentarism and outranks M+KD+L (S alone 66.1, SD alone 61.0 against 59.5). Lifting Poland needs a ruling on how a minority cabinet ranks or holds. That ruling is Elias's.
