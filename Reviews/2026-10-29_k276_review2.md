## Findings

### High

**H1. `ParticipationCohortDiagnostic` is not registered in any check table, so no bar ever runs it.**
- **Where:** `Assets/Editor/CheckSuite.cs:518` (the `Simulation` table) and `:171` (`Suite`).
- **What is missing.** Neither table has an entry for it, and `CheckSuite.cs` is not in the diff at all. `RunNamedBatch` (`:773`) refuses names outside the two tables.
- **The report is wrong here.** It says "simulation group; registration as in round 1", and its "UNITY RUNS OWED" expects the simulation bar to print `PARTICIPATION COHORTS: FAIL (USA 2040, Poland 2070)`. There was no registration in round 1 either; review 1 missed it.
- **Failure scenario.** The integrator runs the simulation bar with `-TimeoutSec 3000`. The bar runs its registered checks and never prints a `PARTICIPATION COHORTS` line. It can report clean while (a) the rates by age, (b) the player's statute as the input, and (c) the labour force against the sources are all false.
- **Why it matters.** This is exactly "a diagnostic that fails while the statement is false" in name only. The item's own diagnostic is reachable only through a hand-typed `-executeMethod` or the headless loop instrument, which is not the bar (anchors.sh's own header says so).
- **What still guards the item in the bar:** PensionParticipationDiagnostic (sign only past a horizon, see L2) and LongRunAnchors' (d1)/(d2). Neither checks the rates against the sources or the labour force.
- **Fix.** Add `("ParticipationCohortDiagnostic", ParticipationCohortDiagnostic.Run)` to the `Simulation` table, with its K276 comment, and state its cost (it runs the six centuries again unless NoPolicyCentury's records are shared with LongRunAnchors).

### Medium

**M1. The branch's own pre-registered acceptance fails on two lines. It cannot land green until Elias rules K279 and K280.**
- **Where:** `Assets/Editor/ParticipationCohortDiagnostic.cs:347` and the USA branch below it.
- **The two failures (reproduced headless):**
  - USA 2040: −3.43 % against ±3 %;
  - Poland 2070: −4.95 % against ±3 %.
- The band was not loosened, which is right.
- **Poland's attribution holds in 2070.** The labour-tax term is −3.25 pts, which is more than the whole departure of the state's rate from its anchor (45.11 − 47.45 = −2.34 pts). The reversion lag offsets about +1.3 pts.
  - It is not a K276 defect as such: `LaborTaxParticipationTerm` (MacroSystem.cs:1037) reads the statute's effective rate, which rises under the player's book.
  - But it is a stopping result the new r2-3 form exposes.
- **The USA's attribution** rests on CBO's actual all-people rate standing in for its potential rate (declared). Read it as "mostly population", not "the whole miss".
- **Failure scenario.** Merged as is, once H1 is fixed the simulation bar goes red on `PARTICIPATION COHORTS: FAIL`. Before H1 is fixed, it goes green while a pre-registered check fails, which is worse.

### Low

**L1. Poland's reference is not the age the Ageing Report projected Poland under for women.**
- **Where:** `Assets/Scripts/Data/PensionParticipationResponse.cs:84` with `PensionAgeStatute.cs:106`.
- **The two ages.** The AR's Table I.1.3, row PL, projects 65 for men and **60 for women** throughout. Poland's reference is the statute's single 65.
- **Failure scenario.** A Polish bill to 67 is measured from 65 for every band. But the AR's 60-64 rates for women already embed retirement at 60, so the response's starting point is off for half the band.
- **What is wrong with the record.** The one-age deviation predates K276 (PN-1 declares it). K276's new rule says the reference is "the age the projection was made under", and its declared readings do not name Poland as the exception.
- **Fix.** Name it in the declared readings or as a K-line.

**L2. In the registered bar, nothing checks the size of the negative response past a horizon.**
- **Where:** `Assets/Editor/PensionParticipationDiagnostic.cs:77`.
- **What the check asserts.** For a past-horizon year it asserts only `reference > age`, `response < 0`, and `with == pyramid + response`. The last holds by construction, since `StructuralRate` is the pyramid plus the points.
- **Where the size is checked.** Only in ParticipationCohortDiagnostic (b1), at 2045, which no bar runs (H1).
- **Failure scenario.** A regression that halves the fiche ages' effect, or reads the wrong fiche year, would still pass the registered bar.
- **Fix.** H1's registration covers this.

**L3. The USA attribution clamps its indices silently.**
- **Where:** `Assets/Editor/ParticipationCohortDiagnostic.cs:360-361` (`Math.Min`/`Math.Max` on `UsAll16Plus`).
- **Effect.** It prints information only, but a year outside 2025-2099 would print a ratio against the wrong year without saying so.
- **Note.** The stand-in of the actual for the potential rate is declared.
