## 2. Is "2025 interest over end-2024 stock" right for starts in 2022-2026?

I checked each start (`WorldClock.StartDate`): Italy 21 Jul 2022, France 18 Jul 2024, Germany 6 Nov 2024. Sweden, Poland and the USA take the pre-campaign default, which the hold prints as 2026, 2023 and 2024. **A per-start-year seed would be wrong, so this is not a finding.** §621 is ruled (`WorldClock.cs:634`, `SeedVintage`): there is one seed vintage per country, mid-2026 data, never re-seeded per start and never mixed within a country. The opening stocks are already that vintage. France opens at 116.0 % against the DSM's 2024 figure of 112.7 %, Poland at 59.0 % against 49.7 %, and Italy at 138.0 %, which is Eurostat's 2025 figure of about 137.6 %. Seeding Italy at a 2022 or 2023 rate would put a 2023 price on a 2025 stock, which is exactly the mixing §621 forbids. The size is also small. Eurostat's own start-year rates are Italy 2023 2.81, Poland 2024 4.77, and Germany and France 2025 the same as the seed. The anachronism that does exist is older than K252: the hold reads a 2025-vintage economy against the DSM's path from each start's calendar year (q32's L5 alignment).

The real issue is the denominator's year, not the interest year. See M1.

### Medium

**M1. The rate's denominator is a year older than the stock it is charged on.** Locations: `Tools/k252_debt_rate_prep.pl:151` (`$stock{$n} = $gd->{$c}{$year - 1}`) and `DebtRateSeeds.cs` StockMillions.

- **The mismatch.** The EC's convention, i(t) = INT(t)/D(t-1), suits the DSM's annual recursion. This model is different: it charges the rate daily on the *current* stock (`GetInterestOnDebt`), and under §621 that stock opens at the end-2025 level. Day-one interest therefore overshoots the sourced 2025 interest bill by D(2025)/D(2024): Poland **+16.0 %**, Sweden +6.4 %, the USA +6.1 %, Germany +5.6 %, France +4.7 %, Italy +4.4 %.
- **Poland as a percentage of GDP.** 4.88 x 59.0 = 2.88 % of GDP, against the 2.51 the seed doc's ECB GFS D.41 line anchors (`WorldFactory.cs:63`).
- **The alternative.** 2025 interest over the end-2025 stock reproduces that D.41 line almost exactly: Germany 1.72 x 63 = 1.09 (1.10), France 1.90 x 116 = 2.20 (2.20), Italy 2.81 x 138 = 3.88 (~3.9), Poland 4.20 x 59 = 2.48 (2.51), Sweden 1.77 x 35 = 0.62 (0.61). Interest over the average stock gives Poland 4.51, Sweden 1.83, Germany 1.77, France 1.94, Italy 2.87 and the USA 3.35.
- **Failure scenario.** A Poland game's first-year deficit sits about 0.4 pp of GDP above the notification its primary balance was calibrated to. The hold's Poland interest term starts high, which flatters the "interest smaller than the DSM's" reading. Every alternative lowers France's and Italy's seeds, so it moves (h1) away from the fan's top. I judge USA (h3) ten-year (+11.7, floor 9.6) to stay inside, but it should be re-measured.
- **Recommendation.** Either adopt interest over the average stock, or keep the EC convention and state the day-one overshoot in `DebtRateSeeds`' doc and the prereg. Either way, record it.

**M2. The seeds depart from the ruling's own figures, and nothing records it.** K252 quotes "the source's implied" rates as France 2.24, Italy 2.65 and Germany 1.70. These are the DSM's figures (`DebtPathAnchors`): DsmInterest in the year after the start over DsmBaseline at the start, so 2.521/112.72, 3.665/138.35 and 1.069/62.97. The branch seeds from Eurostat outturns instead: 1.99, 2.94 and 1.82. Italy is now above the ruling's figure and France below it. Neither the prereg nor the commit mentions the ruled numbers.

The branch's choice is the better one:
- it uses an outturn, not a 2024 projection;
- it does not seed from the hold's own comparator, which would make (h1)'s interest term match by construction;
- it keeps §621's single vintage, whereas Italy's 2.65 is a 2023 rate.

Under the standing instruction, though, a departure from a ruled figure needs a K-line Elias can strike. Failure scenario: Elias reads the ruling, sees France at 1.99 rather than 2.24 with no note, and the item is re-litigated. My estimate is that the ruling's 2.24 would put France (h1) near +23 against the fan's +23.8, so the departure is not cosmetic.

### Low

- **L1. The commit names "family k252", but `TrajectorySentinelCheck` is not re-pinned** (`TrajectorySentinelCheck.cs:171`, still `ps8`, digests `:179-180`). The trajectory dump prints `Country.EffectiveDebtRate` every turn, so all six move from turn 1. The sentinel and `ReviewLedgerCheck` (baseline rows, and the digests of the four touched code files) will fail until the integrator re-pins and adds rows. By convention that is the integrator's step (see the k210 and q32 integrator files), so this is flagged for them.
- **L2. `SeedHeld` checks a different world from the run.** It reads the world `OpeningRatio` builds, not the world `NoPolicyCentury.Unchanged` runs. Both are `CreateDefault` after `ApplyStart`, so they agree today. But the doc's "a world built past WorldFactory's seed fails here by name" (`DebtPathHoldDiagnostic.cs:204-205`) overclaims for the run's own world.
- **L3. One old-save path opens at the issuance rate.** A v67 save cut by the pre-K252 build before its first daily accrual still holds -1 and opens at issuance, not the seed. This matches the old behaviour and is acceptable; it is stated here only so nobody thinks the sentinel is dead.
- **L4. The prep tool's status-flag guard is too wide** (`k252_debt_rate_prep.pl:63`). It dies on a flag in *any* year fetched (2021-2025), not only the two years it reads, so a future refetch with a flagged 2021 cell would block regeneration for no reason.
- **L5. The USA's seed sits beside its override.** `BaseDebtInterestRateOverride = 3.3` (`WorldFactory.cs:969`), the old hand-set "blended" rate, still sets the US issuance target at 3.47. The seed of 3.45 now carries the sourced blended rate. The override's comment ("its effective interest rate on EXISTING debt reflects the real blended average") now describes the seed's job and should say so.

## Summary

The sources are byte-exact and every rate recomputes from them. The seeding reaches every world-building path, and the rollover and save format are right. The diagnostic's closures and restated reasons match the decomposition. Seeding all six countries from one 2025 vintage is the correct reading under §621, not an anachronism. M1 is a measurable convention mismatch (Poland's day-one interest is 16 % high) best fixed or stated before integration. M2 needs a K-line recording why the seeds are not the ruling's DSM figures. Neither breaks a band, a check or a save.
