## What was verified and holds

- Generator reproducible: `perl Tools/it_count_prep.pl --check` in a scratch `git archive` copy prints "is current" (2.7 s). It has teeth: one 2022 college cifra bumped by one vote makes it print STALE and exit 1. The catalog is consistent with ElectionsData/italy/collegi2020/.
- Raw data: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt` in raw/seats_2022 - all seven files OK.
- National stage (Rosatellum.AllocateNational): 3 % lists, 10 % coalitions with a 3 %-or-minority member, the 1 % strike from the coalition figure (minority exempt), lett. g among the coalition's 3 % lists, floored quotient both times. Matches art. 83 lett. b-g.
- Lett. h text checked on disk: the floored circoscrizione quotient, integer parts, largest decimals, the exclusion of units already at their lett. f seats ("Esclude dall'attribuzione ...") and the compensation (largest excess first, its decimal seats in ascending order, a deficit unit's highest unused decimal in the same circoscrizione, ties to the national cifra, then the next circoscrizione, then the cross-circoscrizione last resort) are all present in `Distribute`.
- Minority route: a fifth of the region or college wins in a quarter of the circoscrizione's colleges rounded up - matches lett. e n.2 / art. 16-bis.
- Senato: art. 17's single lists and coalition lists admitted on 3 % nationally or 20 % "nella regione" (region-specific); the coalition admission on 20 % "almeno in una regione" (anywhere). The code reads exactly those two (TwentyIn vs TwentyAnywhere). Each region is apportioned by itself with no national compensation - correct.
- Estero: the brief says "d'Hondt per ripartizione". The statute on disk (L. 459/2001 art. 15 c.1 lett. c) is the Hare quotient with the fraction dropped, then the largest remainders, ties to the larger cifra. `Estero` uses `FlooredHare`, so the code follows the statute. The brief's wording is wrong, not the code. The joint list's d'Hondt split on national shares is DECLARED.
- Swing: shares are floored at zero (`Math.Max(0, ...)`). Keys with no 2018 lineage (AzIV, IC, ScN, created parties) take the flat national share. FdI's small 2018 base just gets a large uniform add. The (f) identity check (2018 shares in, 2018 votes out) is right. The 2013 prior figures in PartySystem match previous_elections.md line 152-153.
- No 2022 RESULT read into a played count, except the declared holds: the Valle d'Aosta Camera college (`UV`, the record's 2022 winner) and the VdA Senato college. Both have no 2018 list votes. The six Trentino-Alto Adige Senato colleges are candidates-only in 2022 but have 2018 votes, so they are swung, not held. The Estero bases on 2022 votes per K158 (a base, not an outcome).
- CommentClaimCheck: the three backticked Type.Member references added (`ItalianCount.SenatoSeated`, `ItalianCount2022.CameraLinks`, `WorldClock.NoElectionYet`) all exist. ## Findings

### H1 (High) - ChamberVerdictCacheCheck's who-governs guard fails on SimulationManager.Italy.cs:36
`sitting.Caretaker = true;` matches the guard's DirectWrite regex (`(\w+)\??\.(?:...|Caretaker...)\s*=(?!=)`, receiver `sitting`). That receiver is not `view` or `projection`, so the check adds a failure: "writes who governs in place outside GovernmentRecord's methods". The defect behind it is real too. The record's Version does not move, so a ChamberVerdicts cache keeps verdicts keyed to the non-caretaker government, and CaretakerSince stays MinValue. Fix: `sitting.Discharge(CurrentDate);` (sets Caretaker and CaretakerSince, bumps Version).

### H2 (High) - two existing diagnostics still pin Italy's rules as Unsourced
`ConfidenceDiagnostic.cs:424` and `ConstructiveVoteDiagnostic.cs:63` both assert `RulesOf(CountryId.Italy) == Rules.Unsourced`. RulesOf now returns `Parlamento` for Italy, so both go red on the next bar. Neither file is in the diff. Update the pins, and their messages ("Italy's and France's not yet modelled", "the other two unsourced").

### M1 (Medium) - the election-night verdict misreports Italy's outcome
`SeatPlayerElection` returns `government = GovernmentFormation.Form(country, reading)`, which is the Camera-only formation. For Italy it then runs `InstallByMandates` and returns true. GameController (lines ~6530-6560) has no Italy branch: it falls through to the generic path and builds the verdict from `government`. Failure scenarios:
- (a) Mandate 1 is refused by the Senato and mandate 2 or 3 installs a different cabinet. The night says "In office: the chamber formed a <Camera cabinet> government with X in the cabinet", but that is not the government installed.
- (b) All three mandates fail and art. 88 dissolves the chambers. The night still says the player is in office, or "No government could be formed ... You stay in office until one can". The dissolution and the new polling day are never shown.

The verdict should come from the installed record and the mandates (the `Line` strings already exist).

### M2 (Medium) - the Estero swing compares the game's roster-sum shares with 2022 valid-vote shares: a seat flips on the record's own vote
`EsteroVotes` sets the change as `now - was`, where `was = pub[k] / CameraPublished2022Valid` (share of the whole valid vote). `now` is the game share, which sums to one over the roster (the diagnostic's own (f)/(i) convention). The roster held 94.3 % of the 2022 Camera vote. So on the record's own vote every keyed list gets a phantom swing: FdI+Lega+FI joint +2.59 pt, PD +1.15 pt, M5S +0.93 pt.

I reproduced `EsteroVotes` and `Estero` in a perl script over the catalog arrays. With the 2022 shares normalised to one, America Meridionale's second Camera seat goes to LEGA-FI-FDI instead of PD: Estero 7/8 against the record. With zero change it gives the record's 8/8. Diagnostic (e) misses this for two reasons: it calls `FlooredHare` on `EsteroRecord` directly, never `EsteroVotes`/`Estero`, and only for the Camera. The Senato's four Estero seats are never compared. Fix: read `was` on the roster's own 2022 sum, as the colleges' swing reads 2018 on the mapped lists' own sum. Then add a live check: `Camera(normalised 2022 shares).EsteroSeats` equals the record.

### M3 (Medium) - thresholds and the national stage read roster-normalised, clamp-inflated college sums, not the national share
The national cifra is the sum of the swung colleges. `valid` is the sum of the keys' votes only; `otherVotes` is null in a played count, and `rosterShareOfVote` is ignored for Italy. Italy also has no RecordFactors entry, so the 25 Sep 2022 polling day inside the start's window is counted on raw model shares (Gate18's window line will read "counted, but no factors are carried").

My perl reproduction of `SwungVotes` on the 2022 Camera shares normalised to one gives:
- +Europa at 2.973 % against the 3 % line in the Camera, and 3.074 % in the Senato when the Senato's own 2022 shares are used. On the record it was 2.83 % of the valid vote, so the margin shrinks from 0.17 to 0.03 pt. A small favourable move seats +Europa inside the centre-left coalition, which the record did not.
- Clamping adds about 1.1-1.4 % of phantom votes. Lega's counted national share becomes 10.23 % against its 9.32 % input, roughly +2-3 proportional seats for any party whose 2018 base was uneven and that fell.

Either pass the roster's 2022 share as the threshold base (GATE-18 piece two's pattern), or rescale each key's national cifra to its national share before the national stage. In both cases, add a live-path check: `Camera`/`Senato` on the normalised 2022 shares equals the published proportional seats. The claimed 245/122 "list for list" backtest only covers `Count` fed the record's cifre plus OTHER, never the path a game runs.

### M4 (Medium) - the ordinary calendar ignores an art. 88 dissolution; repeated dissolutions are possible
`TryNextPollingDay(Italy)` is anchored statically on 13 Oct 2022 and steps only through ordinary terms. After a dissolution election (for example 4 Dec 2022, first sitting 24 Dec 2022), the next ordinary day is still 10 Oct 2027. A dissolution in 2026 gives a roughly one-year legislature, against art. 60 (five years from the new chambers' first sitting). The doc's "every later one from the first sitting its own election gives" is true only for ordinary elections.

Separately, nothing breaks a cycle. If the chamber arithmetic is hung (no cabinet plus support holding a majority of members in both chambers - plausible, since Article94 counts every non-government member against), the mandates fail on each count day and a new election follows 70 days later, indefinitely. Art. 88 c.2 (the semestre bianco) is not modelled either. At minimum, rebase the calendar on the game's last Italian first sitting. State the loop risk, or guard it, for Elias.

### L1 (Low) - Draghi's PM party is M5S (declared and pinned by PlayerRoleDiagnostic): consequences
`AtStart` gives PmParty = `Largest(cabinet)` on the XVIII Camera by its 2018 groups, which is M5S. Consequences:
- A player who picks M5S at the 21 Jul 2022 start is PRIME MINISTER (RoleOf). It takes the head's portfolio weighting in AllocatePortfolios and the PM's economic-vote weight (EconomicVote.cs:84). M5S had withheld confidence on 20 Jul 2022, the act that brought Draghi down. By July 2022, Lega was the larger cabinet group after Di Maio's split.
- The start opens on CAM-729's own caretaker day, but the installed record is not Caretaker.

Ask Elias whether a non-party head should take no PM party, or whether the record should be marked caretaker from the start.

### L2 (Low) - compensation details in `Distribute`
- The statute fixes the order of excess units once ("proseguendo ... in ordine decrescente"). The code re-picks after every single seat, so a tie can interleave units.
- The last resort takes the excess unit's smallest-decimal seat among all its seats, where the statute says seats "ottenuti con le minori parti decimali" (decimal-won).
- At lett. i the cross-circoscrizione move breaks the invariant that a coalition's lists sum to `entityByCirc[e][i]` in each circoscrizione. The list totals stay right, so only ByCircoscrizione is affected.
- Lett. i's quotient uses lists with seats > 0, where the statute says lists admitted by lett. g. They are the same in practice.

None of this is exercised by the record backtest. A planted case would pin it.

### L3 (Low) - smaller points

- (b) Mandates 2/3 call `ViewOf(country.Id, keys, counts, ..., reading.Vintage)` while mandate 1 calls `ViewOf(country, reading)`. Two entry points could read declarations differently.
- (c) All mandates are tried, and dissolution decreed, on the polling day itself, before the new chambers' first sitting (declared and asked).
- (d) Seats won abroad by unkeyed lists (USEI etc.) leave the Camera short of 400 in `record.Seats` (declared).
- (e) The Senato is seated, but bills still pass on the Camera alone (bicameral passage is not in scope; worth stating).
