## The questions asked

**(a) Reproducible, and the three countries' rows:** confirmed above - two byte-identical runs equal to the committed file, and DE/IT/PL match the annex.

**(b) kappa in both forms.** The turn form (`ApplyNationalAccounts`, seeded branch) and the daily form (`ApplyNationalAccountsDaily`: `contraction = rho + kappa (a - a_seed)`, `attractorTerm = kappa (G + NX + DI) + (1 - rho - kappa gamma_seed) P`) both read `GapImpact(country)`, so the D daily slices still compose to the turn map, and `PreviewTurn` goes through the turn form. The disposable-income delta enters as C in the turn form and as a level in the daily form, at kappa both times. The steady state of a sustained impulse is kappa / (1 - rho) = 0.8 exactly; the daily clamp on A to [0, 0.99] would need a - a_seed below -2.2 and cannot bind. The old form is untouched: where `GapSeeded` is false (a save without seed references, the AI countries) both forms still read `OutputGapReversionSpeed` ((1 - s) a and (1 - s)(G + NX + DI) + s P), and `GapImpact` is called only behind `GapSeeded`. No other code path reads `OutputGapReversionSpeed` or rebuilds the gap form.

**(c) The treatable factor.** `TreatableFactor` takes L(x) of the scaled schedule (c x M x R x m_seed) over ages 0-74 as weights, S = sum L r / sum L, and returns f = 1 + Delta / S. Applying f to the 0-74 rates on the SAME weights gives sum L (f r) / sum L = f S = S + Delta: the standardised rate moves by exactly Delta, the standard being the pre-factor stationary population (a fixed-standard reading, as the OECD's is; it drifts with the year and with M, which the summary declares). The two floors cannot bind in practice (the smallest scaled rate is about 1e-4, and 0.1 x 1e-4 is ten times `MinRate`). The guard [0.1, 10] is symmetric in log terms. At k <= -1 the factor is at most 0 and is clamped to 0.1, and `Math.Max(MinRate, ...)` keeps S positive: no NaN and no division by zero. A NaN reaches the factor only from a NaN treatable mortality, which `TreatableExcessRate`'s `<= 0f` test lets through - as the old additive form did; not new. Within the health family's clamps (treatable mortality 10..400 per 100 000, the anchor floored at 10) Delta stays between about -0.0014 (a high anchor pushed to the floor) and 0.0039, against S near 0.002-0.004 at the seed, so the guard should not bind on any reachable state; the remark says so, and that is plausible but not asserted. The diagnostic's own `E0` re-implements the factor with the same weights and guard, so (a2)'s daily equality cross-checks the implementation.

**(e) Provenance and the text checks.** Every new constant is tagged: `LongRunDemandMultiplier` SOURCED with the citation, `MaxTreatableFactor` CONVENTION, `NoPolicyGapBound` [AUTHORED] and declared before any run, `TreatableAgeLimit` SOURCED.
