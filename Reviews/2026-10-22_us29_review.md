## Findings

### M1. The holdover is credited to 12 U.S.C. 242 on the player's page, but the statute's holdover covers Board MEMBERS, not the chair's designation
`UsFedConsent.cs:29-30` (the class note), `UsFedConsent.cs:106` (`HoldoverWords`), `UsFedConsent.cs:111` (`RefusedWords`), `GameController.PoliticsV35.cs:833`, `SimulationManager.cs:3828-3829`.

The saved page (`us_12usc242_govinfo2024.htm`) says: "Upon the expiration of their terms of office, **members of the Board** shall continue to serve until their successors are appointed and have qualified." That sentence is about the fourteen-year member terms. The chair's four-year designation has no holdover in 242. The same section sends the chair's absence to a Vice Chairman ("1 of whom shall serve in the absence of the Chairman, as provided in section 244"). On the record, the Board names a chair pro tempore when the chair's term lapses before reconfirmation (from memory, unverified: Powell in February to May 2022). AF-7 already read the members' holdover for every head and flagged it DECLARED for the other banks (`CentralBankTerms.cs:13-15`). US-29 goes further: it prints "THE CHAIR SERVES ON UNTIL A SUCCESSOR IS CONFIRMED (12 U.S.C. 242)" and "serves on - the Senate has confirmed no successor (12 U.S.C. 242)" on the bank tab, the governor block, the slip and the verb's refusal. So the player reads a citation that does not say what the line claims.

*Failure scenario:* an AI president's nominee is refused, or a player-president faces a hostile Senate. The Fed page then tells the player, citing the statute, that the chair holds over. The statute says no such thing about the chair.

*Fix:* in code, mark the reading DECLARED ("the members' holdover in 12 U.S.C. 242 read for the chair's designation, DECLARED, asked"). On the page, drop the parenthesised citation from the serves-on clause, or reword it so the statute is not credited, e.g. "THE SITTING CHAIR SERVES ON (THE GAME'S READING)". If Elias wants it sourced, fetch 12 U.S.C. 244 and the 2022 pro tempore record, and add an ask: holdover by the sitting chair, or the Vice Chair acting.

### M2. A player-president under a hostile Senate can never nominate, and the page says "THE SENATE REFUSES" about a nominee nobody sent
`SimulationManager.cs:3830-3835` (`CentralBankHeadAwaitsPlayer`), `UsFedConsent.cs:102-107` (`HoldoverWords`), `GameController.cs:4146-4157`, `GameController.DocketV35.cs:45-49`.

So `_fedChairCandidates` is never drawn, NOMINATE stays disabled (`GUI.enabled = pending`), the docket shows no item, and the AI does not appoint (`PlayerAppointsCentralBankHead` is true). The lever column then shows `HoldoverWords`: "THE TERM ENDED … · THE SENATE REFUSES, 47-53 · THE CHAIR SERVES ON …". Directly under it the state line reads "NO NOMINEE · ESTIMATE DRAWS ON THE DOCKET". The page says a refusal happened. Nobody was nominated, and the player is never told that he cannot nominate, or why. The verb's refusal text (`RefusedWords`, "THE PRESIDENT'S PARTY HOLDS NO MAJORITY THERE") is reachable from no UI path. Only the diagnostic calls the verb under a hostile Senate. The plan's done-when, "a hostile Senate refuses a nominee by the count", is met for the AI president only. The player's nominee is pre-empted, never refused.

*Fix (either):*
- (a) Keep the pre-emption and give the player-president's holdover its own words, e.g. "YOUR PARTY HOLDS 47 OF THE SENATE'S 100 SEATS · NO NOMINEE OF YOURS CAN BE CONFIRMED · THE SITTING CHAIR SERVES ON". Give the docket a non-holding item that says the same.
- (b) Let the player nominate without holding time (candidates drawn, NOMINATE enabled), and show the verb's refusal with its count. This matches the plan's wording and makes the refusal a played event.

Either way, the choice belongs in the ask to Elias.
