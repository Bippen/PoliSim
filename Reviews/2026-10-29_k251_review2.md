## New findings

**M-N1. The dead-beat release has no damping, and EDP breaches are now more frequent.** AiFinanceMinistry.cs:97 and :104 release the WHOLE gap to the target in one year, read on last year's balance, while the lever's effect on the next balance is incomplete (the restore clamps, the seed band, the multiplier and the revenue response). Measured, France years 39-45 (branch): debt 65.0 with the fall under pace -> cut (the consolidation, plus the net-expenditure excess) -> y40 balance +2.11, debt 60.9; release above 60 of min(3.6, 2.61) -> y41 still +2.01 (the release barely showed), debt 57.1, now below 60: release 3.01 more -> y42 +0.01 -> release 1.01 -> y43 -2.58 -> y44 -3.55, an EDP breach, then close-back. Release, release, release, then cut: the oscillation the brief asked about, with an amplitude that crosses 3 %. Country-years with a deficit beyond 3 % over the century, the five together: base 28 (France 6, Germany 6, Italy 2, Poland 14, Sweden 0), branch 43 (8, 9, 3, 20, 3). Year-on-year balance volatility is otherwise unchanged (rms 0.84 / 1.84 / 1.01 / 2.79 / 1.13 vs 0.79 / 1.69 / 0.78 / 2.81 / 1.12) - Poland's large swings are pre-existing. Part of the rise is honest (a -1 % resting point leaves less buffer to 3 % than the old [-1, 0] band; real countries at -1 do breach in downturns), part is the stacked release. Failure scenario: an AI France or Sweden enters an excessive deficit procedure a decade after the ministry released a surplus, with no shock. Not blocking; file it (a K269 (e), or into (a)): release a fraction of the gap (e.g. half), or net last year's release that has not yet shown before releasing again.

**L-N1. The debt-ratio memory goes stale across a player spell, and the release now reads it.** `Observe` runs only under `aiGovernment` (SimulationManager.cs:6851), so after the player has governed N years `fall` (AiFinanceMinistry.cs:156) is an N-year change read as one year. Before the rework a stale large fall could only zero a cut; now it also triggers the above-60 release. Failure scenario: the player governs France for five years taking the debt 110 -> 95, then loses office in a year when the ratio is barely moving (true fall 0.3, under the 0.5 pace - the rule should cut 0.2); the AI's first turn reads a fall of 15 and releases down to a 0.5 % deficit instead. Fix: Observe every turn whoever governs (the memory is a fact of the books), or zero `DebtRatioLastReport` when the AI takes office.

**L-N2. The H2 guard is not exercised by the diagnostic.** AiFinanceMinistryDiagnostic.cs:111 (the 2 % deficit close-back raises no rate) and :72 (no raise below 60 inside 3 %) can only fire when `CutLinesUniformly` is clamp-limited, but a 1 % of GDP cut is about 2 % of the lines against clamps of 15 % (mandatory) and 30 % (discretionary): `RaiseHouseholdRates` is never reached in that fixture, so deleting `&& consolidating` at AiFinanceMinistry.cs:189 still passes. Likewise :101 checks the income tax was lowered and not below its seed, but not that it reached its seed before any line was restored - a proportional split between rates and lines would pass. Would each case fail while false: (3) the 2 % release - yes (the old rule released 1); the rate-first part only partly; the at-limit case - not discriminating (the old rule also wrote nothing at -1), harmless; the close-back no-rate part - no; (8) the fast fall releasing 0.5 - yes (the old rule wrote nothing); the slow fall - not discriminating, harmless.
