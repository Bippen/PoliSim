**N3 - `-shotabroad` lays the AI ministry's budget as an AI government's bill in a country the player governs.** `UiScreenshotDriver.cs:3498-3503`.
- **What happens.**
  - The player's own budget window opens only where the player governs. Where the AI governs the player's country, the manager tables the bill itself (`SimulationManager.cs:3465-3472`), so `GetPendingBudgetProcess` is never true there.
  - Every call the walk makes to `TableGovernmentBudget` therefore happens with the player's party governing: the arrival window from day one, and the new arrival window after the 2026 round's `Install` → `ResetArrivalBudgetWindow`.
- **Why that state is not play's.**
  - `TableGovernmentBudget` is documented as the path "where the AI governs THE PLAYER'S country" (`:1403-1411`).
  - Its bill comes from `AiFinanceMinistry.AsBudgetBill`, with `GovernmentBill = true`.
  - The code's own convention is `GovernmentBill = !PlayerGoverns(country)`: see `SimulationManager.cs:2292` and `SimulationManager.DebtLimit.cs:101`. The UI reads it as "not yours" (`GameController.cs:9695`, `GameController.BudgetV35.cs:567`, `GameController.Desk.cs:1411`).
  - The bill also bypasses `IntroduceBudgetBill`'s role gate and range check, and FORK-2's `Answered`.
- **What follows from it.**
  - The pending bill resolves through `ResolveGovernmentBudget`, the AI government's path.
  - While it is pending, `TableShadowBudget`'s only gate (`:1444`, `pending.GovernmentBill`) lets the **governing** party table an "alternative" to its own bill.
  - The Budget page and Desk read it as the opposition's view.
- **Failure scenario.** The 2026 Riksdag's round installs in October. The next walk day tables a 21-day `GovernmentBill`. On 4 Nov `abroad01_docket` then shows the player's government facing "the government's budget" as if it were in opposition, with the alternative's verb live. That is the same class of state N1 was raised for: a Docket no game reaches.
- **Precedent.** Neither precedent answers a budget window this way. GATE-18 leaves it open. Play's neutral answer is "introduce your current draft as a bill" (`GameController.cs:9713`), that is `IntroduceBudgetBill` with `GovernmentBill = false`.
- **Fix (either of):**
  - introduce the player's current draft through `IntroduceBudgetBill`, logged; or
  - leave the window open as GATE-18 does (the film already holds `_daySpeedTimer`) and say so in the log.
