**Findings**

   - Scenario: France's 27 fund at −10 % (about 2.6 a day on GDP 3,200) is empty by about day 10. On each of the remaining ~111 days, the day's planned return lands in the empty fund and the next day's capped draw books it.
   - Result: about 1.6 bn reaches the budget from assets no longer held (6.5 % of 27 per period at the mean, more on a good draw). The old floor swallowed this.
   - The drawdown bill reaches the same artifact today: an emptied fund refills from the flat return (Sweden's 195 gives about 12.7 per period at the mean).

## What was done

- **1 (MEDIUM): filed, not built.** The flat return is a second, smaller leg of GA-2-1's class (the fund pays out what it no longer holds), and its fix is a modelling choice that moves the BASELINE (the return's daily accrual form reaches every fund whose assets fall within a period, a bad market year included) - so it is a ledger row with an ARCHITECT question (GA-2-10, `docs/audit/AUDIT_FOR_ARCHITECT.md`), not a Phase 4 fix Elias marked.
- **2 (LOW): filed with the ceiling's question.** The fund's ceiling discarding a contribution the budget booked is documented as built (`MaxSwfToGdpPercent`'s summary: "only the STOCK stops compounding further"); it joins GA-12-1's question on the debt's ceiling, as the reviewer proposes.
- **3 (LOW): all three fixed in the harness.** FUND is counted exercised only when two consecutive days of one period were compared; the invariant now derives what the BUDGET booked for the fund (the day's total spending less the spending beside the fund's and the plan's fixed slice of government spending - every one of them written only by the daily accrual, the plan only at a boundary) and requires it to equal the period's accrual ("one draw, two figures"), and requires the fund to have been debited what the budget took (its assets at most the evening's plus the day's booking and planned return - the floor, the ceiling and the structural draw only lower them); the tolerance carries the standing contribution's day and the planned return's.
- **4 (LOW): declined, under R-N5.** A new check is written only after a defect has cost something twice; no earlier record shows a stock booked past empty costing anything, so GA-2-1 is the class's first instance. If GA-2-10 is ruled a defect it is the second, and the check the reviewer specifies (a standing −10 % rate on a small fund across a boundary in `SwfDrawdownBooksDiagnostic`) is then earned - noted on GA-2-10's row.
- **5 (LOW): (a) and (b) fixed** - the preview's estimate says it is the period's draw capped once at today's assets, agreeing with the days unless the fund drains within the period; `SovereignWealthFund.ContributionRatePercent`'s summary says "up to what the fund holds". **(c)** GA-2-1's row is closed with the rule built: the contribution is capped at the assets BEFORE the day's return (the drawdown bill's rule).
- The changes after the report are the harness's (tooling) and two comments; the money-path code the reviewer read is unchanged but for comments (the two dates and 5(a)), so no second pass was run.
